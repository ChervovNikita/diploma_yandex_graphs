# Final rebuttal campaign: list of experiments

The full list of what has to be run, and how to check it is done. The plan is
generated by `ablation/campaign/plan_fixed_protocol.py`, launched by one command
(`RUN.md`, step 4) and trained under `PROTOCOL.md`. Backbones: ResNet, GCN, SAGE,
GAT, GAT-sep, GT, GT-sep. Datasets: Roman Empire, Amazon Ratings, Minesweeper,
Tolokers, Questions.

## In the plan

| # | Experiment | What is trained | Fits | Queue | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parity with the archive | Roman, GCN, depth 2, splits 0-4, BASE and GNNM; these runs stopped by patience in the archive and must reproduce it | 10 | 1 | `parity.csv` |
| 2 | Main table (Table 1) | only the 212 archived runs that hit the 5000-step limit (200) or are missing (12), listed in `rerun_list.csv`; everything else is reused from the archive | 362 | 1 | `main_table.csv`, `rerun_changes.csv`, `runs/` |
| 3 | Hyperparameter search, GAT | 24 configurations x 5 depths x splits 0-2 x 5 datasets for BASE, ENS, GNNM; selection by validation, then confirmation of the selected configuration | 9,390 | 2 | `tasks/report/` of the campaign root |
| 4 | Hyperparameter search, other backbones | the same for the 6 other backbones (3 configurations), **only if** step 3 passes its validation rule (GNNM beats tuned ENS on at least 3 of 5 datasets) | 9,270 (conditional) | 5 | `tasks/report/` |
| 5 | Collab link prediction | GCN and GraphSAGE, OGB recipe, 5 cohorts, BASE, ENS, GNNM, GNNM + contrast; Hits@20/50/100 | 60 | 3 | `tasks/report/collab` |
| 6 | Roman ablation, splits 0-4 | 12 variants x 7 backbones x 5 depths | 2,100 | 4 | `roman_ablation.csv`, `roman_ablation_summary.csv` |
| 7 | Roman ablation, splits 5-9 | the same, remaining splits | 2,100 | 6 | the same |
| 8 | Contrastive term on node tasks | GNNM + contrast at the paper's selected depth, 5 datasets x 7 backbones x 10 splits | 350 | 9 | `node_contrast.csv` |

Total 23,642 fits: 9,270 of them (row 4) run only if the gate opens, and the 420 GAT confirmation fits wait for the GAT selection. The 12 Roman variants are input_only,
output_only_identity, output_only_signs, all_layer_be, gnnm_m1, gnnm_m2, gnnm_m8,
xavier_all, ones_all, xavier_rms, heads_single_pass, independent_projectors.
BASE, ENS and GNNM for the ablation come from the main table (row 2).

  its limit.
