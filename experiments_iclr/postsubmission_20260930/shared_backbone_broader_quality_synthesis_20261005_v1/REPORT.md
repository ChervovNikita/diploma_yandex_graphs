# Graph evidence extensions for shared backbone ensembles

5 October 2026. Research memo for the current accuracy objective.

The saved research does not establish a theoretical or practical breakthrough in predictive quality. It does support two concrete, attributed compositions beyond the active private-learning rule. Both reuse previously considered evidence pathways. The user’s permission to build on published methods makes their utility worth distinguishing experimentally; the existence of their ingredients alone is insufficient grounds for rejection. Neither composition has an established sharing advantage or cleared methodological novelty.

The completed private endpoint-frame candidate was not promoted, and the saved Collab private-completion decision preserves its failure against independent four. Unchanged sharing has encouraging development evidence with inconclusive primary intervals. These are conclusions from saved reports, with no score payload reopened. Generic learned routing, Jaccard repulsion, graph-filter diversity and marginal update credit would repeat assessed mechanisms. The useful remaining question is whether preserving particular evidence improves a competent predictor, and then whether allocating its learning across shared members adds value. [S1, S2, S3, S5, D1]

Weight sharing does not imply identical activations. The modern adapter has private stems and member-dependent GAT attention. Current NCN F4 also applies a private `xlin` to node states before common-neighbor summation. An obstruction for purely terminal heads therefore cannot be asserted for the whole current architecture. Information already absent from all decoder-visible states remains unavailable to these transforms. [S3, C1]

## Composition one Preserve neighborhood distributions before propagation

**Published foundation:** PNA supplies multiple neighborhood statistics and degree scaling; Fourier Sliced-Wasserstein Embedding supplies projected multiset distributions and quantile representations. BatchEnsemble and TabM supply shared matrices, private factors and supervised member learning. The saved private-quantile proposal already combines these ingredients. [P1, P2, P6, P7, S4]

**Concrete refinement:** retain a branch from the source-native normalized input features, before graph propagation can smooth them. For member m and node i, compute the fixed lower quartile, median and upper quartile of `{u_mᵀ x_j : j is a nonself neighbor of i}`, center them by that neighborhood’s projected mean, and append the three values and `log(1+degree)` to the existing nonlinear private prediction route. Learn unit directions `u_m` through ordinary member supervision. Preserve the native root path, full neighborhoods and fixed deployed pool. Empty and degree-one neighborhoods use the saved zero centered-signature convention. Normalization, sorting ties and gradients remain source-qualification requirements.

This changes the placement of the saved final-state signature to a retained input branch. It is an attributed architecture modification, with an explicit information hypothesis: mixed neighbor populations can have the same average while differing in distributional shape, and later common smoothing may remove that distinction. The raw branch preserves a chance to use it. Labels must actually depend on that shape, and a learned direction must recover it; neither premise has been established on our tasks.

**Falsifiable benefit:** on one complete node-classification task with mixed local populations, separately supervised signatures improve the deployed pool beyond unchanged sharing, a common-signature variant, and competent same-evidence singles and untied ensembles. If a PNA or full-signature single matches, retain distribution-aware architecture utility and simplify. If the untied operation wins, a quality benefit from sharing is unsupported. Attention and nonlinear message functions may already capture the useful statistic. Three quartiles remain noninjective and supply none of FSW’s general injectivity guarantees.

## Composition two Preserve query relative structural evidence

**Published foundation:** LPFormer combines GCN features with target-pair attention, PPR relative encodings and distinct common-neighbor, other one-hop and distant context. BUDDY/ELPH supplies query structural sketches; OCN supplies richer common-neighbor structure. LPFormer is the precise starting architecture for this composition; the other two locate its nearest evidence alternatives. OCN’s saved candidate-batch normalization qualification prevents treating it as an interchangeable native ranker. [P3–P5, S2]

**Concrete modification:** retain LPFormer’s pair-relative context and context-selection rule, share its encoder and common value maps, and give four supervised members private low-rank adjustments to the query-attention scorer and private nonlinear readouts. Each member aggregates its own query-conditioned weights before scoring. All four predictions remain in a fixed pool; there is no expert selection, disagreement reward or transfer episode. Sharing encodings does not eliminate the four attention and readout computations.

The graph-specific hypothesis concerns queries whose useful evidence lies in different structural roles: common-neighbor aggregation can merge role information, while endpoint embeddings alone omit pair-relative context. A retained structural descriptor can distinguish such queries even when the endpoint interface coincides. Current NCN already receives common-neighbor context and has private transforms before its sum, so the proposed delta must be the particular richer pair-relative evidence and its supervised weighting. A new name for a generic structural correction would be insufficient.

**Falsifiable benefit:** private query weighting improves complete link-ranking quality beyond unchanged LPFormer and its shared port, after giving the same context to capable singles and independently acquired members. The plausible sharing benefit is a finite-sample bias: common feature maps pool scarce supervision while private query weighting accommodates evidence variation. That conjecture can fail through shared feature bias, redundant attention or weaker members. A capable single with the same four attention branches and a joint nonlinear decoder can represent the pooled predictor. A gain against NCN alone would establish neither the sharing effect nor an ensemble requirement. This is the concrete LPFormer-based version of S2’s previously unretained structural-correction avenue; changed utility criteria justify assessing it, while the earlier absence of evidence remains intact.

## One representative comparison for each composition

Assess one selected composition on one complete task, rather than run both as a sweep. The existing Amazon-ratings official blocks illustrate the node setting; Pubmed-HeaRT illustrates the link setting. These examples create no new study, release, threshold or permission and leave current families unchanged. A later design must establish that its role is prospective and select any fresh confirmation task before outcomes.

Use three predetermined native split or seed blocks and these seven cells:

| Ordinary independently acquired four of that foundation | Does it beat conventional ensembling? |

For the last cell, retain the same complete evidence bank and disclose any changed capacity and work. It is an attribution control, not automatically an exact capacity match. Freeze preprocessing, permitted labels, support graphs, pooling, selection opportunity, native optimization competence and all acquisition costs before outcomes. An independent-four cell includes all four full fits. Three blocks on one graph have substantial uncertainty; graph queries are not independent replicates. A second task is needed for a general quality claim.

## Uncertainty and stopping

Report every paired complete-task gap, member competence and full costs. Use the task’s deployed accuracy or ranking metric as the decision variable. A positive mean on three blocks, or better attention separation, is insufficient evidence of a breakthrough.

Before any future comparison, choose a worthwhile effect size and the planned uncertainty summary. A nonpositive complete-task gain against an essential strong control stops promotion of the corresponding quality explanation. If the capable single, common-selector or untied arm matches, narrow the claim respectively to architecture utility, shared evidence utility or untied ensemble utility. Positive but uncertain gaps remain unconfirmed; stop tuning on the same validation population and seek at most a separately frozen fresh confirmation. Failure cannot be rescued with selected subgroups, extra signatures, new attention masks or post hoc seeds. Engineering failure leaves utility unresolved.

No new primary retrieval is needed for this bounded choice: the saved scopes already establish the relevant operations. Exact native ports, optimizer behavior and runtime remain unresolved prerequisites for any implementation. The memo supplies hypotheses and comparisons, with no launch, new gate, acceptance prescription or novelty claim.
