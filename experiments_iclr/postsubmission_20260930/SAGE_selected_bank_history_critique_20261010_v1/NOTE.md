## 1 Bank selection and realized selection windows differ

Every bank serves mean probabilities, but selection is not identical: shared4 maximizes complete pooled VALID accuracy, whereas I4 independently maximizes each member's own VALID accuracy and assembles a mixed-step bank. Ties always retain the first maximum regardless of NLL. Shared4 selects steps 78/318/165 and completes 378/618/465 updates; factorized I4 selects steps 33–53 and completes 333–353 per body. Common max1000/patience300 limits therefore produce different realized search windows and selection objectives. [Selected and completed steps](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/common_wrapper_SAGE_root_20261010_v1/ACTUAL_COMPLETE_FAMILY.json>)

This does not invalidate the observed comparison of declared complete predictors or establish that I4 is undertrained. It limits attribution of the small pooled accuracy advantage to sharing: the shared endpoint is chosen directly for the reported bank accuracy. Accuracy-only selection can also accept severe NLL deterioration; the complete files do not establish its cause.

**Study implication.** If the ordinary shared signal remains worth pursuing after both transfer families close, a targeted selector control would retain own-selected and pooled-selected I4 banks from the same prospectively acquired trajectories under one declared common horizon. It answers whether the serving-aware selector explains the gap. It cannot be reconstructed from the current overwritten selected checkpoints or added retrospectively as another SAGE result. Preserve the original accuracy/NLL outcomes.

## 2 Dense initialization diversity is bundled with untying

Shared4 and the factorized I4 member0 share the native dense start; the other independent bodies use distinct native initialization seeds. The four input-factor Rademacher rows are matched, and other factors begin at one. Thus the comparison changes dense-start diversity, parameter tying, simultaneous function capacity and joint updates together. It does not isolate a benefit of common weights or a cost of conflicting shared gradients. [Fixed pairing contract](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/common_wrapper_SAGE_full_family_source_20261010_v1/README.md>)

**Study implication.** A copied-untied factorized four-body reference, paired to the shared dense core, all factor rows and dropout streams, with the same pooled selection contract, would isolate tying from dense-start diversity. Its extra body capacity remains explicit; it still cannot by itself separate representation capacity from joint optimization. This is a specific missing causal comparator, not a reason to reopen every existing negative or add a gradient-remedy grid.
