# Interpretation fixed before the fresh fits

This is an exploratory mechanism pilot, not confirmation of a conference contribution. The two graphs, native configurations, three paired seed/split contexts and five arms are fixed in the sealed protocol. All arms start from the same newly trained warm state within each context. Existing original-paper scores will not be recomputed or replaced.

After successful completion of all 72 phases, the canonical comparison must bind every selected checkpoint and member-logit array before test labels are released. The report will retain every arm, accepted step length, fallback, selected epoch, cost and paired difference. Validation and test results must be distinguished. Three paired contexts permit a descriptive consistency check, not a strong significance or broad robustness claim; seed and split both change between contexts.

The main comparison is the complete graph-guided initialization operation against warm copying. Common-only descent tests whether an additional safeguarded update explains a gain. Random-tangent and topology-permuted arms test whether generic diversity or changed topology can explain it. Each arm independently selects its safeguarded step length, so their contrasts do not isolate topology at one common step length. Centering cancels the additional first-order pooled-logit displacement; this mathematical property does not imply useful predictive diversity.

A direction warrants a new, prospectively frozen confirmation study only if the graph arm shows consistent useful decision or predictive differences against the controls without relying on one favorable context, and the costs remain practical. A result can be mixed or negative; all such evidence remains recorded. A promising result still needs a competent native single model, an independent ensemble and a relevant efficient-ensemble baseline before an outperforming-method claim. No pilot outcome alone establishes methodological originality or conference acceptance.
