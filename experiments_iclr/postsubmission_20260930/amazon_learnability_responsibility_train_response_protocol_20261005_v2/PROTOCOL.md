# Prospective W/S/R response protocol V2

V1 is preserved. Root adopted this design before any acquisition or payload read. A identity, native full graph, seed17, G0, six controls, H16 and fixed scoring rules are unchanged.

B is ordered once by `(sha256(UTF8("amazon-response-G0|split=0|seed=17|WSR|<node_id>")), node_id)`. W is its first floor(B/2), S its next floor(B/4), R the remainder. Source counts: W4898/S2449/R2450, with unchanged A2449/B9797. Stored row orders are increasing IDs. Missing S support fails without rebalancing or reseeding.

Common acquisition uses only W labels: local200/global200, exact native construction/wrap and Adam defaults, no selector/rollback. S/R enter the scientific process after the last common state freezes; their public graph/features remain present throughout. Subsequent episodes repeatedly train S and R; R is not held evaluation.

A compact TRAIN label member jointly stores W/S/R/A. The custodian necessarily decodes it and projects separate W-only, B-only and A evaluator artifacts without metrics or label-dependent roles. This boundary excludes A from new fitting/selection; it does not claim unread bytes or historically untouched A. No new VAL/TEST label or mask members are decoded.

The initial response diagnostic uses a separately disabled exact unchanged-warm private-response call, discarding all virtual states. This is an explicit departure from ideal first-episode reuse: +16 member forwards, +8 private derivatives, +4 probe/+4 main virtual steps, +80 pair solver iterations. Planned total is5100 member forwards including seven served evaluations. Response scale, r/epsilon, normalized response, Q movement/marginals and member probe CE change are retained without an empirical threshold or tuning. Zero responses are interpretable finite outcomes. No broad rejection follows from a failed G0 gate.

Common plus all six exact H16 banks, and all seven complete native served predictions, must exist immutably before the separate evaluator opens A. All original accuracy/NLL/Brier margins and FP32-serving/FP64-scoring conventions remain fixed. No A-driven rescue, selection or recipe change.

Source preparation only: no execution or fit authorization. Native higher-order/sparse equivalence, runtime/resources and future released bindings remain pending. Capable ordinary whole-bank, nonlinear single, independent4 and same-operation untied4 controls remain necessary later.
