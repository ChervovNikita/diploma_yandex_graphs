## Statistical scope

`inference.py` applies one-sided paired t tests at zero-gain null to raw E4−BASE and E4−equipped I4 integer-accuracy differences over the ten fixed mask/optimizer blocks. Holm adjusts exactly those two tests at alpha .05. It reports paired df9 95% intervals and two simultaneous Bonferroni 97.5% two-sided intervals using t(.9875,9), giving at least 95% family coverage under the stated t assumptions. The observed .2 pp screens are separate from claims about a population margin.

E4−N4 attribution is reported in full and its confirmatory gate opens only after both primary superiority tests pass; its observed .1 pp screen is distinct. There is no all-positive-block veto. Exactly 1,024 integer sign assignments supply sign-flip sensitivity under a stated symmetry/exchangeability assumption, without replacing the primary t tests. Calibrated accuracy is not a second primary test. The calibrated-NLL mean .02/every-block .05 screen is a separate practical diagnostic, not statistical noninferiority.

Missing/failed native or scalar endpoints remain explicit. Paired and arm summaries require all ten entries: no survivor mean, replacement seed, topup, rescue or heldout-selected choice. Degenerate/nonfinite t inference is reported inconclusive without a replacement test. Masks/readouts share one graph; nodes and I4 bodies are not independent inference units. Member/error decomposition is descriptive, without an isolated causal claim.
