## Required compact report

For every selected bank report original epoch(s), global/local member flags,
stored and collected accuracy, actual served/member NLL and Brier, each member,
mean and separately defined worst member. NLL remains the original float32
log-softmax and logsumexp true-class probability mean, without clamp/calibration;
Brier is the class sum, without division by10. Different metrics may have
different worst members.

Report all three fixed paired seed differences, equal-seed mean, sample SD and
the existing descriptive df2 interval. All three slots are required. Development
selected these same5274 nodes; the interval describes optimizer-seed dispersion
on one graph, with selection optimism. Nodes, members and repeated seed-node
instances are not independent replicates. No node-iid bootstrap, graph-population
confidence claim or TEST confirmation is supplied.

Use the unchanged full-population/error-mask contracts for any-correct coverage,
all-member errors, pooled-only rescues, pool harms, unanimous wrong predictions
and strict common wrong rivals. For each paired comparison report repairs,
introduced errors, net repairs, persistent wrong classes, coverage gained/lost
and common-rival appearance/clearance/persistence. Cohorts are alphaF-frozen and
overlapping; full-population results remain primary. Common rival means one
same nontruth class strictly above truth in every member; ties are excluded.

A small descriptive extension can split pairwise member disagreement into
exactly-one-correct and both-wrong disagreement, alongside pairwise error
intersection/union counts. This prevents wrong-only disagreement from being
presented as competence. Use the saved prediction arrays; no new graph probe or
fit is needed. When examining reversal of a baseline rival, retain the original
alphaF competitor identity, not a candidate-selected easy rival. These counts
interpret useful alternatives and do not prove a gradient mechanism.

loading, fresh reconstruction, CPU transfer, analysis, serialization, wall/CPU,
RSS/CUDA peaks and storage. Reused historical collection costs remain historical
paid work, not new free inference. Unknown costs remain unknown.

Apply only the unchanged relationJ−alphaF screen and placement attribution gate
