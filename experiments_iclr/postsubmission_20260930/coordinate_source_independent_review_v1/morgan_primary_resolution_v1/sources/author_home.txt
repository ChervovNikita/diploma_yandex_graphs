I am an assistant professor at Wayne State University. My research interests lie in Neural Symbolic AI, Agentic LLMs, knowledge graph reasoning and graph mining, with a growing focus on connecting these to large language models. I lead the AID Lab — AID (AI and Data Science) — working on these topics — visit the lab page for research areas and openings.
