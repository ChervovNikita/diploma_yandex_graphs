is live, the adapter reads ONLY that process's direct-child list at
/proc/<owned PID>/task/<owned PID>/children. It does not enumerate /proc or use
ps/pgrep. A worker is recorded only when its parent, birth ticks, new session,
exact reviewed worker argv, fixed job path/hash and original release/source agree.
WORKER_<pid>_<start>.json retains that verified relation. Transient pre-exec children
can be resolved by the exact reviewed fitter's eventual job/owner/reap closure;
unresolved observations are never signaled or called terminal.

On fit timeout the adapter first stops ONLY the exact owned fit-parent PID, then
captures its direct workers while new admission is frozen. It terminates the exact
parent session and saved verified worker sessions, resuming the stopped parent to
deliver pending TERM. It kills only still-verified sessions within the original
cleanup allowance. Incomplete closure is retained as failure, never successful
all12. Collect/read terminate their owned phase
session directly. A recorded worker surviving unexpected phase exit is similarly
cleaned only after current identity/argv/job revalidation. Numeric PID/group reuse
or lost ownership causes no signal. The adapter claims actual wait/reap only for
its direct phase child; descendant terminal observations and the fitter's own
reap evidence are recorded separately. Missing closure or unresolved descendants
block the fit terminal claim and subsequent reader admission. Original failed,
capped or incomplete outputs/logs are preserved. No resumption or retry.
