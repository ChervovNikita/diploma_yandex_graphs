## 3. Exact gradient mechanics and limits

At one TRAIN node, set `p_m=softmax(z_m)`, `r_m=p_m[y]/sum_l p_l[y]`, and `e_y` to the target indicator. Then

```text
∂F/∂z_m = (1/M) (p_m − e_y)
∂L/∂z_m = r_m (p_m − e_y)
∂(.5F+.5L)/∂z_m = (.5/M + .5r_m) (p_m − e_y).
```

Node averaging and the independent regularizer pullback follow the source reductions. Shared native/scanner parameters sum the route Jacobian pullbacks; each private factor/bias row receives its own route pullback. This is the established correct-class probability responsibility of mixture likelihood. Use the live log_softmax/logsumexp-minus-log(M) scalar; differentiating a separately responsibility-weighted CE with live weights adds another derivative and is not this loss.

For four routes, the weight is `.125+.5r_m` versus own-only `.25`: above-average true-class probability gets more credit; below-average gets less. Correct argmax is not the weighting predicate, so an incorrect high-true-probability route can outweigh a low-confidence correct route. The own half supplies a positive credit floor, **not** a member-competence or diversity guarantee. Algebraically `F−L=KL(U_M || r)` and the mixture equals `L+.5KL(U_M || r)`; it is ordinary pool fitting plus a pressure toward equal true-class probabilities, not guaranteed preservation of distinct correct alternatives.

The Jacobians include clipped logits, all native layers, original learned q gate and scan recurrence. TF1Adam's carried moments and clipping mean this cotangent does not guarantee a useful next update. Zero initial W_o still blocks upstream scanner-map learning at startup; B does not change that initialization fact. TRAIN has 100 labels, while the observed repair cohorts are encountered DEV evidence: their 608 alternatives cannot be supplied as labels, weights, ownership or a new selector. The hypothesis concerns transfer of ordinary TRAIN gradients, not direct supervision of those repairs.
