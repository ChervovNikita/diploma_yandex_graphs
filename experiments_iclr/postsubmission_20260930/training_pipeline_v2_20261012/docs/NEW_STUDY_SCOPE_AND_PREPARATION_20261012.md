complete trajectories, selected-checkpoint restoration, paid continuations,
independent members, source optimizer ownership, distributed gates, runtime

selected ensemble. Private Adam modifies only private R/S epsilon and weight
decay; it does not divide the gradients.

with raw prediction quality as the primary comparison. Historical calibration

new study does not claim to reproduce that historical scoring protocol.

earlier panel. That historical gate, adoption status and outcome remain

prospectively included as unconditional development comparisons after the
whole-search gate. An explicit new policy receipt binds that change. The

heldout accesses or predictive scores. The largest observed Torch allocation
was 24.86 GiB, with 25.375 GiB reserved. These are random-weight TRAIN
forward/backward and serialization proxies. Optimizer kernels, selection,

about 112 seconds per training pass with optimizer actions intercepted. This
invalidates the old short scheduling placeholder. If every pass in a
225-fit, 300-epoch MolHIV roster took this case's time, the training passes
would represent roughly 87.6 days of work before other costs. Route counts
and methods have different costs. This uniform-case arithmetic is a planning
scenario, not a wall-time ETA or measured trained-model throughput.
