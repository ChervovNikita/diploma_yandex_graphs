## One recurring false premise

**A gain over ordinary I4 establishes usefulness beyond a capable single, or identifies an ensemble/sharing effect.** The completed PubMed comparisons contradict that inference twice:

1. Older shared M4 beats ordinary native I4 by +0.684932 pp, but exceeds capable factorized M1 by only +0.084559 pp with +/+/− seed signs. Its pooling lift over mean members is almost zero.
2. Capable factorized I4 beats ordinary native I4 by +0.608828 pp at every seed, but exceeds its included factorized M1 by only +0.008456 pp, with −/+/− signs. Its mean NLL improves 0.045352; that proper-score gain is retained without claiming an accuracy advantage.

These are competing factorization/optimization and reference-strength explanations, not a causal proof of either. M1/M4/I4 differ in coordinates, slow bodies, optimizer state, work and selection. The capable single must stay beside genuine equal-information/equal-serving I4; favorable weaker-reference comparisons cannot choose a new combination or paper claim.
