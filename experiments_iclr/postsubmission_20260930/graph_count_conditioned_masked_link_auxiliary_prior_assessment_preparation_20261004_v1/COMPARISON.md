## Required diagnostic strata and endpoints

Record the coverage of extreme counts k=0/n; categorical/complement cases min(k,n-k)=1; and genuine multielement subset cases min(k,n-k)>1. Cross-side association is active only when both sides are informative. A gain confined to categorical/complement contexts supports mixture pair training, without an ESP-specific subset interpretation. No nontrivial subset coverage leaves that question unanswered. The strata and TRAIN-only diagnostic contexts are frozen before outcomes; no stratum chooses a checkpoint.

The primary endpoint is the authentic complete VALID ranking measure already used by the fixed selector. Retain its entire trace and selected-state replay, adverse outcomes and physical receipts. At the selected states, report the prescribed TRAIN-context whole/side conditional identity NLL relative to the uniform fixed-count baseline, conditional inclusion competence, observed-positive versus observed-zero competence, ordinary source count/offset calibration diagnostics, member responsibility usage and target served-score behavior. Repeated masks/overlapping graph supports are dependent views, not new independent graph samples. P0/J_K/J_K_sep need the same evaluation contexts and denominators.

Fixed-parameter per-side equality isolates a model-family association change. Fitted marginals can diverge, so a J_K ranking gain must be interpreted with side-competence changes in view; training does not provide a pure association-only causal experiment automatically. A conditional-NLL-only improvement fails the useful-ranking hypothesis. J_K matching J_K_sep rejects shared-association utility in this representative recipe. Both auxiliary arms matching P0 rejects usefulness at this coefficient/recipe, without warranting retrospective retuning.

## Claim this screen can support

A favorable paired result could motivate separately fixed replication of a useful native auxiliary adaptation and its shared association mechanism. It cannot establish global novelty, a missing-graph posterior, an MI bound, or necessity of retaining multiple predictive members. A bank-necessity claim needs the already proposed capable identity-sensitive structured single S_K, with the same permitted context/count labels, authentic serving and fully charged extra parameters/sequential work. C_mu and a weak affine single are inadequate sole controls. Distinguishing the fixed-size binary law from a recommendation-style summed categorical/multinomial auxiliary also needs a separately frozen matched comparison; the three-condition screen alone does not establish that advantage.
