# Shared training interpretation after the WikiCS head and association studies

E improves its fresh BASE through more accurate members and fewer correct alternatives lost in pooling, while its members remain weaker than capable independent I4. Receiver conditioning supplies most of the observed gain. The two completed diagnostics sharpen this account without identifying a learning cause: extra affine-head freedom does not improve the served E bank, and the selected conditional predictors use their factual source-context/message association. E's greater served sensitivity than I4 includes a pooling effect, rather than greater loss of member competence.

The prediction-acquisition target is therefore joint training that produces capable members **and** correct alternatives that survive the declared pool. Different graph actions, more accessible alternatives, common-rival removal or increased sensitivity are insufficient individually. A cause such as harmful shared-gradient interference, missing neighborhood information or necessary private builder factors remains a hypothesis. These findings concern three dependent selected-development readouts of 5,274 WikiCS nodes.

## At most three next actions

1. **Close the completed diagnostics as bounded findings.** Retain every harm and capable reference, the negative affine-head criteria and the association pooling decomposition. End the exact head recipe; do not substitute a more favorable class/cohort or call the association diagnostic a training improvement. The leading explanation remains descriptive prediction acquisition and retention.
2. **Finish the unchanged full-procedure studies before choosing an acquisition change.** Judge the complete released E/N/M1/I4 procedures by the frozen whole-population rules, member competence, proper losses, repairs/harms and actual work/storage scopes. A failed complete comparison is not rescued by selected WikiCS sensitivity. Preserve V4; incorporate only fully closed later evidence in a subsequent revision.
3. **If the full E procedure confirms, retain the already specified common-builder acquisition as the sole default attribution.** Train it afresh with separate live route states and native factors, while tying only builder factors; retain own CE, selectors and serving. Its 234,210 parameters versus E's 237,474 expose the 3,264-parameter difference. Matching E would remove the claim that builder-private factors materially help under that protocol. A private win would support this parameterization, not pure tying causality. The saved shared-training credit/capacity proposals stay inactive until a separate evidence-based decision; no new mechanism is nominated by this synthesis.
