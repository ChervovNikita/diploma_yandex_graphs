# Historical hypothesis synthesis and audit

It does not reopen jobs, run scientific/numerical programs, transfer raw arrays, alter scores or shared state, or provide a manuscript verdict. Original five-dataset paper scores remain unchanged.

The accumulated evidence supports rejecting the fixed unsuccessful recipes, while keeping their scope precise. It does not justify a universal conclusion that shared backbones, contrastive learning, or persistent contexts cannot work. The recurring practical difficulty has two parts: member competence and acquisition of useful alternative predictions. Different experiments expose different mixtures of those difficulties. Positive changes against a weak or differently selected reference often fail against the genuine independent ensemble or an objective-matched single.

## Assumptions to revise

1. **A failed recipe is a failed principle.** Negative conclusions apply to exact source, provider, initialization, graph/split, horizon, selector and objective. They do not cover feature-masked reconstruction, independent native scorer starts, or arbitrary end-to-end learning. Conversely, source-only proposals and pending families cannot reverse a completed failure.
2. **Diversity can be measured independently of quality.** Wider coverage/angles/pool-minus-mean can coexist with weak members, wrong alternatives and introduced harm. The question is whether changed correct rankings survive actual serving while mean/worst competence and proper risk remain protected.
3. **Better members are sufficient.** Centered Tolokers and IMDB role3 show why coverage/overlap is separately needed; QK/MolHIV show why member strength cannot be ignored. These are descriptive patterns, not a universal decomposition theorem for AUROC/F1.
4. **Output mixture changes can recover all common errors.** A strict common wrong rival blocks unchanged convex class-probability mixtures. The analogous link statement requires actual common negative identities; global Hits@50 decision overlap alone does not supply them. New graph-dependent predictors or rankings are needed where the obstruction really holds.
5. **A named graph/source objective learns that source.** Root features, shared transformations, bias codes, coarser-peer compensation, or generic weighting can satisfy parts of the objective. Source-aligned versus erased/permuted/rewired controls and factual task quality are indispensable; an increased ablation utility alone is insufficient.
6. **Optimizer/mass and state details are secondary.** Mean versus sum changes coupled-decay/epsilon balance; pooled versus own checkpoint selection changes opportunities; source augmentation changes BN population state. The fresh masked-source audit found a concrete decoder-gradient normalization confound; corrected V2 resolves it at source level. Such details must be fixed or disclosed before attributing gains to parameter tying.
7. **One score improvement narrows the ensemble gap.** NCNC narrows part of its gap but remains below I4; QK widens its matched gap; Amazon calibration heads lose to a processed single. Keep the strongest source-compatible reference and all seed signs visible.
8. **The literature index is a count of papers fully read.** Index_v72 contains 260 conclusion records/207 normalized paper identifiers and explicitly does not certify cumulative full-paper reads. The current supplement v39 has one new bounded scope, zero full papers and reused CORE/view/BE ancestry. This audit adds zero new primary-paper reads. Novelty cannot follow from a scoped absence finding or a new combination name.
