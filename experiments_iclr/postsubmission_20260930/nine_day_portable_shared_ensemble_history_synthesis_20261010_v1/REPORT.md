## Direct answer

We have found useful local effects, but their gains repeatedly fail to survive
the **complete served predictor against equally informed capable references**.
The history contains several different failure mechanisms. A single explanation
such as hidden collapse, gradient conflict, calibration or insufficient private
capacity is not established. Final pooled accuracy and proper risk are decisive;
a useful collective can contain weaker members. Each member need not beat I4.

## What sharing and capacity can explain

Shared dense weights with private diagonal factors restrict the joint parameter
family relative to four independent dense bodies. Different private factors
still generate different nonlinear graph trajectories; the current source does
not compress every route to one common hidden state. Sharing can regularize
learning beneficially, and its restriction alone predicts no accuracy sign.

The completed interventions show a tradeoff: coherent starts repair competence
while losing useful correctness differences; sign starts and bootstrap acquire
alternatives with weaker members and substantial serving losses; global
orthogonal pairs, rank1 LoRA, extra head capacity and live exchange fail their
specific final comparisons. None isolates a universal capacity bottleneck.
Factor coordinates also change the optimizer geometry. PubMed's factorized M1
and I4 revisions demonstrate why this explanation must remain visible.

Costs likewise differ: PubMed factorized I4 stores8.335 M parameters versus shared
2.126 M, has four native bodies/optimizers and own selection. These are genuine
capacity/work differences, not a causal estimate of sharing. Shared storage
does not remove four route-specific graph forwards. No completed result here
certifies a portable quality/resource advantage under a common measured budget.

## Why elapsed effort has not become a result

Implementation qualification and trustworthy negative comparisons consumed
substantial effort. We also revisited already saved GPN and functional-tangent
ideas, carried stale prospective status after closure, and initially used
references later shown weaker than competent singles/I4. Those choices slowed
useful discrimination. The user’s 7 October objection to excessive tiny-FP32 parity
work is already recorded. Readiness, source novelty discussion and long audit
trails do not deliver pooled utility.

Repeated proposal selection on the same development graphs further limits the
meaning of small favorable clues. Three backbones on WikiCS are not three unused
graphs, and aggregator held folds follow all-VALID native checkpoint selection.
The responsible next decision is the whole current pilot, followed by a frozen
unused confirmation only if its complete pooled comparison succeeds. Nine days
of failure establishes the limits of the tried recipes; it establishes no
general impossibility theorem for portable sharing.
