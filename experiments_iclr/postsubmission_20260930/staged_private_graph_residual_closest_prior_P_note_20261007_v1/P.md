TreeNets' identical gradient statement is about the averaging layer's inputs. It does not establish identical logit, parameter, or GNN gradients after different softmax Jacobians and different private representations. For this candidate, Brier supplies the shared residual through J(p_m), while own CE supplies p_m-y. The initial logits may be identical, yet independently drawn graph representations can give different output-weight gradients. ## The exact narrow question

Let H0,z0 be the serving classifier input/output of the selected frozen GNN. A private member computes

`u_m = H0 + V_m x; z_m = z0 + U_m B_m(u_m,A) + c_m; p_m = softmax(z_m)`.

Here B_m is a full private native local GAT/root/h/gate/LayerNorm block. U_m,c_m start at zero. The served predictor is the fixed average of four p_m. At a stage, the active path trains on mean own CE plus `2 * mean ||(p_m + sum(detached serving peers))/4 - y||²`; earlier and future private paths are frozen. The active path has ordinary training dropout while its peers serve deterministically. This is coordinate correction of a probability pool, not ordinary additive-logit/Newton boosting, no matter that z0 anchors every member.

The inspected sources do not jointly specify that entire operation. That leaves a **bounded implementation and mechanism comparison**, not verified originality. Frozen shared trunks, raw/prior hidden inputs, neural/graph residual learning, own/pool loss combinations and staged learning each have close ancestry. The new August2026 AdaGNN method and the unread GENNN/FAGEL bodies remain limitations on exact overlap resolution. Hyperparameters, a different native GNN, zero-output initialization or four members cannot carry a novelty claim by themselves.

The current E_joint arm is interleaved coordinate fitting from common pre-update deterministic peers with detached peer probabilities. It is a useful schedule control. It is not GrowNet's fully corrective refinement of a completed staged model, nor full end-to-end training of a live shared backbone and all ensemble members under one simultaneous objective. Any staging conclusion must name those actual mechanics.
