## 3 Treat acquisition and pooling as separate failures

**Evidence.** Original shared PubMed has 343/356/363 no-correct-member errors and only 4/8/2 lost alternatives: unchanged-vector pooling has little room to help that bank. Strong factorized I4 adds 99/102/93 correct alternatives over its included M1, serves only 38/33/40, and loses 61/69/53 through pooling. Its final mean accuracy gain is just .008456 pp, despite better NLL at every seed. Conversely, normalized own reduces pool losses from 7/5/9 to 1/1/5 while competence, coverage and final quality worsen. [Exact complete flows](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/periodic_completed_history_revision_20261010_v5/REPORT.md>)

**Decision.** Require mean/worst competence, acquired and lost correct coverage, acquired alternatives actually served, full-population repairs/harms and proper risk together. A stronger bank can still have a readout bottleneck; a smaller pooling-loss count can merely reflect fewer useful alternatives. Pooling losses describe serving failure, not its calibration or gradient cause. This keeps useful hidden acquisition and reliable retention distinct when the current families close.
