# Bounded supervised member-error allocation: no new wrapper nomination

10 October 2026. Saved complete evidence and method/gap notes were read before this decision. **Retain no new hypothesis or implementation from this pass.** Bounded extra true-label supervision aimed at weak shared members is a coherent attributed learning rule, but its proposed operations already occur in the saved responsibility, selective-credit and competence-safeguard proposals. The complete CMCL closure also contradicts treating an own-loss floor as competence preservation. This is a bounded queue decision, not a claim that all supervised allocation is useless or fully exhausted in the literature.

## The operation assessed

Consider one live shared bank with shared parameters theta and persistent private parameters phi_m. Every member keeps complete TRAIN CE, F = mean_{i,m} CE(z_m(i), y_i). Add stopped, nonnegative, bounded per-node/member weights a_im to extra true-label CE or correct-versus-competitor margins. Shared coordinates keep the current own-risk gradient; private coordinates receive own risk plus their allocated extra credit. Weights may use current cross-member errors, a correct supplier, member balance or measured private learnability. Inference retains the existing fixed pool.

This family directly supervises factual errors. It introduces no independent teacher, arbitrary embedding repulsion or inference gate. Those exclusions are useful design properties, but they do not supply an untested operation.

## Competence is the unresolved claim

For finite positive weights w_1 < w_2, take two node CE derivatives a and -b with b > 0 and b < a < (w_2/w_1)b. The unweighted member derivative is positive, while the weighted derivative is negative. A sufficiently small SGD step on weighted CE therefore increases its own mean CE. This saved analytic counterexample applies even with bounded weights and a positive own floor. It is not a native-model observation. Adam moments, decay, shared movement and later private/shared feedback add further trajectory differences.

The complete private-CMCL18 decision supplies direct empirical scope: private credit versus matched own_floor loses pooled accuracy at all three seeds (mean -0.668017 pp), mean-member accuracy by 1.109840 pp and worst-member accuracy by 2.359207 pp. It increases net available-correct coverage at two seeds, yet loses final serving quality and proper risk. Thus specialist acquisition can occur alongside member harm and lost alternatives. This rejects that exact recipe; it does not reject all specialization. It does refute using retained access to all TRAIN labels as evidence that competence was preserved.

A first-order displacement halfspace limits only the declared realized TRAIN-risk contribution. It does not certify finite-step own-risk nonincrease, future trajectory, VALID accuracy or useful complementarity. Exact finite-loss rejection would be another declared optimizer contract with paid evaluations and a moment/clock convention, not a held-performance guarantee. It is not nominated here.

## Decisive capable references and expected failure

Any genuinely different future operation must first be specified beyond weight selection, clipping, a floor or the saved protected step. Its decisive learning-rule reference is the **same complete shared multibranch bank under ordinary own-plus-actual-pool supervision**, matching data, parameterization, gradient mass, update/state convention, full selection opportunity and fixed serving. Uniform extra CE/margins distinguishes added supervision dose; an otherwise identical selector-free or unprotected rule isolates any claimed allocation/protection change. These are reference obligations, not a new roster.

Capable factorized M1-native, the applicable matched multi-view/own-loss single, and genuine separately fitted factorized I4 remain necessary practical references. A one-head weak single, jointly selected untied bank, or exact relabeling of the same bank cannot replace them. The all-branch graph can reproduce the same functions and update rule; equality under relabeling is not a quality gate.

Expected failures are: allocation stays uniform or ceases after TRAIN errors vanish; a hardest weak route lacks private freedom or useful gradient response; positive weights change cancellation and worsen its own risk; specialization increases oracle supply while the fixed pool loses it; or generic own/pool supervision and capable M1/I4 explain the gain. Member-strength improvement alone can also destroy coverage, as the saved complete SAGE exchange comparison demonstrates. Complete served quality, mean/worst competence, acquired-and-served alternatives, full repairs/harms and proper risk must agree before a later claim. No coefficient, assignment or protection grid follows from this no-go.
