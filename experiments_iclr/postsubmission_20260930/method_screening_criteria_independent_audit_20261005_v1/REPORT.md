# Independent audit of method-screening criteria

**The active endpoint-private-learning idea remains an unresolved empirical test. It has not been logically rejected.** Broad novelty for differentiated private learning is already contradicted by retained prior. The exact graph/persistence design, its utility and the strength of its methodological increment remain open. Some successor-screen language goes beyond what its premises establish; the quality target and frozen gates should stay unchanged.

This audit reads retained reports and the saved control specification only. It performs no literature search, primary reread, outcome-artifact review, code change or experiment. Historical observations quoted in the triage are not reassessed or used as evidence here.

## Passage audit and corrected criteria

Items1 and3 identify overreaching wording. Item2 is a valid boundary that must not be promoted into an impossibility criterion.

1. **A capable single's availability is not a logical rejection of a sharing method.**

   Information-failure scout, [line58](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/graph_shared_backbone_information_failure_scout_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:58>): “The failure is concrete, but the ensemble-specific hypothesis does not survive the information boundary and capable-single comparator.” Its [line54](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/graph_shared_backbone_information_failure_scout_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:54>) also rejects an upstream intervention because it “adopts an already available single-model propagation remedy without evidence that sharing improves quality.”

   The fixed-input information boundary is valid. The upstream conclusion is stronger than the evidence: no trained capable-single comparison was performed, and neither representability nor an available propagation remedy rules out a useful finite-sample sharing/optimization extension. The report's decision not to promote its under-specified successor is defensible; presenting that upstream extension as logically defeated is not.

   **Correct criterion:** reject a downstream remedy for a proved fixed-input obstruction under those exact assumptions. For an upstream or representation-changing design, credit the propagation prior, specify the substantive sharing/training change, and treat its added benefit as unproved until strong comparators test it. A method need not prove ensembles fundamentally necessary or introduce a new propagation primitive.

2. **A valid applicability boundary must not invalidate a shared extension.**

   Persistent-graph follow-up, [line53](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/persistent_graph_private_learning_credit_followup_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:53>): “Their interpolations and proximity terms can also be applied to an untied bank and encoded by a capable branched single with the same blocks, streams and paid paths.”

   The literal passage correctly prevents claims of a representational property exclusive to sharing. It does not explicitly prove a shared extension useless; treating it as such a rejection rule would overreach. Failure to establish the new papers' structural/semantic problem in the current task is the stronger reason that scout had no supported successor.

   **Correct criterion:** compare the shared extension with relevant untied/independent versions and attribute its inherited operations. Require evidence appropriate to a claim about tying; do not require a learning rule that is undefined or useless when untied. Benefits from regularization, estimation, optimization or resource allocation may be legitimate. Resource benefits cannot replace this project's required predictive gains.

3. **Similar gains do not uniquely identify a causal mechanism.**

   Method decision, [line17](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/shared_ensemble_method_decision_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:17>): “That pattern explains the gain through generic meta-regularization” and “It rejects the ensemble-specific endpoint-credit explanation for that gain.” The antecedent is similar live gains under both geometries and capable-single/untied models matching the shared predictor.

   That hypothetical pattern would weaken a claim that endpoint eligibility or sharing is essential. It would be consistent with generic meta-regularization. Similar point estimates, especially in a small descriptive study with the disclosed role mismatch, do not demonstrate practical equivalence or identify the true cause. These are conditional screening predictions, not observed findings in this audit.

   **Correct criterion:** describe whether the complete contrasts support or fail to support the prespecified endpoint/sharing prediction. Reject a particular practical-effect claim only with evidence capable of excluding that effect, under the relevant comparison assumptions. Do not turn a missing detectable interaction into proof of a generic cause or rejection of the whole training method.

## Valid objections that should remain

- **Exact operator redundancy is stronger than function-class equivalence.** Counterfactual synthesis [line48](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/private_update_marginal_value_design_synthesis_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:48>) identifies routed marginal credit with the existing served-loss meta-gradient under its stated routing/state conventions. That can reject the claim of an *additional credit pathway*. Its counterexample can reject a guarantee that increasing the proposed reward improves served predictions. Neither proves that every attributed objective variant has no empirical utility; the report already preserves that distinction at lines3 and129.
- **The information-loss claim is conditional and valid.** Information scout [line18](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/graph_shared_backbone_information_failure_scout_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:18>) holds fixed all common inputs and changes only downstream heads. Equal complete inputs cannot produce query-specific distinctions. Its line20 explicitly allows a changed encoder or extra context. The active rule updates the shared encoder, so this fixed-input argument does not logically reject it.
- **Unmatched roles limit attribution.** Partition audit [line5](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/shared_private_transfer_member_count_partition_audit_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:5>) says the capable-single contrast does not isolate member count. That is correct: outer/private allocation, label access, adaptation capacity and optimizer history differ. It remains a useful whole-algorithm quality comparison. Role matching is needed to attribute a gain specifically to member count or a sharing interaction, not to declare every whole-method comparison meaningless. Its line41 correctly preserves the richer single as a quality alternative.
- **Strong single and independent-ensemble comparisons remain essential.** Quality triage [line5](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/quality_literature_to_design_triage_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:5>) treats a stronger single with the same structural bias as a necessary comparator. Its line24 treats the graph supervision condition's additional benefit as a scientific question. These are sound criteria. The endpoint-by-live interaction in line30 concerns the narrower interaction explanation; it is not a universal prerequisite for every possible contribution.

## Three equivalences that must not be conflated

**Representability:** a sufficiently rich single can express the same function. This challenges a representational-necessity claim; it says nothing decisive about which training procedure finds a better predictor with finite data and a real budget.

**Exact reimplementation:** counterfactual synthesis [line116](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/private_update_marginal_value_design_synthesis_20261005_v1/REPORT.md:116>) constructs one graph with the same M private blocks, output, optimizer states and streams. That is the same algorithm packaged as a branched “single,” not an independent baseline the method must outperform. Its equality properly excludes a representational distinction based only on naming. It does not prove methodological contributions impossible.

**Prior-operation equality:** if the purported new update/gradient is exactly an existing operation under matched state, normalization and routing, its new-mechanism claim fails. A broader contribution would still need a substantive, correctly attributed design or analysis beyond renaming that operation; improved scores alone do not supply novelty.

The saved [finite-sample note, line51](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/quality_literature_to_design_triage_20261005_v1/FINITE_SAMPLE_CONDITIONS.md:51>) uses the proper standard: an equally representable shrinkage solution motivates a capable comparator rather than an ensemble-necessity theorem. Architecture expressivity, attainable trained performance and a method's contribution are different questions.

## Disposition of the active idea and required evidence

**Keep endpoint-private learning as the unchanged unresolved empirical test.** Retained prior covers its meta-learning principle. No retained argument proves its endpoint-conditioned, persistent, recomputed graph training sequence cannot help. Conversely, an unmatched combination of ingredients, a correct derivative or a positive development contrast would not alone establish a substantive advance.

Evidence distinguishing a methodological advance from a recipe/application should include:

1. **A precise attributed increment:** specify the eligibility, shared/private roles, optimizer-state treatment, outer objective and commitment schedule; explain a coherent reason those coupled choices address the graph learning problem. Compare that increment with the closest applicable prior, rather than demand a new mathematical principle.
2. **Demonstrated quality:** complete the existing authenticated study and gates, including competent strong singles and true independent ensembles, with support, compute, fitting and selection differences disclosed. An untied jointly trained bank is not an independently trained ensemble. Do not require superiority over a syntactic clone of the proposed algorithm.
3. **Claim-matched attribution:** the F4 endpoint/random × live/detached contrast tests the specified interaction. The saved role mismatch and absent untied factorial limit stronger member-count/uniquely-sharing claims. Lack of those comparisons narrows what may be concluded; it does not logically reject the algorithm. This audit adds no fits or control requirements to the frozen cohort.
4. **Representative fresh tasks and honest limits:** confirm a locked method on fresh tasks under competent fitting and selection, including conditions where endpoint eligibility loses useful supervision or tying suppresses useful private evidence. Keep the known correlated-history/context limitation; do not claim independent tasks, unbiased cross-fitting or unseen-node generalization from endpoint eligibility alone.

A coherent nontrivial extension with those gains and appropriate attribution can be an ML method contribution even when a rich single represents its predictor and untied models admit its rule. If the demonstrated improvement is explained entirely by a standard prior operation plus dataset-specific choices, with no substantive design or analysis beyond that adaptation, the narrower recipe/application description is warranted. The retained evidence does not yet choose between those descriptions.

No promotion gate, quality objective, selector, access restriction or frozen experiment is relaxed. No acceptance verdict, global novelty clearance or positive outcome is implied.
