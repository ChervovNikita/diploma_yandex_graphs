Our Proposed Method: AdaTask
It is observed in the above section existing MTL optimization
methods still suffer from task dominance, and such domi-
nance leads to the dominance of learning rate in optimizers.
In order to tackle such dominance, in this section, we propose
a Task-wise Adaptive learning rate approach, to separate the
accumulative gradients of each task for each shared parame-
ter. We name this approach asAdaTask.
