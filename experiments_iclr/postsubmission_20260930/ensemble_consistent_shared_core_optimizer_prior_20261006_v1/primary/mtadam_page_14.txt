Figure 9: Samples from the CycleGAN experiments employed on the zebra2horse dataset, mapping horse
images to zebras. CycleGAN-Balanced-Adam employs CycleGAN with the prescribed weights (Zhu et al.,
2017). CycleGAN-Unbalanced-Adam runs with unbalanced initial weights, which fails to generate zebra images.
CycleGAN-Unbalanced-MTAdam (our method) starts with unbalanced weights but produces images of the same
