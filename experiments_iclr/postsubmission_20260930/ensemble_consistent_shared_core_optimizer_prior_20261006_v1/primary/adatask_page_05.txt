Experiments
In this section, we experimentally verify the effectiveness of
the proposed AdaTask. We conduct experiments on a syn-
thetic dataset, and several real-world datasets: the CityScapes
dataset in computer vision, and the TikTok and WeChat
datasets in recommender systems. For the baseline methods,
we choose two loss weighting approaches GradNorm (Chen
et al. 2018), UW (Kendall, Gal, and Cipolla 2018), and
two gradient conﬂict-avoiding approaches PCGrad (Yu et al.
2020) and CAGrad (Liu et al. 2021). More details of our ex-
periment, including baselines, datasets, and implementation
details, are provided in Appendix B.
We adopt the commonly used SharedBottom architecture
(mainly based on fully connected layers) and RMSProp op-
timizer in the synthetic and recommendation datasets. In
the computer vision dataset, we choose the commonly used
MTAN architecture (including complex operations such as
convolution and attention mechanisms, etc.) and the Adam
optimizer. Each experiment is repeated over 3 random seeds
