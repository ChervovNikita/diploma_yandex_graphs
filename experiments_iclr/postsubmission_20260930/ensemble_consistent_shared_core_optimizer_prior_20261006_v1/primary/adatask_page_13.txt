Appendix F: Adaptive Learning Rate Methods
(RMSProp and Adam) v.s. SGD Method
In this section, we compare the performance of the adaptive
learning rate methods and the global learning rate method
on the CityScapes dataset. Like CAGrad (Liu et al. 2021),
we use average per-task performance improvement to mea-
sure the overall performance improvement of RMSProp and
Adam over SGD. As shown in Table 11, we ﬁnd that adap-
tive learning rate approaches (RMSProp and Adam) perform
better than the SGD. Speciﬁcally, in the CityScapes dataset,
we observe a 28.33% increase in RMSProp relative to SGD
and a 27.86% increase in Adam relative to SGD. However, as
pointed out in this paper, these traditional adaptive learning
rate optimizers accumulate gradients across tasks in multi-
task learning. Therefore they still suffer from accumulative
gradient dominance.
