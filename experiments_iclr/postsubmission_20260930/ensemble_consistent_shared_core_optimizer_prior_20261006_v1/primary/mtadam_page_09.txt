6 Conclusions
MTAdam is shown to be a widely applicable op-
timizer, which can dynamically balance multiple
loss terms in an effective way. It is a general algo-
rithm, which can ﬁnd its usage in additional types
of tasks that require the optimization of multiple
terms, such as domain adaptation and some forms
of self-supervised learning. Our code is attached
as supplementary. MTAdam is implemented as a
generic pytorch optimizer and applying it is almost
as simple as applying Adam.
