No proofs, author implementation or full paper was read. Experiment prose/numerical claims and a proposition statement were exposed in these sections; no performance or theorem is adopted.

An action GNN π receives each node's current state and its original neighbors' states. It predicts four actions: standard (send and receive), listen, broadcast, isolate. Straight-through Gumbel-softmax samples an action. The environment GNN η receives an external message `u→v` only when v listens/standard and u broadcasts/standard. Broadcast/isolate nodes update from their own state without external messages. Each layer can therefore induce a different directed subgraph of the original support. π and η can be ordinary Sum/Mean/GCN/GIN/GAT networks; §5.1 says the action network is shared across depth. AppendixE.1 gives a node-state-dependent learned inverse temperature. This is learned computation topology, not merely final output reweighting or a fixed augmentation.

## A precise failure mechanism, with an unproved premise

Suppose the same misleading source context is transmitted into all members before useful evidence can affect their ranking. A source-broadcast decision paired with a receiver-listen decision can suppress that context for a recipient while retaining another informative path. Changing the computation before aggregation can change member logits; a fixed convex output combiner cannot repair a common wrong competitor while all existing members retain that ordering.

Wiki24 establishes the output barrier and competence/coverage tradeoff. It **does not establish that misleading broadcasts caused its errors**, that its recomputed private attention lacks the needed capacity, or that CoGNN actions will discover useful alternatives. A source useful to one receiver may harm another; node-wide broadcasting is more restrictive than edge-specific attention. Learned policies may agree, remove necessary paths, isolate too much, or weaken members. Distinct action maps alone do not establish complementary evidence.

## Exact prior overlap

- **CoGNN:** learned listen/broadcast/isolate communication, dynamic directed support and cooperative action/environment networks are direct prior. Four private policies do not create a new principle.
- **DIVE:** continuing learned member masks and own supervision are already established. Its saved scope explicitly uses separate encoders and selected-single serving; a shared uniformly pooled adaptation must earn its own utility.
- **GEENI:** its indexed abstract's likely-error-node outgoing-message suppression is especially close. Full methods remain unavailable; this scout certifies no exact difference.
- **GNN-FiLM / native attention:** incoming-message modulation and recipient-conditioned evidence transformation are prior; the actual internal BE model can already change attention through private nonlinear states. No universal boundary-factor incapacity is claimed.
- **GMoE / Node-MoE:** node-conditioned hop/filter expert combination and intermediate graph mixtures are prior. CoGNN changes permitted communication rather than just expert weights; both are supervised topology/evidence selection.
- **Graph augmentation / GNN-Ensemble:** different supports and feature subspaces are prior. Learned task-conditioned policies differ operationally from fixed random views, without establishing novelty or semantic invariance.
- **Boundary/internal BE and GNCL:** private multiplicative maps, persistent nonlinear member trajectories and own/pool risk are prior. Graph-side credit placement is a known-component empirical question, not novelty by combination.

**Disposition:** retain this single conditional communication-credit question, attributed to CoGNN and GNCL. There is no justified originality claim and no demonstrated native graph-evidence failure yet. If current ordinary risk placement already repairs competence, or new policies have no advantage over capable controls, close this route rather than rename another gate or optimizer.
