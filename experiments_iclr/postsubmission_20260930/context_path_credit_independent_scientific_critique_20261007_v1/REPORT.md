# Independent scientific critique of the frozen context-path-credit proposal

Reviewed 7 October 2026. Proposal MANIFEST SHA-256: `64694b8e31e5f4044dd78d2ea9865ae70f449094c5de96e90909916d4c48bf6f`; all five listed payloads match their hashes. This is a source-only proposal critique, not manuscript peer review or an acceptance recommendation. No author conversation/history, dataset, model, checkpoint, raw outcome, server, or training execution was accessed. Past-result sentences already present in NOTE.md were treated as supplied context; their linked outcome reports were not reopened or used as independent evidence.

**Disposition:** a scientifically coherent, narrow robustness/coordinate-selection hypothesis with substantial attribution and activity limits. The current pair can screen the total effect of the specified recipient rule on one developmental trajectory. It cannot establish a graph-specific complementarity mechanism, novelty, or superiority over ordinary gated learning, graph-view training, path masking, or GNCL. Native implementation parity remains unqualified. A null can make this frozen recipe inapplicable on this graph without disproving the broader idea.

## Ranked concrete blockers

1. **The claimed consensus mechanism exceeds what its derivative measures.** `s_m,e` differentiates the *pooled*, cohort-averaged ranking risk through member m. Positive values in every member certify agreement about contributions to that pooled objective, not that every member's own wrong-ranking margin worsens, nor that every cohort node is harmed by the edge. For one cohort node, its sign depends on `dp_m(k)/pbar(k) - dp_m(y)/pbar(y)`; the member's own log-ratio uses `p_m(k)` and `p_m(y)` instead. These can have opposite signs. For example, member probabilities `(k,y)=(.6,.3)`, other members `(.4,.3)`, and local changes `(.10,.06)` give an improving member log-ratio derivative `-.0333` but an adverse pooled derivative proportional to `+.0222`. This is a valid infinitesimal probability direction. The operational rule is meaningful, but should be named and interpreted as consensus *pooled-risk contribution* selection.

   Attention normalization also couples the entire receiving row. For its aggregate `mu=sum_e a_e h_e`, `dmu/db_e=a_e(h_e-mu)`. Suppressing one selected coordinate increases relative weights on unselected neighbors. Disjoint recipient coordinates therefore do not imply disjoint affected messages, independent evidence, or distinct predictive patterns. This matters directly to any claimed path explanation.

2. **The pair identifies a recipient policy's total effect, not the proposed graph mechanism.** Both conditions have the extra supervised edge table, the same kind of label-derived challenge, the same pooled ranking objective, and the same guard. Consequently, a favorable difference can support gradient-based coordinate selection over random sparse coordinate descent. It cannot show that the whole added correction improves over ordinary gated learning or distinguish its gains from generic graph robustness, pooled-loss optimization, or path masking.

   After the first divergent update, each condition's current state creates its own challenge, cohort, eligible counts and acceptance history. The view is frozen *within an opportunity*, not across the paired trajectories. “Matched” should specify whether row counts are matched within each arm's own state or across arms; the former does not imply equal paired exposure. Matching count and maximum coordinate magnitude also leaves different L2 step norms, directional derivatives, clipping and acceptance rates. The selected arm only decreases positive-gradient biases; the random-support arm can increase or decrease them. Those are valid policy differences, but weaken a claim about path identity alone. Shared initialization and predeclared ordinary-view/RNG coupling, plus per-opportunity exposure logs, are required to interpret the pair. Same-state branched action diagnostics would separately identify the immediate coordinate-policy effect; they would not replace trajectory comparisons.

3. **Activity and feasible correction are unproven, and the dose is small.** Clean-TRAIN perfection is avoided only if this particular challenge produces a wrong competitor shared by all four members. Individual challenged mistakes with different competitors do not suffice. Empty `U`, no consensus coordinates, clipped directions, and universal guard rejection remain distinct failure modes. A gradient can be nonconstant but negligible, making its extrema a weak basis for a finite perturbation.

   At a strict minimum of a member's clean empirical loss, any nonzero direction in a positive-curvature subspace raises that loss to second order. The requirement that every member and the pool have nonincreasing clean CE can therefore reject all corrections; preserving correct decisions does not alleviate this CE constraint. Four fixed scales need not reach an existing smaller feasible step. Deterministic arithmetic still needs a declared dtype, finite-value handling and exact comparison convention, without relaxing the frozen guard after seeing activity.

   With the specified constant learning rate, a coordinate receives at most `55*.001=.055` total direct correction in log-bias units, versus challenge magnitude `log(2)=.693`. That bounds the direct multiplicative change at about 5.7%, before subsequent ordinary-learning feedback. This does not prove ineffectiveness, but an inactive/null screen cannot separate deficient credit from inadequate dose. Log each activity stage and accepted scales; retain the proposal's prohibition on manufacturing activity post hoc.

4. **The native attention hook is not yet source-qualified.** The supplied native file constructs `GATConv(..., add_self_loops=False, bias=False)` at lines 113–114, without an explicit attention-dropout argument. Its visible dropout at lines 149–151 and 162 is feature/activation dropout; the recipe's `.5` does not establish `.5` attention dropout. The proposal's “native attention dropout” wording therefore needs qualification against the actual pinned PyG implementation. Self loops, duplicate/directed edges, row grouping and persistent edge-table identity depend on preprocessing not established here. The native residual `lins[i](x)` remains separate from attention at line 160. An equivalent zero-bias forward, bias application exactly once before incoming softmax, native support/order, optimizer membership, projection, trial restoration, local/global transition and complete joint snapshots are prerequisites for an interpretable implementation. This review does not certify them from an import and constructor alone.

5. **TRAIN-only construction avoids direct label leakage but does not establish transfer.** Using TRAIN labels to construct and learn a challenge is supervised training, provided VALID/TEST labels never enter its cohort, competitor, eligibility, guard or response-panel selection. Transductive graph features/support can be used under the declared task, but global attention means TRAIN losses can assign credit to edge rows far from labeled nodes. An edge-indexed table can specialize to this one graph; it supplies additional memorization capacity and no inductive evidence rule. A guard on the same fitted TRAIN population can preserve that memorization while development competence worsens.

   VALID already chooses a maximum among complete snapshots. A difference reported on that same population is a developmental screen with selection optimism, even with equal selection schedules; one seed provides no replication estimate. Graph nodes are dependent and should not be presented as independent experimental replicates. Freeze the diagnostic response panel, thresholds and readout before any outcomes, and distinguish cohort surrogate decreases from clean full-population net repairs. `C_V` can decrease through probability changes without correcting a decision or producing complementary errors.

6. **Closest-prior clearance is incomplete.** The proposal candidly avoids a novelty claim; maintain that limitation. The scoped GEENI indexed abstract already makes error-conditioned outgoing-message suppression and diverse ensembles a particularly close conceptual antecedent, but supplies no exact method evidence to certify a difference. Missing primary retrieval is uncertainty, not novelty evidence. The scoped papers below support operational distinctions only; their results and whole-paper coverage were not audited here.

## What would make the representative screen informative

The frozen two-arm pair can meaningfully reject usefulness of this exact recipient policy on the representative graph if it is inactive, cannot pass the guard, or has no useful improvement over its random-support counterpart. A positive outcome supports only that narrow comparison. For attribution beyond it, the proposal's own obligations are material: the same gated bank with ordinary own learning; unrestricted correction with the same challenge/risk/guard; and a competent same-architecture GNCL reference, all with disclosed acquisition, update work, selection and serving rules. A qualified DIVE/SuGAr or related learned-evidence comparator is necessary only for the broader graph-diversity claim and must disclose adaptation differences.

The mechanism evidence must join three facts: changed relative neighbor-response behavior, improved clean full-population served decisions/NLL, and retained member competence. Common-competitor repairs, more coverage, or distinct coordinates alone supply none of those jointly. The proposed pair is useful as a bounded falsification screen once source semantics are qualified; it is insufficient as a representative demonstration of the entire method's advantage.
