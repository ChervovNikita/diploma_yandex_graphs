## Decision

**Close this fixed private-CMCL recipe as an unsupported improvement.** Private CMCL loses to its matched own_floor on accuracy, NLL and macro-F1 at every seed; it also loses accuracy to all-block CMCL and vanilla CMCL at every seed and to both capable M1 singles at every seed. Its small positive contextual ordinary-I4 comparison is not the strongest-reference result.

**Specialist learning did change useful factual predictions, but at competence and serving cost.** Private credit increases net correct-member coverage at two seeds and loses coverage at the third. It creates a substantial lost-alternative pool, unlike the nearly redundant own bank. This revises the earlier “mostly no alternatives to pool” explanation for this new bank, while preserving that explanation for the original unchanged banks.

A pooling remedy is now a plausible *partial diagnostic hypothesis* because correct alternatives are present. It is not an adequate standalone next success claim: the frozen private bank cannot beat M1-native in seed9307 under any nonnegative class-shared mixture, and reweighting cannot restore its weak individual members. No new pooling reader or weight/threshold search is justified to decide the current recipe.

## What pooling can and cannot do in the new private bank

For a node with a strict common false rival r above truth t in all members, every mixture sum_m w_m p_m with w_m≥0 and sum_mw_m=1 preserves r>t. Allowing weights to depend on that node does not change this fact. Class-dependent transforms, new member functions or different decision rules are outside this bound.

The private bank has strict common-rival303/291/359 errors and no-correct307/292/360. The4/1/1 no-correct nodes lacking the stored strict witness are *not proven recoverable*. They only enlarge a conservative upper bound.

It could still improve some errors or a mean; neither possibility preserves member competence or establishes useful specialization.

This **supersedes the tiny4/8/2 reweighting ceiling only for the new CMCL bank**. The original shared/M1-reference bank and matched own_floor remain nearly redundant. It does not imply that a gate should be added to the next protocol.
