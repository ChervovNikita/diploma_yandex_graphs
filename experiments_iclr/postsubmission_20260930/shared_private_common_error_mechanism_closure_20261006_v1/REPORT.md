**No distinct learning-credit extension is retained in this branch, and it supplies no new pilot or GPU request.** The strongest apparent extension is to train shared features so member-private corrections transfer to other supervised queries. That mechanism has strong prior support, but it is already present in the current live learning rule. The saved evidence does not establish a missing operation or a shared-specific failure that another learning-credit rule would repair. This closes this exact branch; it does not reject broader quality ideas or the possible quality of known-method compositions. ## The mechanism and the limit

With a private supervised step phi'=phi-alpha grad_phi L_S, the local change of an outer loss is

\[
F_R(\theta,\phi')-F_R(\theta,\phi)
=-\alpha\langle\nabla_\phi F_R,\nabla_\phi L_S\rangle+O(\alpha^2).
\]

The cross-query private Jacobian kernel determines which correction on S can help R. Changing the shared representation can change that transfer. Useful correction could repair a mistake common to several members, without a disagreement reward or another graph/sign prior. Finite updates, the actual optimizer, susceptibility and the serving map matter; this local expression is not a quality guarantee.

The current G0 map already evaluates the shared objective after private adaptation, differentiates its complete direct and adaptation-mediated shared credit, and recomputes the private update at theta+ from original private state. Its responsibility generator adds further live credit. Adding generic private lookahead, extra inner steps or a new name for transferable feature learning would not identify a distinct repair mechanism. The older NCN raw-logit/BCE argument also cannot be imported as an identity for the current softmax probability-mean serving rule.

The architecture does not have one fixed activation tensor followed only by terminal heads. Its source-defined private stem factors act before every complete nonlinear graph trajectory; private local/global head factors act afterward. A claim that the shared representation has erased the information required for correction therefore needs to include the complete decoder-visible paths. No such information collision or inability of the admitted private learner was established in the saved assessments. Enlarging a private adapter would currently be an undiagnosed capacity change.

## Why sharing could help quality and why it may not

One constrained representation supporting several private learning trajectories could reduce encoder estimation noise, while distinct competent private paths make complementary corrections. That offers a conditional route to beating independently acquired encoders: useful common structure is estimated jointly without collapsing the private corrections. It can fail when sharing preserves a common bias, when the corrections are redundant, or when independent encoders learn the same useful structure with less bias.

A single adaptation path could miss heterogeneous correctable patterns that several private paths cover. This offers a conditional route to a better served ensemble, provided the members remain competent and the fixed probability pool yields net repairs. A capable single with the same information and learning opportunity can also learn such patterns. Indeed, a complete shared bank is itself one nonlinear multibranch predictor, and an untied bank can contain a tied solution. There is no expressivity argument here that forces superiority over either comparator.

These are reasons to test the already specified learning rule. They do not establish that another extension should be added before its competent comparisons are interpreted. Lower internal loss, a utility-trajectory proxy, extra rank or larger disagreement cannot supply the missing served-quality evidence.

## One necessary falsifier before any further pilot

On predetermined training/development queries, hold one supervised private correction fixed. Determine whether it improves the actual served predictor at the original shared state, then whether an ordinary shared displacement erases that benefit while the private state is held fixed. A sharing-specific explanation additionally needs that erosion to exceed the corresponding same-operation untied behavior. If useful correction is not erased, stop the shared-erasure explanation. If a role-matched single or untied bank reproduces the benefit, narrow it to generic adaptation or ensemble training rather than a special value of sharing.

This is a necessary diagnostic distinction, not a designed intervention or a claim that the recorded models show the pattern. A real extension would still have to specify what operation prevents the identified damage without merely preserving overfitted training predictions or introducing new pooled errors. The existing live/detached, matched utility and ordinary comparisons are the first evidence to interpret. No additional fit is proposed here.

Consequently this learning-credit branch supplies no new pilot. The separate architectural note gives one conditional developmental screen for private propagation capacity. For any later intervention, source, comparisons, budgets and one selector must be fixed before held scoring. Held labels and scores remain closed, no subgroup or hyperparameter grid is proposed, and the current fits and seven-arm family stay unchanged. More training time or a longer private horizon is not a supported substitute for the missing mechanism in this branch. **Additional GPU allocation requested: none.**
