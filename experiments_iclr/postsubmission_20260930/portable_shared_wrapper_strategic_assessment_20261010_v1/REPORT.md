# Why portable shared ensemble utility remains difficult

10 October 2026. **The completed evidence makes the difficulty real.** No fixed
shared wrapper has yet delivered protected predictive improvement over capable,
equally equipped independent ensembles across the tested native backbones.
Several local hooks survive. Their comparative limits prevent a portable result;
nine days of work establish no general impossibility or manuscript verdict.

Ordinary I4 itself improves mean accuracy and NLL over ordinary M1 on SAGE,
GCN and GAT. That is evidence that competent independent acquisition and averaging
can work, and a reason to retain it as a serious reference. Shared storage savings
are real architecture properties; four private nonlinear graph trajectories and
actual work still have to be charged. No portable quality/resource advantage is
established by parameter count alone.

## What obstructs portability

The observed failures involve different mechanisms. Some shared banks have weak
members or few correct alternatives. Others acquire alternatives and discard
them in pooling, or repair errors while introducing enough harms to cancel the
gain. Coherent starts can improve members while making correctness redundant;
exchange can improve mean members while lowering pool accuracy. These observations
support the competence/acquisition/serving distinction. They do not identify a
universal cause such as hidden collapse or gradient conflict.

Capable comparisons repeatedly remove apparent benefits. An added information
or serving operation can help independent bodies too. Sharing restricts the
joint parameter family and couples learning; that can regularize beneficially
or couple errors. Neither implication determines an accuracy sign. Genuine I4
also differs in capacity, optimizer ownership and selection opportunity, so
quality and measured costs must be reported together.

The complete live-retrieval and recurrent-decoder rules now fail their full
criteria. The graph decoder gains 0.145367 points over original shared, misses
the frozen threshold, loses to retrained P=I on average and worsens NLL.
Zero same-state strict-rival repairs describes its selected terminal operation.
Its full procedure repairs 274 original shared errors and harms 251; those
cross-state changes include learning, stopping and selection. They do not
identify repairs within the original strict-rival cohort. A conditional zero
must not be promoted into a claim that training repaired no original error.

## Where our search overinvested

We qualified implementation and developed symbolic mechanisms before obtaining
some of the strongest whole-family controls. That work made failures trustworthy,
but excessive tiny FP32/parity work and repeated source/theory packets delayed
the utility question. Strong factorized singles and genuine I4 should have
been discriminating controls earlier.

We also revisited saved ideas and carried stale prospective status after
completion. Generic disagreement, geometry, credit assignment or more heads
were sometimes given attention before a concrete route to retained correct
predictions was established. Exact joint repair identities arrived late for
the bootstrap-serving combination. Literature ancestry and source readiness
were too easily treated as progress toward predictive advantage.

Repeated method selection on the same development graphs further weakens small
positive clues. Three seeds are optimization repeats, and calibration/fusion
folds after whole-VALID checkpoint selection are encountered development.
They do not create unused confirmation. None of these workflow criticisms
changes a preserved result or supplies a causal diagnosis of model failure.

   mechanism test.** Read no partial quality. Its exact hidden-neighborhood

2. **Only after a protected positive, freeze one portability comparison of the

   A failed current screen earns no automatic replacement mechanism or rescue grid.
