# Graph conditioned private correction training map

Treat the candidate as an attributed extension of specialization and meta weighting: **allocate extra competitor supervision by finite private learning response, couple those responsibilities through the public graph and balanced mass, and train the shared core through the complete allocation and adaptation map.** Reproducible quality gains under the existing close comparisons can support that methodological contribution. A new universal principle, deployed function class or tangent-rank increase is not a prerequisite.

## The operation defined by the current source

At fixed shared parameters θ, member m takes one discarded private own-CE step on training S. For every node i and every competitor k≠yᵢ, define:

```text
bᵢₖ,ₘ = softplus(zₘ(i,k) − zₘ(i,yᵢ))
φ̂ₘ = φₘ − ηprobe ∂φₘ CE_S(θ,φₘ)
cᵢₖ,ₘ = bᵢₖ,ₘ(θ,φ̂ₘ) − bᵢₖ,ₘ(θ,φₘ)
```

Negative c means this private step reduces that competitor loss. It need not repair the node's predicted class. Costs are centered across members and divided by one smooth RMS scale per unordered class pair. Thus Q allocates relative response, with sensitivity to the fixed ε scale.

For each pair, Q starts uniformly and takes exactly eight differentiated feasible descent steps for:

```text
⟨normalized c,Q⟩ + τ Σ Q log(MQ) + (γ/2) tr(QᵀLQ)
Σₘ Qᵢₘ = 1;  Σᵢ Qᵢₘ = n_pair/M;  Q > 0.
```

L restricts fixed public adjacency to S nodes in the pair, removes affinity self-loops and normalizes by 1+maximum degree. It retains same-label and cross-label edges. Labels define the two correction directions; they do not create a homophily-only affinity. The Laplacian term encourages neighboring rows of Q to agree about member responsibility. Balance, member centering and pair scaling add coupling even when γ=0. The source differentiates all eight steps of this finite algorithm; optimizer convergence is not assumed.

Every member retains complete own CE, with additional loss .1 M/[|S|(C−1)] ΣQb. The fixed rates are ηprobe=ηprivate=.01 and ηcore=.001; ε=.001 and τ=γ=1. Q is an independent argument when taking the private partial gₘ=∂φₘℓₘ(θ,φₘ;Q). Its dependence on θ and all original private rows remains live for the subsequent shared derivative:

```text
φ′ₘ = φₘ − ηprivate gₘ
Dθφ′ₘ = −ηprivate [∂θgₘ + (∂Qgₘ)DθQ]
DθJ = ∂θJ + Σₘ (∂φ′ₘJ)Dθφ′ₘ.
```

J on R mixes mean member CE and NLL of the arithmetic probability mean, with ρ=.5. Its total shared credit includes the direct prediction path, probe gradients, finite response, centering/RMS, all eight Q steps and main private adaptation. After shared SGD, the entire response/Q/private update is recomputed at θ⁺ from the **original φ**, then committed. Episodes truncate history. The W/S/R protocol makes R repeated training supervision; it supplies no untouched evaluation claim. Deployment averages all four complete probability vectors without assignment routing.
