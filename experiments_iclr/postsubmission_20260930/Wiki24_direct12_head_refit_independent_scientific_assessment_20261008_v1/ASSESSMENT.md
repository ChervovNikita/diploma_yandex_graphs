## What is already scientifically sound

The BE pair holds frozen head inputs, actual common bias, mean-own CE, class-centered effective-map penalty and initial effective functions fixed. Shared TRAIN-only RMS scaling without centering preserves its diagonal family. Centering the effective class rows avoids penalizing a softmax-irrelevant common row. Unrestricted full matrices contain the factorized family at that interface. The contrast includes optimization coordinates as well as parameter-family freedom; the factorized nonconvex fit is not a global comparator certificate.

Ordinary native private biases are an explicitly stronger control; a bias ablation is unnecessary. Original ordinary own-selected modes and BE joint-selected modes must remain intact. Different feature metrics and upstream objectives limit across-family causal claims, while the refitted controls still test practical competitiveness. The fixed lambda, no development selector, no restarts and complete closure rules avoid choosing a convenient head after scoring. C2/scalar caveats correctly prevent an unrelated general capacity claim.

## Material remaining requirement before activation

“If a frozen full head helps” is too broad a continuation rule. A lower TRAIN objective, generic refitting/calibration, or stronger but still redundant members would not establish a remedy for the observed coverage weakness. Before fitting, bind the following cohorts to the **untouched native unit+contrast bank** for each seed: complete population; native pooled-correct/wrong; native any-correct/all-wrong; and all qualifying strict common rivals. This is root's clarified primary convention. Do not inherit be_init-frozen masks as the endpoint or construct cohorts from fitted outputs.

Use actual native pooled wrong→fitted correct repairs and native correct→fitted wrong harms. Coverage acquisition/loss uses native versus fitted **actual member top1 correctness**. For an old common-rival object, one same member beating every old rival is an order diagnostic; it becomes an actual new correct alternative only if that same member predicts truth against every class under the declared argmax convention. A new rival can defeat it. A served repair additionally requires the original pool to be wrong and the fitted pool to be correct. Rivalry reversal is not a harm definition. Keep native tie/strict-rank distinctions explicit.

For the full-minus-factor comparison, count excess repairs and lost repairs on these same fixed native sets. If R_U,R_F are native-error repairs and H_U,H_F are harms on native-correct objects, then

```
N*(accuracy_U-accuracy_F) = (R_U-R_F) - (H_U-H_F).
```

Similarly report new correct alternatives on native all-wrong objects and losses on native any-correct objects, for both fits. New member correctness, actual pool recovery and removal/replacement of common rivals are different quantities. If all members merely improve the same boundary, coverage can rise without useful complementarity; retain that explanation rather than naming every acquired correct prediction “diversity.”

## Prospective operational decision ladder

This is a suggested activation rule to freeze before fits, not a revision of the original seal or any running gate. It uses directional/count conditions rather than an effect-size threshold chosen after seeing results. All seed values, remaining ordinary gaps and costs still need reporting; passing is an exploratory opportunity screen on one selected split.

1. **Evaluable experiment.** Bind exact original states/heads/features/roles and the common initial affine kernel. Freeze the backend and line-search/failure/function-evaluation conventions. Use the proposed1000-iteration cap and gradient-infinity tolerance1e-6, TRAIN-only stopping, no restarts or alternate penalties. Nonfinite objectives, line-search failure and cap termination remain explicit; no survivor-only mean or substituted best iterate selected by development. Score only after all12 terminal records/failures are sealed. A solver/gauge stopping receipt is not factor-family global optimality. All required paired rows and references must be usable for an affirmative complete-screen decision.

2. **Full-specific predictive opportunity.** Require fullBE to have positive full-population net served gains over factorBE in each of the three seeds, no worse equal-seed mean pooled NLL, and no worse equal-seed mean member and minimum-member accuracy. Full/factor improving similarly over native indicates generic refitting/regularization. Better TRAIN fit alone, a favorable error cohort with offsetting harms, or unresolved comparative failures stops this continuation claim. A failing fixed screen does not prove that all nonlinear or differently regularized readouts are useless; it supplies no automatic retry.

3. **Coverage/correlated-error target actually addressed.** Require an increase over factorBE in actual new top1-correct alternatives on the native all-wrong population in all three seeds, positive equal-seed mean global any-correct gain, and positive equal-seed mean excess actual served repairs on native all-wrong/common-rival objects. Report all-seed signs and any offsetting coverage losses/new common errors. Order reversals without true predictions, or pool improvement without acquired alternatives, do not pass this coverage-remedy branch. They can be reported as a narrower confidence/readout opportunity.

4. **Ordinary gap and action.** Evaluate both untouched authoritative ordinary predictions and ordinary_full_refit, with single_full_refit retained. To call the original practical weakness remedied in this diagnostic, fullBE should meet or exceed each strong ordinary accuracy reference in equal-seed mean without worse mean pooled NLL; compare every seed and retain any remaining coverage gap. Meeting an ordinary readout that degraded does not erase the stronger original bank. A full-specific partial repair while ordinary remains stronger is a useful limited head finding, not automatic admission of an expensive extension. Passing the preceding conditions and closing the practical gap can justify **considering one from-scratch private task-head extension with complete capable controls**. It still does not establish end-to-end quality, efficiency, useful specialization, novelty or generalization.

These conservative conditions intentionally distinguish three conclusions: no useful opportunity under this fixed refit; partial frozen-head opportunity; and a representative opportunity to pursue the coverage remedy. They do not demand microscopic replay equality. Record material initial-function/provenance mismatches and resolve those; preserve original scores rather than replacing them with FP64 replay. Irrelevant tiny FP32-versus-materialized differences are numerical diagnostics, not a new scientific gate.

## Scope of any later extension

A later zero-initialized private task-head residual is a known architecture/optimization change. It can be trained from scratch with the intended shared backbone and objective, without acquiring independent teachers or compressing a teacher bank. The probe does not select its hyperparameters, guarantee its benefit, or show that whole-network private paths cannot already compensate. Full native trajectories, objective-matched ordinary references, all costs/failures and an unused split/task confirmation remain necessary before a broad claim. No additional probe variants or unused outcomes are required for this review.
