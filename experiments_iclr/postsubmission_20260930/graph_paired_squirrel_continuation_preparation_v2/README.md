## Block sequence and scientific boundary

1. Verify immutable source/role/TRAIN/donor identities and full preprocessing. Restore the exact native donor; keep a preservation snapshot.
2. Run the pinned **disposable**, dropout-off warm Adam equivalence audit. All new R/S are frozen/excluded from its update. This tests transported shared/private-bias coordinates and warm optimizer history for one step; it does not establish equivalence of complete trajectories with learned private transforms.
3. Verify native/K1/K4 identity, exact full-node FP32 output, seed+90000 active AD and true seed+80000 `Pi S Pi^T`, saving the permutation vector.
4. Invoke the sealed four-arm helper once. Preserve joint failure with four blocked terminals and no per-arm fallback. Observed resource failures take precedence over helper acceptance/failure, preserving its complete report. Geometry exceptions retain charged reports and cause-chain classification.
5. Only on joint acceptance, qualify **all four** fresh installed K4 states against common warm outputs and four closure references each. Transport named Adam before installing useful factors; save their exact initialized state, named optimizer and restored warm RNG. Any installation failure blocks all continuation in the block.
6. After all four installed states pass, admit only that block's compact source-validation pack. Restore each initialized state on a fresh K4 and continue serially. New R/S are explicitly required trainable in actual continuation. Train mean member CE with the pinned1950-update cap / patience250; select initialized epoch0 or the earliest strict validation-NLL minimum using softmax of mean raw logits. All arms start from the same post-warm RNG and optimizer convention. Save the fixed midpoint when reached and selected member logits/state without final labels.
7. Preserve all fit errors and all three block denominators. Compare only after the three block records and twelve arm terminals are available. If any fit is missing/failed/deferred, emit an unevaluable trigger, with no successful-subset score. Otherwise full_node's three-block mean validation NLL must be strictly lower than every control to trigger the separately frozen Photo followup. The driver never launches Photo.

The trigger is a development decision, not superiority. Squirrel is heterophilic; a negative trigger defers Photo and leaves the homophilic full-support alternative open. Final labels remain closed on either Stage1 outcome. Root's original running cohort retains its all72 closure rule. Broad claims require the independent heldout and stronger-family/wider-graph work specified in the design.
