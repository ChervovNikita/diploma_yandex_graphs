## Why nine days have not produced that result

The completed history repeatedly separates three quantities:

- **Member competence:** a route must learn useful predictions. Private CMCL, missing-hop credit and normalized-private Adam supplied measured competence or utility harm despite access to truthful labels or protected immediate shared credit.
- **Useful complementarity:** different routes must correct different relevant errors. Coherent starts improve mean member accuracy over the original shared banks, but their correctness nearly coincides. Graph pair adds only 47/42 covered readouts across GAT/SAGE seed readouts; its total coverage remains below ordinary I4.
- **Serving:** the deployed prediction must retain the alternatives acquired. Those same pair increments add 47/40 pooling losses, yielding zero/two net correct readouts. In the old shared banks, a correct member exists on a majority of every I4-correct/shared-wrong cohort; all18 dominant common-rival predicates fail. An oracle can identify that supplier after seeing truth; the record contains no learned selector proving it can do so prospectively.

For these classification counts, pool-correct = coverage − lost alternatives + aggregation-only correct. Lower disagreement, fewer pooling losses, better mean members or greater coverage can each accompany worse final quality. The saved ranking studies likewise show that lower error correlation is insufficient. These observations do not diagnose hidden collapse, shared-gradient conflict or calibration as the cause.

Many initial positives shrink under capable controls: factorized singles explain much of the PubMed gain, genuine I4 strengthens the comparator, and same-information single/untied controls weaken context and transfer claims. Source parity, finite derivatives and successful integration establish executable studies; they supply no accuracy gain. The elapsed nine days have narrowed these explanations. They have not accumulated independent confirmation of a successful universal rule.
