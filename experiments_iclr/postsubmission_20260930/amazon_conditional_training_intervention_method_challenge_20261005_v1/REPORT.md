## 2. Smallest discriminating next step, if separately authorized

**First specify one operator and one fixed acquisition unit on paper.** Prefer allocation as the closer attributed question; do not test both recipes or a coefficient/operator grid. A new dependency is unnecessary for testing utility of an honestly attributed adaptation.

The smallest informative future qualification is one predeclared finite-update response on held TRAIN queries A, using support B. Freeze graph operator, coefficients, horizon, initial-state rule, update ownership, pool, controls and pass margins before acquisition. A labels must be excluded from every probe fit, difficulty estimate, assignment/scorer fit and selection decision. If h on B is cross-fitted, its probes must remain inside B: ordinary two-way cross-fitting that trains h_B on A reintroduces held labels. Public transductive graph/features may remain, with no independence claim. Current checkpoints chosen using VALID cannot certify this fresh TRAIN-only selection boundary; use a prospectively fixed TRAIN-only horizon/state rule.

Measure before/after changes over **all A queries** under the deployed original graph and fixed probability pool, plus each member's full-query quality. Use the intended finite shared/private update, rather than silently replacing it with a frozen-core or oracle experiment. Graph-specific gain must survive the candidate's closest controls in the table; count distinct informative items, graph links, all unsupported/unmatched cells and all acquisition failures. No winner-selected subgroup, best member, lower correlation, better assignment stability or smaller mask overlap is a pass.

A positive result would justify only **one small representative prospective comparison of the attributed operation**, with a frozen full pooled accuracy/NLL/Brier rule, explicit member-strength margins, capable nonlinear single, ordinary independent4 and same-operation untied4, complete acquisition/exposure costs and identical selection opportunities. It would not admit superiority or novelty. The small gate need not certify an eventual sharing benefit: that requires these competent references in the prospective comparison. No numeric margins are invented from the exposed outcomes here; until the operator, rule and response are supplied, admission remains null.

## 3. Comparison with the running pilot and the unresolved dependency

The pilot already differentiates shared-core training through private learning on endpoint-separated TRAIN support. Conditional on the shared core and episodes, each virtual update is blockwise: U_m(theta, phi_m; B_m). The outer own/pool objective couples credit, while the inner maps have no other-route private state input. Virtual states are discarded; private steps are recomputed and committed after the shared update; serving uses committed states and mean raw logits. ANIL (`1909.09157v2`), BMAML (`1806.03836v4`), MLDG (`1710.03463v1`) and the saved OML/La-MAML/FTML conclusions already precede this broad meta-learning structure. Endpoint separation on one graph is not independent-task evidence. Amazon probability/Brier observations cannot be transferred to its link/ranking endpoint.

The two new recipes do not supply a distinct shared/private learning dependency. Allocation uses detached responsibilities and the same scalar update for shared/private parameters. Q has cross-member forward dependence, so it is inaccurate to call it independent, but it supplies no differentiated credit through the assignment's learning response. View diversity already couples scorers through overlap, as in DIVE; merely differentiating that penalty through a later update adds no demonstrated corrective mechanism.

A concretely different dependency **relative to the pilot** would require a source-defined graph-conditioned, nonseparable private-learning map whose update for route m uses another route's measured corrective learning state, with retained derivative paths that change shared-core credit and a counterfactual removal preserving the same evidence/exposure. Even nonzero off-diagonal update derivatives are insufficient novelty: BMAML already has interacting particles, and overlap, own/pool losses and ensemble distillation have prior coupling (DIVE, GNCL, ONE). Graph-conditioning these operations may be another adaptation. No such operator, equivalence analysis, or TRAIN-only corrective-response evidence is currently supplied. This note does not propose an implementation or clear a new method.
