# Counter and failure semantics

The observed common closure increments `calls_started` before each forward, then `calls_completed` or `calls_failed`. It records each failed call and its error. Successful active AD must contribute exactly sixteen closure forwards. Successful paired return must agree with the sealed helper's `vjp_forwards+jvp_calls+line_search_forward_calls`; rejected candidate work is included. Four installed-slice references add four actual observed closure calls per accepted arm.

VJP/JVP primitive counts are retained from the pinned AD/paired receipts. The qualifier's maximum is one VJP primal, four pullbacks, three JVP primals and twelve finite-difference forwards. The paired maximum is one VJP primal, thirteen pullbacks, twelve JVP primals, six scheduled sparse products and 96 candidate forwards. A native AD abort can occur before a complete receipt; the output retains the failing operation, actual observed forwards and the expected maximum, without claiming unknown reverse calls completed. A `PairedGeometryError.report` carries the helper's attempted primitive counters.

The sealed helper charges three scheduled sparse products before each band-bank call. On a bank abort, completed sparse products are unknown and must not be reported as six completed products. Successful geometry completes both three-product banks. This preparation does not change the sealed helper.

Outside the observed closure, the initial pinned identity audit comprises three model-container invocations and six scheduled member predictors (native1 + K1 1 + K4 4). Each accepted arm then has one fresh-warm K4 container and one installed K4 container (four scheduled members each). These are charged before the operation. On a container abort the scheduled member count can exceed completed internal member passes; the output explicitly labels that limit. Direct observed reference forwards are separate and must not be double-counted as K4 container calls.

For a maximal six-trial joint acceptance, the assay has up to **142 observed closure forwards** (one contract forward, sixteen AD, 109 paired, sixteen installation references), plus 38 scheduled outside-closure member predictors, for up to 180 member-predictor forwards under the successful-container convention. Joint failure omits installations. Graph preprocessing, restoration, cloning, hashing and all failed operations are additional measured work. The exact callback counts and measured time/memory receipts take precedence over this planning account.

The pinned ledger records each native operation's elapsed time and CUDA peak allocated/reserved memory, including failed operations. Nested loader operations reset their local peak counters; report the per-operation peaks rather than calling an outer receipt the entire process peak. Stdlib fingerprint/probe/runtime stages separately record elapsed time and process RSS without claiming GPU peaks. Whole-process wall/RSS and all errors are retained in the final receipt. Resource planning has no watchdog or scheduler; root's future execution context owns any external time bound.

Input fingerprint mismatch or unsupported AD remains a source qualification failure. A failed TRAIN separation/CE joint grid remains an explicit `joint_failure` and receives no installed substitute. Insufficient current resources or actual OOM remains `resource_deferred`, retaining work/errors. All statuses leave scientific merit unassessed and preserve the original cohort. No task uses validation/final labels or scores.

V2 examines the complete explicit/implicit exception chain through `__cause__` and `__context__`, including suppressed contexts. Visited exception identities prevent repeated traversal and cycles. Builtin `MemoryError` subclasses, `OutOfMemoryError` classes/subclasses (including Torch's allocator type), and the legacy CUDA out-of-memory message are resource failures even when wrapped by `PairedGeometryError`. A receipt records the matched exception and chain link, preserves the outer traceback and charged paired report, and sets its outer status to `resource_deferred`. A nested helper report can still contain its original `joint_failure` status; it is retained diagnostic evidence and does not override the outer resource status. Nonresource exceptions remain qualification failures. Deterministic stdlib mocks check both classifications and fingerprint preservation without native execution.
