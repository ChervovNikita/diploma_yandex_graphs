Member competence and served quality need separate accounting. Matched Roman SAGE improves through stronger members, whereas internal SAGE can improve its pool with weaker members. Relative to its freshly acquired BASE, E4 improves mean member accuracy by 1.0729 points. Its coverage falls by 106 readouts while pooling losses fall by 196, leaving 90 more correct pooled decisions. Its mean members remain 0.7632 points weaker than equipped I4. These counts repeat the same 5,274 development nodes across three optimizer seeds and compare separately fitted and selected procedures; they describe prediction changes, not their learning cause.

A common-rival error imposes a further serving constraint. Under exact softmax probabilities, a false class strictly above truth in every member's raw scores remains above it under any nonnegative scalar probability average with weights summing to one. Thus pooling unchanged probabilities cannot correct that ranking. E4 satisfies the raw-score predicate on 2,064 readouts among its 2,975 pooled errors, even though its overall accuracy exceeds BASE's. The remaining errors include correct member alternatives lost in pooling. The constraint does not show that hidden information is absent, and removing a rival predicate is not necessarily a served repair.

E4 changes the member scores upstream of pooling: paired maps generated from live source and receiver states act on each nonself neighbor value before the native SAGE mean, with private factors in the shared builders. Conditional filtering and endpoint maps have established ancestry in ECC, GNN-FiLM and learned sheaf models. Receiver-conditioned N4 uses the same parameters and matrix action and accounts for about 74% of E4's BASE accuracy gain. The narrower question is whether the selected E procedure depends on which source context conditions each carried message. At an intervention site, holding graph support, actual carried values, receiver states and each receiver's source-context multiset fixed while changing that association can probe this dependence; it does not establish private-builder necessity, a training mechanism or unused whole-pipeline confirmation.
