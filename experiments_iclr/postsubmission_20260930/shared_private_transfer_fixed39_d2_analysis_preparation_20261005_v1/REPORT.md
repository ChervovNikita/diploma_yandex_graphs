## Fixed calculations

- All member and mean-raw-logit pooled MRR/Hits10 use native tie-aware midranks, `rank=1+0.5*(count(neg>=pos)+count(neg>pos))`, over all227 fixed VALID queries and their500 negatives. Native float32 reductions reproduce selector checks.
- Pooled-minus-mean-member reciprocal-rank gain averages all query gains. Higher/equal/lower fractions use exact rational RR from integer twice-midranks, avoiding an arbitrary equality tolerance; signed differences are averaged in float64. Small float32 reduction differences from subtracting the displayed aggregate MRRs are numerical, not a new selector.
- All six pairs for each four-member arm report float64 Pearson correlation of `1-RR` and joint Hit10-error overlap, `queries where both miss Hit10 /227`. Marginal/union error counts and intersection-over-union make overlap denominators explicit. Constant-error correlations and empty-union ratios are undefined, with null values and reasons.
- Aggregate BCE, per-member BCE, mean member BCE and Jensen gap use the source negative-logsigmoid operators in float64. Positive losses average over227 positives; negative losses average separately over227×500 negatives; the two means are added. Member losses are averaged equally across real members. Positive/negative components and gaps are retained, along with `0.5*aggregate+0.5*mean_member = aggregate+0.5*gap`. Tiny negative floating-point gaps are retained and never used as a gate.
- F1 and capable single have one real member, not four members from their streams. Their pair list is empty/inapplicable, and pooling is the single-member identity. SharedF4, untied4 and ordinary native4 retain their four actual members.
- Every39 cell/block value is retained. Each fixed cell reports an arithmetic mean over b0,b1,b2 for every member, pooling, BCE and pair statistic. If a block correlation/ratio is undefined, its three-block mean is undefined; no block is dropped. There are no subgroup/member/checkpoint choices, tuned stabilizers, CI/p-value gates or new quality thresholds.
