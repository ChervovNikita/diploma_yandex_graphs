## Mechanism and symmetry boundary

Projected neighborhood distributions can provide information not available in a fixed mean-like summary. Labels can teach each private classifier whether that information improves its predictions. A shared encoder may already retain the relevant information, or smoothing may have removed it. Neither distribution richness nor a different direction guarantees useful corrections.

The corrector **does not break exact copied symmetry**. Identical directions, heads and realized stochastic paths yield identical signatures, functions and gradients. Different predeclared nonzero directions can expose different signatures, but ordinary distinct initialization is established practice and supplies no new initializer contribution or guarantee. Directions can reconverge; heads can ignore signatures; distinct signatures can support the same mistakes. No automatic specialization is claimed.

PNA establishes multi-statistic neighborhood aggregation and degree scaling. Fourier Sliced-Wasserstein embeddings establish projected quantile/multiset information, including graph aggregation. BE/TabM supply shared matrices, private factors and member supervision. Three quartiles inherit none of FSW's injectivity or Wasserstein guarantees. The surviving question is an empirical sharing/learning bias, not a new primitive or a whole-network expressivity separation.

## Representative controls and rejection conditions

Before any admission, qualify and freeze one complete representative task, native schedule, paired seed blocks, initializer, pooling, selection opportunities and complete-population decision. The comparison must include:

1. A competent source-native single and ordinary independent ensemble.
2. A shared bank without signatures, with the same degree input.
3. Private signatures versus one common signature.
4. A capable nonlinear single receiving **all four directions' twelve quartiles**, native state and degree.
5. An untied ensemble using the identical signature operation.
6. A competent PNA/statistic-rich comparator.

Richer neighborhood statistics, an extra effective local hop, extra capacity or different optimization can explain gains. The common/all-signature single and PNA controls test that confound; the same-operation untied control tests whether sharing earns utility. A restricted one-signature single is insufficient.

A worthwhile result needs replicated full-population served accuracy/risk improvement with protected mean and worst member quality. Preserve NLL/Brier, coverage, pooled repairs **and** harms, common wrong rivals and actual computation/storage costs. Increased signature dispersion is insufficient. If the common signature matches, the private-view account fails. If the all-signature single/PNA matches, ensemble-specific necessity is unsupported. If the same-operation untied ensemble matches or wins, shared superiority is unsupported. Favorable seeds, degree cohorts or subgroups cannot rescue a failed complete comparison. Any positive exploratory result still needs unused confirmation and a closer complete-recipe prior assessment.
