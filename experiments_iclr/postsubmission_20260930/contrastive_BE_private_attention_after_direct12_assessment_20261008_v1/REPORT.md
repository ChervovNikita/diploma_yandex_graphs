# Private attention after the completed head diagnostic

8 October 2026. Source and saved-literature assessment only. The parent admitted the complete direct12 aggregate report. No pending Context9/Wiki12/Mol18 outcomes, scientific arrays or model executions were opened.

## Decision

**Private local attention scoring is a more pointed next hypothesis than another final-head intervention, if a useful hidden-evidence question remains after the current studies close.** It can alter what is aggregated before the final representation, whereas a head refit can only reinterpret the supplied representation. This is a reason to prioritize a conditional experiment, not evidence that attention caused the errors, a strict capacity theorem or a new attention method.

Direct12 supplies no affirmative head result. Full and factorized refits tie in mean served accuracy, about 80.5714%; their paired changes are 0, −1 and +1 development nodes. Both trail the untouched unit+contrast model, about 81.63%. Six fits did not satisfy the declared raw-gradient tolerance, and the affirmative screen remains blocked. All four refit conditions reduced their native accuracy. Preserve that complete diagnostic and stop that intervention; do not retune its penalty, horizon or solver to obtain a better story. It neither proves that the hidden representation lacks evidence nor proves that final-head freedom cannot ever help.

## The actual intervention

The pinned native WikiCS backbone has seven local GATConv layers, one head and 512 hidden channels. The adapter factorizes each dense projection but leaves attention vectors, LayerNorm affine parameters and mixing coefficients shared. Each member still computes its own hidden states and attention. Its global key/query and value dense maps are already factorized; this proposal does not newly supply member-dependent global attention.

At an eligible local layer, let u_m be the projected member state. The native additive score uses a shared sender vector and receiver vector on u_m. Changing a BE projection factor can change both that score and the transmitted values. Giving each member its own initially copied sender/receiver vectors allows a direct score change while holding the current projected values fixed. During training the values and shared matrices continue to learn; they are not frozen or equal across members. “Shared values” here means shared dense parameter objects plus the existing private factors, not one common value tensor or one common graph operator.

Replace only the local scorer vectors with four trainable copies of the original native vectors. Keep existing factors, dense maps, normalization, beta, residual/global paths, task heads, target archives, losses and probability-mean serving unchanged. Copy after native reset without drawing another initialization. All routes still run; there is no teacher, router, learned final selection or inference shortcut.

This changes scorer parameter ownership, gradient aggregation and Adam histories together. It is a parameterization and learning-policy intervention. Under the standard no-edge GAT scorer with two 512-coordinate vectors per local layer, untying seven layers would add (4−1)×7×2×512=21,504 stored prediction scalars, plus gradients/moments. This is a conditional count from the declared scorer contract; the actual installed PyG fields/shapes and shared-object aliasing still require qualification before source admission. No runtime saving follows.

## What it can and cannot explain

Private scoring can make a member rank a different neighbor above another without first coordinating a change to all value-consuming branches. The earlier scale-absorption assessment shows why that direct permission can matter for optimization: some score changes can be emulated by existing BE scales, but the actual parallel branches, outside biases, LayerNorm mixture and accumulated states defeat the simple general inverse-scale construction. Its failure does not prove whole-network noncontainment.

The local GATv1 limitation remains. Within each head, its neighbor ordering is static with respect to the receiving query; untying vectors does not turn it into GATv2. A changed vector can mainly change temperature, emphasize nuisances or weaken competent members. Shared value learning may still omit important evidence. Attention disagreement or lower auxiliary loss is therefore not the desired endpoint. New correct member alternatives, pooled-only rescues, complete-population repairs minus harms, member competence and actual pooled risk remain distinct required observations.
