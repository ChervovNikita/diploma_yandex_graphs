On the Initialization of Graph Neural Networks
A. Proof and Empirical Verification
In this section, we show empirical verification of assumptions, and proofs of one lemma and multiple theorems in the main
body of this paper. All experiments are conducted on a 3-layer GCN initialized by Xavier initialization. We argue that
the initialization method does not affect empirical results in this section since they are not related to distributions of GCN
weight matrices.
