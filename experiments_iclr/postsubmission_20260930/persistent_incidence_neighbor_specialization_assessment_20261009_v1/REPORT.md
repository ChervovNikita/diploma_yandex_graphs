## 2. Global-latent collisions remove a representation-only story

### Rank-1 BNN and structured BSNN

The saved [Rank-1 BNN, 2005.07186v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.07186v2) §3.1 scope establishes distributions/priors on fast factors, a point-estimated W, expected likelihood, factor KL and W prior. The saved initialization conclusions also apply. No new primary reread was needed.

For an incidence site, a graph adaptation can draw global r/s once per complete forward and use `W_eff=diag(s) W diag(r)` at every ordered endpoint input. Even a linear scalar special case `a_e^T r` has

`Cov(a_e^T r, a_f^T r | X) = a_e^T Cov(r) a_f`.

Thus independence of factor coordinates does not imply independent edge/map values. A state-conditioned generator can additionally transmit those draws through subsequent member states. This is a declared graph application of an existing stochastic factor representation. Per-edge redraws would be a different family. Native factor priors/KL, W prior, sampling and loss scaling must be preserved or named as adaptations; deterministic factors are not posterior samples. Gauge normalization of sampled factors cannot retain the old independent posterior/KL automatically.

Original inspected BSNN remains a direct shared-network/private-sheaf predictive ensemble. Its incidence-product/input-conditioned/per-layer sampling restricts **joint stochastic uncertainty**, not coherence of conditional means. A global-latent extension or global Bayesian incidence factors can represent correlated operators. A categorical latent selecting a stored member also gives the same finite predictive mixture when enumerated. That is a representation identity, not an unchanged continuous sheaf prior/ELBO or an additional scientific training arm. Do not turn deterministic atoms into finite-KL approximations of a continuous original prior without a valid probability model.

## 5. At most one optional extension: initialization measured by live neighbor action

The option is **initialization only**, followed by ordinary full-task mean-own CE. It does not add the theory packet's continuing action-energy repulsion or combine a new initializer and the V2 auxiliary rule in a first attribution test.

Start from one competent shared native checkpoint with identity incidence factors, keeping all θ parameters common. At the first incidence learner only, propose eight fixed, small unit perturbation directions in its input factors, with plus/minus pairs and unit output factors. Later-layer factors start at identity. Every direction is generated from a sealed seed/rule before scores; no topology reconstruction, feature/label selection or new head is added.

For each trial, a full eval forward provides the first layer's common actual value V*, its live normalized off-node action difference from baseline, full TRAIN NLL and final class-margin difference. Screen finite NLL/visibility conditions at a fixed small step. Choose two admissible plus/minus direction pairs with the largest linear independence of normalized live action differences; make four members from those pairs. Pairing balances parameter perturbations, not predictions or losses. If fewer than two pairs qualify, retain failure rather than increase dose, directions or a query search. No continuous gauge projection or function-preservation theorem is asserted.

The initializer is automatically invariant to a raw edge-row sign relabeling of the same operator action. It can still choose harmless-looking TRAIN perturbations that later fail, self-term effects mistaken for neighbor utility, amplitude-only changes or spurious source responses. Initialization screening therefore supplies no generalization/competence guarantee. The original complete model must continue training and be restored/tested under the matching controls in `PILOT_DISABLED.json`.

The key comparison is with a random choice from the **same** admissible candidates and a choice based on finite classifier responses from those same trials. This separates the particular live-action statistic from generic initialization asymmetry and output-function screening. All arms pay the same trial forward budget. Global Bayesian-factor/structured-latent, genuine independent and matched pointwise/frame/joint capacity controls remain required for broader attribution. Identical categorical enumeration or exact mean-readout relabelings are reduced by algebra and receive no redundant training arm.

This is potentially useful because it initializes measured graph-side differences near a qualified predictor and rejects exact cosmetic/ignored actions. Whether it preserves strong individual paths or improves complementary errors is entirely open. No current source or primary passage justifies novelty, optimality or automatic numerical admission.
