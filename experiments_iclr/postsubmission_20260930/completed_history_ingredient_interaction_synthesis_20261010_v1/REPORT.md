## Strongest conclusion

**Competence, correct-alternative acquisition and serving those alternatives are separate requirements. No completed history presently qualifies a new shared A+B combination for execution.** A negative package does not prove either ingredient universally useless; a positive mean interaction or many repairs does not prove complementary repair sets. Exact matched error identities and introduced harms are needed for the latter claim.

**The newly closed M1-six controls materially change the interpretation.** M1 factor-native reaches90.8168%, and M1 factor-four-dropout reaches90.7830%. This is a strong competing optimization/reparameterization explanation, not causal proof: M1 adds13,943 coordinates, M4 adds55,772, and coordinate Adam/decay differ. ## Exact unresolved implications: two, neither a new run recommendation

1. **Factorized independent acquisition as a stronger PubMed reference.** Complete12 used ordinary native I4 without factors; M1 provides a competent factorized single. An own-selected committee of independently acquired native factorized bodies is not present in these matched completed reports. Its competence/coverage tradeoff and actual cost remain unknown. This is a reference qualification question, not a new shared mechanism.
2. **Exact first-input Rademacher×privateCMCL on PubMed.** That exact task/credit interaction is unexecuted in admitted completed history. Generic sign×auxiliary is already tried on WikiCS and supplies contrary tradeoff evidence. Without completed matched repair identities showing distinct useful changes and tolerable competence harm, the exact interaction stays a conditional design with a no-run disposition.

Current CMCL18 and Wiki native-scorer6 are not read for partial quality. Their pending state cannot support either implication.
