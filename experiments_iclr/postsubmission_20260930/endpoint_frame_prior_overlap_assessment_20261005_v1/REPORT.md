## Why a stronger bilinear alternative matters

For a linear channel after the candidate's product,

\[
q_{m,j}=b_{m,j}+h_u^T H_m^T\operatorname{diag}(c_{m,j})H_mh_v.
\]

Thus each channel is a constrained symmetric bilinear form. Within one member, the channel matrices share the reflected coordinate basis. A learned symmetric tensor bank can represent these channels directly; a low-rank factorization can trade capacity against cost. This is an analytical interface comparison, **not whole-network containment**: native context, normalization, recursion, supervision and serving still matter.

A future capable single with symmetric bilinear channels entering the native endpoint/context decoder would test whether the useful effect is compact ensemble learning or merely a better pair head. Full tensor capacity is costly, so complete budgets and a prospectively fixed factorization matter. The existing same-four-frame single remains indispensable for the current screen; this assessment authorizes no additional fits or tuning.

Independent four-member reflected NCN already contains the shared candidate by tying encoders and effective maps. Any quality win against it concerns finite-sample estimation or optimization, rather than an unavailable interaction function. A win against the existing single alone cannot establish that sharing caused the improvement.

## Is another method change justified?

**No change is justified before resolving the frozen comparison.** Additional reflections or unrestricted projections would mainly relax a familiar bilinear parameterization, without adding a graph-specific mechanism.

A credible later graph-specific hypothesis would let a compact **symmetric** interaction depend on native common-neighbor/residual evidence, so endpoint compatibility can vary between closure-supported and sparse edge regimes. Link-MoE and LPFormer already motivate structural conditioning; that would be an attributed extension requiring its own controls and serving contract, not a novelty rescue or a modification of this screen. Evidence that static private frames fail specifically across those regimes would be a reason to investigate it.

The bounded contribution still available is empirical utility of the precise constrained sharing recipe against competent same-operation alternatives at measured complete cost. This search provides neither an absence certificate nor a manuscript/acceptance verdict.
