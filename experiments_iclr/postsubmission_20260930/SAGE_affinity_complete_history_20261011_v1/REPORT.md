## Two interpretations requiring correction

1. **More objectives do not automatically make a stronger I4 control.** Same-target assigned I4 beats mixture I4 by +0.088484 raw pp at all three seeds, despite slightly weaker mean members. It has 11 fewer covered readouts and 25 fewer pooling losses. Both controls must remain visible; the assigned bank directly preserves Q_m assignment while removing sharing. Calibration reduces the gap and leaves nearly equal NLL, so no universal assignment superiority follows.
2. **A shared-versus-I4 gain does not identify the added private learning mechanism.** Private wins against equipped mixture/assigned I4 by +0.587789/+0.499305 pp, but unchanged shared BASE is marginally better than private. Part of the enlarged gap versus mixture I4 comes from that reference losing 0.107445 pp to original factorized I4. The shared accuracy advantage over equipped singles also already exists in BASE. Unlike PubMed, SAGE’s capable factorized M1 does not nearly close the shared pool gap; the capable-single explanation is task-specific.

The exact 64-pattern evidence supplies genuine raw exclusive repairs, including 28 class-7 repairs at seed 7301. That class gains 11.794872 pp at that seed but loses at the other two. Counts provide neither cross-seed node persistence nor a causal reason another loss would preserve those repairs. Calibrated exclusive repairs/consensus harms are 65/64, and private proper-loss protection passes against BASE/common/uniform. These positives are preserved, without replacing the failed raw attribution or the NLL gap to both I4 banks.
