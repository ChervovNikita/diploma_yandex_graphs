This is a bounded finding from saved method documents, not a global proof of

## The whole proposed rule

Use the unchanged shared model, TRAIN data/panel, fixed Qm and their normalized
arithmetic mean Qbar, unit initialization, two realized stochastic views,
alignment coefficient .05, temperature .2, native Adam and joint selector,
1100-epoch horizon, and native mean-probability serving. Let F be the exact
mean-member own CE, Ac common-target alignment and Ar route-target alignment.

At one current parameter/buffer state, collect:

| Parameter block | Supplied derivative |
|---|---|
| Shared parameters reachable from alignment | partial_theta(F + .05 Ac) |
| Designated private internal BE factor rows | partial_phi(F + .05 Ar) |
| Active task-head parameters/factors | Their ordinary F derivative; alignment has no direct path to the captured pre-head representation |

Collect all blocks before **one** original optimizer transition, with the same
views and buffers. Retain the original 1/M scaling for private gradients. No
teacher, representation detachment, new optimizer, sequential theta-first
transition, extra prediction head, learned coefficient or serving change is
part of this rule. A future implementation must exhaustively classify any
remaining private input/boundary tensors rather than infer their permission
from a name; the audited MolI boundary partition is not automatically the
context recipe's partition.

In general this is a nonpotential block field. Writing B = Ar − Ac, it is the
common scalar gradient plus .05 times the private projection of grad B. Its
mixed shared/private derivative need not be symmetric. This is standard
selective-gradient/game ancestry, not a new optimizer principle.

## What the new calculus establishes

The new explanatory note establishes identical total shared gradients for
ROUTE and COMMON when realized score paths **and shared Jacobians** coincide.
Private gradients can differ, but the target difference may be annihilated by
symmetrization or the private Jacobian. With fixed targets and conditionally
iid full two-view paths at the symmetric unit state, the ROUTE shared gradient
has COMMON's mean and covariance plus the positive-semidefinite covariance of
the zero-mean target-difference term. That added covariance may be zero.

The proposed block rule uses COMMON's shared derivative by construction at the
same state. Under the analyzed conditional-iid symmetry it avoids that specific
additional covariance while retaining ROUTE's private cotangents. This is the
precise motivation. Arbitrary correlated paths, unequal Jacobian laws after
learning, target-dependent randomness and coupled clipping do not inherit the
covariance ordering. Native independent pseudorandom streams are not proof of
the mathematical iid assumption. Neither equal mean gradients nor covariance
ordering proves equal/better Adam transitions, convergence, member competence
or heldout predictions.

The earlier split-context report's arbitrary-correlation covariance warning
still applies outside the stronger assumptions. The new conditional-iid result
refines that warning; it does not contradict its unrestricted statement.

## Source feasibility and a conditional representative test

Saved interfaces establish a design pattern for collecting different block
partials at the same old state. They do not qualify this Ac/Ar successor.
Preparation, if elected, must preserve the captured representation, task-head
zero alignment path, complete parameter partition, member RNG and native
buffer/optimizer transitions. Reusing views rather than performing a second
stochastic forward matters. Actual reverse/forward work must be charged; no
cost or speed advantage follows from this static feasibility assessment.

A separately frozen future comparison should contain scalar COMMON, scalar
ROUTE and the block rule, each at all three paired seeds and the complete
1100 epochs, with one .05 coefficient and no grid. It must not be appended to
an active family or borrow a favorable opened seed. The readouts remain served
accuracy, pooled loss, member mean/worst accuracy, paired repairs and introduced
errors, any-correct coverage and strict common wrong-rival support on the full
selected population. Reducing an auxiliary loss or changing gradient variance
alone cannot count as success. Existing capable single/ordinary and
context-matched references, efficient/diversity comparisons and fresh heldout
confirmation requirements remain necessary before stronger claims.

An updatewise frozen, outcome-independent permutation of Qm across routes
would preserve the exact target bank and Qbar and test persistence more cleanly
than changing mask contents. It differs from context cycling and from the
existing content-permuted masks. It still changes learning trajectories and
does not make loss equality hold at arbitrary differentiated route states.
Retain as an optional future falsifier, without adding an arm automatically.
The mirror recipient rule is likewise relevant to a stronger claim that the
specific allocation matters; it is not admitted here.
