## What the source establishes

`native_source/adapter.py:45–59,111–121` inventories the stem, readout, classifier, both maps in every per-order token MLP, Q/K, and both block FFN maps, and compares their exact names and dimensions with all exact `nn.Linear` instances. For this pinned architecture the site count is `3 + L*(2*(K+1)+4)`. Query/key widths are H because head divisibility is enforced. LayerNorm and the learned head/order `bias_scale` are common native parameters, not omitted affine maps in the proposed inventory. The parameter algebra at lines 62–72 includes their parameters and native biases; its symbolic accounting is consistent with the inspected class definitions.

The member algebra at lines 96–109 is `shared_affine(x*R_m)*S_m`. With column-vector notation its effective weight is `diag(S_m) W diag(R_m)` and effective bias is `diag(S_m)b`; there is no new member bias. This matches the local factor algebra with additive B fixed to zero and differs explicitly from Edward2’s separately added ensemble bias. All-one factors preserve the native affine function. Attribution and the stated distinction are accurate on the inspected source; this is not an exact Edward2 port.

All native orders, LayerNorm, Q/K attention, residuals, FFNs, and boundary maps remain in each trajectory. Only final `[M,N,C]` logits are stacked. No intermediate pooling or tensor detach was found. The sequential implementation makes no packed-backend or throughput claim.
