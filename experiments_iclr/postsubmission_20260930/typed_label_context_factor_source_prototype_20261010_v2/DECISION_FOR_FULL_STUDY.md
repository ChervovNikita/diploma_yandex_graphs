# Research decision after source qualification

This is a bounded graph-specific extension of existing ingredients. Its question
is whether observed TRAIN-label coverage can condition shared projector routes
enough to improve individual competence and a fixed committee prediction. The
motivation is the root-authorized error readout, not a claim that shared hidden
states cause errors or that local context will resolve them. No unrelated running
study outcomes were read while preparing this source.

## Closest collisions already retained

| CHoE, HGEN, MoE-NP / MoSE | Typed views, ensembles and local-regime experts | No expert gate or new voting rule; conditioning occurs before native semantic fusion |

These differences delimit a candidate; they do not certify novelty or prior
absence. Saved read scopes are reused. There are no new primary/full-paper reads
in V2 and no global prior-art clearance.

## Decisive evidence

1. First pass all six full-input numerical qualifications without data leakage,
   missed TRAIN targets, source shrinkage, incorrect ownership or replay drift.
   H diversity at U0 is not evidence of useful functional diversity.
2. Evaluate every declared role under the frozen selector and new raw-logit pool.
   Retain every run, failed qualification and costly condition in the research
   record. The comparison is development exploration; repeated VALID selection
   is not an independent test of generalization.
3. Local multiplication must earn its role against the same-information additive
   model. An improvement over point factors alone can be explained by the three
   new mass inputs. An improvement over the global field only supports incremental
   locality: the global model still has native local positive channels.
4. The four-route model must justify the committee against single128. That control
   matches generator parameter count only; use measured full parameter/storage/
   runtime costs and disclose the remaining budget differences.
5. Compare with both independently selected four-body references in the separate
   reference contract. The matched-initialization, bank-selected untied control
   isolates a training arrangement and cannot substitute for an ordinary ensemble.
6. Inspect member competence, pooled competence and errors with available correct
   member alternatives. Better disagreement alone is insufficient. Evidence must
   persist across roles and not depend on one favorable selector epoch or label.
7. A full study needs fresh representative graphs or independently held-out roles,
   strong native comparators, individually selected references and uncertainty
   appropriate to shared splits/paired seeds. Do not infer broad superiority,
   calibration, a causal mechanism or conference acceptance from three selected
   development roles on one task.

## Action branches

- If local multiplication is competitive with the strong references and improves
  on additive/global/single controls with a practical storage-versus-serving cost
  case, propose a broader study. Root must set prospective scientific criteria and
  decide whether available GPUs can support that representative study.
- If the gain is explained by added mass information or additive conditioning,
  develop that simpler mechanism and attribute it accurately; do not rename it as
  an ensemble breakthrough.
- If only a favorable role improves, or member competence stays poor, retain the
  failed hypothesis internally and move to another idea. Further hyperparameter
  search on these same VALID roles is not the intended response.

Nothing in this packet admits a result into the paper. Admission requires audited
results, appropriate references and an independent source/paper review.
