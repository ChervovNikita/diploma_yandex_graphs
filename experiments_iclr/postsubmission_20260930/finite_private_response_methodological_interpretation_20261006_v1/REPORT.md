# What finite private response must add

6 October 2026. Interpretation of saved literature scopes and error-diagnosis notes only; no new paper reading, computation, experiment or novelty verdict.

## The narrow possible contribution

For a member's original private row phi, let g be its mean S-own-CE gradient and b an item's whole softplus competitor margin. The fixed costs are

    finite: b(phi − eta*g) − b(phi)
    utility: −eta <grad_phi b(phi), g>,   eta=.01.

Finite response measures the realized nonlinear margin change along that declared private step. On a smooth bounded-curvature segment, the difference begins at second order in eta; native activation crossings or base kinks need no such uniform remainder. Finite response could therefore distinguish members with similar first-order utility but different useful nonlinear correction. The subsequent centering/RMS/balanced graph map can amplify or suppress that difference. Different costs or assignments alone establish no prediction benefit.

The proposed added value is a forecast that allocates productive corrective learning and transfers from S to disjoint training queries R, then improves the complete committed mean-probability predictor. It is not intrinsically a member-capacity measurement: the proved R/S factor gauge preserves predictions while changing Euclidean finite and first-order responses. Both belong to the specified coordinates, optimizer, step and state. A gauge or output-movement correction would require its own predeclared comparison; it cannot be retrofitted to rescue the frozen recipe.[S1–S3]

## Decisive rejection outcomes in the declared setting

- **No incremental quality:** finite-live fails the frozen complete-pool/member-competence criterion against matched live utility, or ordinary own/pool learning supplies the same benefit. A favorable uniform/current-margin/stop-Q contrast alone does not isolate finite response.
- **Errors only move:** baseline-wrong→correct repairs are canceled by baseline-correct→wrong harms, especially in the baseline common-error cohort. Wrong→different-wrong changes or oracle-member gains do not establish served-pool repair. Better NLL/Brier with unchanged wrong labels is probabilistic improvement, not classification correction.[S5]
- **No useful transfer or aligned mechanism:** benefits remain on S, responsibility scores or favorable subgroups; full R/held-pool quality fails, or fixed affinity-removal/permutation contrasts fail the proposed graph-alignment claim. Preserve every fixed outcome rather than selecting a successful arm.
- **A simpler explanation suffices:** rankings reflect the proved coordinate artifact, or equal-geometry/output-movement ordinary correction matches the claimed effect. Invariance alone would not demonstrate corrective value.[S2–S4]
