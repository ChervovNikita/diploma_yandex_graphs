The pinned author repository supports MSE training only for RootNeighbours. It is a five-output **node-regression** construction with explicit role masks, not molecular graph regression. No ZINC loader/config or native CoGNN/GAT dispatch exists in this snapshot. Do not call a ZINC extension a native competent CoGNN recipe.

RootNeighbours can establish that a source operator or action-gradient path behaves as specified. Its target is the mean feature vector of degree6 neighbors, so a degree-aware exact structural rule is already a capable oracle. It cannot establish real-task superiority or an ensemble need. Even this synthetic source qualification has nontrivial whole-forest work; it is not a cheap independent graph dataset hidden inside one node tensor.

The README/example and saved paper parameter ranges permit an explicit source-derived baseline, but do not recover one uniquely winning benchmark configuration. Any illustrative RootNeighbours choice must be labeled proposed, and no real-task competence is inferred. ## Why regression is a different credit test

For scalar predictions f_m, squared own error and squared error of the arithmetic prediction mean give private-output derivatives proportional to `f_m-y` and `mean(f)-y`. Those signs can differ. Thus ensemble-risk credit can steer a communication policy toward useful compensation rather than only confidence-based weighting of its own residual. Componentwise the same observation holds for the native five-output task. It is standard squared-error ensemble geometry, not a new principle or a favorable learning theorem.

If all members are wrong on the same side of a scalar target, their unchanged mean remains wrong on that side. Compensation does not remove that barrier: the policies must actually change evidence and predictions. Lower pool MSE with deteriorating individual predictors may be undesirable collusion; mean and worst-member regression risk must remain explicit. A molecular real-task result requires contemporary capable single and genuinely independently learned ensemble controls, acquired with complete costs; no pretrained teachers are needed to define the shared policy model.

## ZINC feasibility boundary and role contract

The checked PyG2.3 source documents penalized-logP scalar targets, categorical atom/bond inputs, up-to38-heavy-atom molecular graphs and the predefined12,000-graph benchmark subset with separate role-index files. Standard10k/1k/1k counts should be verified from an exact provider/role manifest before admission; the inspected excerpts independently confirm the12k total, not that breakdown. No dataset archive, molecule, role-index payload or target was downloaded. The default loader processes train, validation and test together, so future acquisition needs a separate reviewed TRAIN/VALID projection.

A ZINC extension needs categorical atom/bond encoders with verified vocabularies, a bond-aware environment, graph pooling and a scalar head, plus a fixed horizon/optimizer/serving/selection contract. The author's `MolConv` adds bond embeddings to neighbor states; it is not automatically PyG GINE's message nonlinearity. Blindly reusing the Peptides/OGB encoder is not a qualified ZINC port. Shared environment weights plus private binary policies and own-loss prediction boundaries are an attributed adaptation. Private feature-factor or head coordinates must also be defined if policy-versus-feature credit is to be compared at equal architecture.

**No ZINC grid is adopted.** If the active risk studies justify continuation, a later admission must first pin a strong GINE/PNA/GraphGPS or other contemporary native regression reference and competent independent ensemble with a disclosed common budget. Freeze MSE-versus-native-L1 training and MAE/RMSE reporting before outcomes; changing loss is an adaptation, not numerical reproduction of published MAE results. The already stated logical controls remain ordinary own-policy learning, ensemble credit to policies, and the same credit to feature coordinates. They must not be expanded or tuned here.

Charge all four environment paths, both receive/send networks at every layer, temperature paths, sampling and backward, categorical/bond preparation, checkpoint selection and complete validation/serving. With p action layers, the unoptimized author structure calls `2p+1` graph aggregations per environment layer/member, not one. A one-layer MeanGNN pair can share its identical unweighted neighbor mean within a member/layer, but this algebraic opportunity is unimplemented and supplies no measured speedup or cross-member cache validity.

Disposition: native operator mechanics are recoverable; a real regression study remains conditional on source/runtime/data/competence qualification. Neither RootNeighbours nor public code resolves the quality question. No implementation, new task grid, training, data/TEST scoring, or method adoption is authorized by this note.
