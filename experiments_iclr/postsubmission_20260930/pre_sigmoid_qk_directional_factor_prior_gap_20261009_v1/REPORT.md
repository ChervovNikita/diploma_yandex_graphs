The generic parameterization collision is exact. Let `a_m=s_m+δ_m` and `b_m=s_m−δ_m`. This is an invertible coordinate change, with `s_m=(a_m+b_m)/2`, `δ_m=(a_m−b_m)/2`. The stated layer is just two unconstrained pre-sigmoid diagonal output scales a and b applied to a tied projection t. At a fixed fitted state, fold them into effective untied matrices and biases:

`W_(Q,m)=diag(r_m) W_shared diag(a_m)`, `b_(Q,m)=b diag(a_m)`;

`W_(K,m)=diag(r_m) W_shared diag(b_m)`, `b_(K,m)=b diag(b_m)`.

Thus an ordinary single model with separate affine Q/K maps can take exactly this operator. Every member of an ordinary independently trained ensemble can take it too.
