# Cost and failure accounting

Two graphs times three families times four configs times three seeds=72 family cells. Per graph there are108 member trajectory cells and72 native model cores:12 single cores,12 GNNM common cores,48 independent cores. Four independent models retain full same-width parameters, activations, optimizers and complete forwards/backwards. GNNM does not reduce native block depth, token count, attention axes, graph propagation or global reductions.

Squirrel cap:36 family cells times2000=72000 family updates, equivalent to216000 complete member trajectory updates. Photo:36 times1200=43200 actual family updates, equivalent to129600 complete member trajectory updates. Squirrel patience may stop a family earlier, but every chosen checkpoint and actual update count is retained. Photo always completes200+1000 updates; restoring Adam can rewind optimizer step state but does not erase work already performed.

Every cell records separate preprocessing/fit/selected-inference measurements, residual-inclusive wall, selected checkpoint, traces, parameter/tensor bytes and all stage/update selections. Qualification separately measures native parity and checkpoint replay. Best checkpoints copy model and Adam to CPU; these temporary/host costs must be measured. Squirrel materializes13 FP32 tokens per node and independent native forward materializations remain chargeable. No disk token cache or logit serving cache is used. Tokens remain resident within the declared warm graph context after charged cold preprocessing. Cold serving loads graph/checkpoint, computes all preprocessing/tokens and constructs teacher/correction; every warm request performs four complete teacher forwards plus correction. Ten warm samples are retained per correction arm.

Family selection retains all12 costs, study closure retains all72 attempts. Correction costs distinguish externally completed teacher fits from checkpoint/preprocessing replay; replay is not mislabeled as a new teacher fit. Fixed secondary probability computation is saved/report-only and cannot be selected. No matched-compute claim is made.

Output folders are exclusive. A failed attempt records exception, traceback, measured cost where available, residual wall and `automatic_retry=false`; the folder is retained. Selection requires all registered cells and rejects failed, missing or qualification-only cells. Root may investigate feasibility/source defects before scientific admission, but cannot silently replace a scientific failure or a cell with favorable results. No runtime duration, memory feasibility, trained utility or efficiency advantage has been measured here.
