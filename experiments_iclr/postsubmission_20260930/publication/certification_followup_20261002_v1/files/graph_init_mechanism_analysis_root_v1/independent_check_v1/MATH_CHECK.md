## Jensen-gap interpretation

The nodewise inequality CE(mean raw logits,y)<=mean CE(raw logits,y) is correct because class-logit cross-entropy is convex. It compares a pool with its own members, and gives no ranking against another predictor/control. For finite logits at a common center z, the exact CE Hessian is H=diag(p)-p*p^T, p=softmax(z). For shrinking route perturbations delta_m with mean zero, the gap is `(1/2)*mean delta_m^T H delta_m + o(mean ||delta_m||^2)`. The label-linear terms cancel.

H is positive semidefinite and has the common-class-shift gauge in its nullspace. Thus predictive spread at a fixed pooled center has a nonnegative objective cost; pure gauge spread has zero cost and no effect on probabilities. The initializer’s class-centering and pairwise JVP/finite-step gates ([graph_band_route_initializer.py:288](</Users/alex/Documents/ChatGPT/anogena allocation/postsubmission_research_20260930/continuous_method_gap_search_v1/round15_graph_route_initialization/prototype/graph_band_route_initializer.py:288>)) appropriately remove that gauge and reject null/duplicate directions. This still supplies no heldout utility or smoothness guarantee. The stated mean-probability-pooling distinction is correct.
