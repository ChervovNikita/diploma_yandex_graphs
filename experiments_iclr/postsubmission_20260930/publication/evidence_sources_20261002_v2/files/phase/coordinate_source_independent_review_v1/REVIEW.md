## Remaining findings, ordered by impact

1. **[P2] The cold measurement does not satisfy the protocol's deployed cold-latency requirement.** Final `coordinate_ensemble_runner_v1/runner.py:652–665` measures exactly one resident forward of the **initial untrained** model, called before training at `765–766`. It has neither a cold median/p95 nor selected-checkpoint cold measurements. Selected-checkpoint reload is timed separately (`875–881`), and subsequent inference is warm (`883–894`). The protocol requests synchronized cold and warm full-graph median/p95 with common graph/feature setup included in cold latency (`coordinate_ensemble_pilot_design_v1/PROTOCOL.md:68`). Also, the only input-transfer component includes train/validation **targets** together with graph/features (`runner.py:708–716`, reported at `928`), so it cannot be directly charged as label-free deployed setup. The runner honestly labels its limits; this is an interface gap between the planning protocol and its implementation, not evidence of a measured performance failure. Add a separately admitted selected-checkpoint cold deployment measurement and separate target-transfer timing, or explicitly narrow the adopted protocol's cold claim.

2. **[P3] Complete training cost is measured, but requested operation-level accounting is absent.** `runner.py:783–809` measures one aggregate forward/loss/backward/optimizer/audit interval; `619–649` measures aggregate inference/gather/pooling. Neither separates all-member forward, backward, optimizer or gather/permutation costs requested at `PROTOCOL.md:68`. Aggregate complete-update timing includes real work and the source's finite/nonzero checks, so it supports a total-cost comparison; it cannot substantiate an attribution of cost specifically to permutation/gather operations. Provide a fixed profiler breakdown under later admission, or declare those detailed fields deferred in the adopted protocol.

3. **[P3] Macro-F1 and member disagreement are requested but not reported or operationally defined by the runner.** `runner.py:576–589` emits NLL and accuracy only, while `PROTOCOL.md:66` also requests macro-F1 and member disagreement. Selected/final validation-only member logits are retained (`843–845`, `860–863`), so a frozen external analysis can supply these without rerunning models or reading test labels. Freeze the precise metric definitions and that analysis step before outcomes; this is missing reporting, not a failure of the primary NLL/checkpoint rule.

## Additional checked behavior and pending admission work

- The split implementation now uses the protocol's exact test-floor, validation-floor, training-remainder rule (`runner.py:252–283`; `PROTOCOL.md:11`). One common test holdout is excluded from every train/validation subdivision. Mask disjointness, coverage, target range and hashes are checked before scientific work (`443–496`).

Strict lower pooled NLL selects a complete ensemble checkpoint; ties remain earliest. Stopping respects the minimum update horizon and update-based patience (`815–856`). Root must apply the protocol's inconclusive rule for nonfinite/capacity failures or a still-improving final-horizon checkpoint. Qualification's train-node evaluation omits validation-logit CPU copies and selected checkpoint writes, so a full budget forecast must separately charge those costs.
