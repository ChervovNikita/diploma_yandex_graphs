# Research priority: predictive quality

The user made predictive quality the main priority on 2 October 2026 and explicitly welcomed disagreement when evidence supports another direction.

The active graph-initialization study tests whether training errors filtered through graph structure can give the compact members useful different starting directions. Its common-only, random-direction, altered-topology and warm-copy comparisons remain fixed. The complete cohort and later paired comparisons are needed before deciding whether one refresh during training is useful. Additional separation of embeddings is not itself evidence of better decisions.

The active BUDDY comparison measures whether compact factorized predictors retain ensemble quality against single, independent and parameter-matched predictors using identical cached graph information. The native larger model remains a competence anchor. This is an architecture comparison and does not by itself establish a new learning principle.

The next literature lane looks for a distinct quality mechanism involving correlated graph errors or member training conflicts, after checking retained conclusions. Recombining known routing, boosting or diversity losses is not enough. A candidate needs a precise changed training operation, a prior-work comparison and a representative falsifier against strong backbones before broader experiments.

Efficiency remains a possible secondary direction if a concrete new operation saves work while preserving predictions. The vector equilibrium maintainer so far applies to independent predictors too. Its asynchronous fixed-point updates are established. Shared weights alone provide no demonstrated extra arithmetic saving. That route is parked rather than assigned GPUs.

Resource availability is a scheduling constraint, not a scientific rejection criterion. A candidate with a defensible changed learning operation and a plausible quality benefit can remain worth testing even if it needs larger models or more graphs than the current GPUs can handle. Such a candidate must receive a representative evaluation design and a GPU estimate. The user has offered to obtain more GPUs once an opportunity is justified. The maintenance route is parked because its current operation supplies neither a demonstrated quality mechanism nor a GNNM-specific computational delta.

A simple compression check explains one difficulty. At a common feature vector x with independent random-sign input factors r_m, member inputs are u_m=x*r_m. Their Gram matrix has diagonal ||x||², expected off-diagonal zero and off-diagonal variance sum_j x_j^4. When features have large effective dimension (sum_j x_j²)² / sum_j x_j^4, the four inputs can have four substantial singular values. Rank compression then cannot save their dense transforms. Learned update inputs might behave differently, but no actual spectrum was measured. This algebra is a feasibility argument, not a dataset experiment or a new theorem.

No original scores changed, no new predictive advantage observed, no new method admitted and no acceptance recommendation obtained in this priority update. Source preparation, reading and bookkeeping must not be reported as predictive progress.
