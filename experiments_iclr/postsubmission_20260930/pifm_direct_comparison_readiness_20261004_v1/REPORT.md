## Decision useful now

**PIFM is a necessary direct comparator for any surviving claim about NCNC plus learned joint refinement. It is not currently a runnable native comparator on either requested benchmark.** The smallest useful path is an explicitly named **PIFM-based Pubmed-HeaRT adaptation**, with its own NCNC prior-only control and complete per-positive candidate ranking. Collab needs sparse graph and candidate-score infrastructure before qualification. Cora is the published transductive bridge but does not answer either requested benchmark; another Cora experiment should not substitute for Pubmed or Collab evidence.

Keep the existing frozen P0/J_P/F_P/C_mu futility study as the first decision. Its serving route exposes pooled completion marginals; a surviving joint-loss benefit would be training-mediated improvement of those marginals/features. It would not establish that serving consumes a coherent joint draw. PIFM integration becomes worthwhile if J_P improves ranking over both P0 and F_P and the capable count-potential single does not explain the improvement. A resource failure of C_mu or a PIFM adaptation is an incomplete comparison, not evidence favoring the bank.

## Official sources and what they actually support

- Paper: Chen, Zilberstein and Segarra, [Prior-Informed Flow Matching for Graph Reconstruction, arXiv:2601.22107v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22107v2), 18 June 2026. Reused primary §§3–4 and Algorithm 1; bounded rereads of §B.4, §D.1, §E.4 and §E.8. No proof or benchmark-table audit; no published numeric outcome is adopted.
- Author repository: [HC-SGW/PIFM](https://github.com/HC-SGW/PIFM), freshly rechecked at immutable commit **ae4d5752bd2de6e6766cd3c9fed55e3cbfb15f86**, committed 19 December 2025. The fresh branch listing contains `main` only; the fresh tags and releases responses are empty. This establishes the inspected public version, not an assertion about unpublished code or authors' actual runs.
- Public recipe: [run_cora_LP_sageheart_edgeCenter_k1.sh](https://github.com/HC-SGW/PIFM/blob/ae4d5752bd2de6e6766cd3c9fed55e3cbfb15f86/scripts/run_cora_LP_sageheart_edgeCenter_k1.sh). It uses a GraphSAGE-HEART prior, 85/5/10 split, one-hop capped 96-node subgraphs, flow batch 128, 500 flow epochs, 1,000 prior epochs, width 64/four layers/four heads, Adam lr 2e-4 and **100 Euler inference steps**. The filename's `k1` refers to hop radius; it does not imply one inference step. The paper's §E.8 instead declares 50/15/35 and an NCN prior, while §B.4 names depth-one NCNC. These differences remain explicit.
- Authentic ranking contract: [Juanhui28/HeaRT](https://github.com/Juanhui28/HeaRT), pinned **c447cbff4c493b60d14b6544c3c39d3b9c5ddff0** in the predecessor. HeaRT_small supplies per-positive hard-negative rows and average optimistic/pessimistic rank. Its GraphSAGE architecture name does not turn PIFM's random-nonedge evaluator into HeaRT.

The paper's exact operation is an NCNC-informed adjacency source, optionally perturbed, followed by graph-conditioned transport of all hidden subgraph pairs. Algorithm 1 initializes A0 from known observations and prior probabilities, trains on At=(1-t)A0+tA1, then applies K Euler updates. §B.4 explicitly identifies `IncompleteCN1Predictor`, depth one. §E.8's Cora variant uses a seed-only MSE loss, masked seed context, no inference noise and logit averaging across covering subgraphs. §E.4's weighted CE is a distinct variant; its positive-class weight capped at 50 changes the objective. Seed-only MSE is the most directly source-qualified bridge; adding the CE version would require a separately frozen implementation and budget.

PIFM has source-declared disjoint TU graph tasks and Cora transductive LP. The inspected public version does not supply a qualified NCNC/weighted-CE subgraph route or native official Collab/Pubmed-HeaRT recipe. The generic prior-array interface is not enough to certify either. A future adapter must be called an adaptation, with published ingredients and local corrections identified.

## Compute estimates and what must be measured

**Global dense arrays are decisive before GPU work.** With N=19,717/235,868, one float32 N×N adjacency occupies 1.448/207.252 GiB. The stitcher constructor's float64 sum plus int32 counts needs 4.345/621.755 GiB. Adding final float64 logits and float32 output gives a named-array lower bound of 8.689/**1,243.510 GiB**, excluding the boolean mask, NumPy temporaries, original adjacencies and subgraph caches. These are arithmetic storage bounds from source dtypes, not measured peaks. Sparse candidate storage is O(unique scored pairs), plus covering-context references.

For S≤96 and B=128, one float32 dense subgraph matrix batch is 4.5 MiB; one 16-channel matrix is 72 MiB. The forward retains multiple channel stacks, intermediate attention tensors and training gradients. `pow_tensor` incurs dense S³ work even though the global graph is sparse. These primitive sizes do not certify a peak-memory bound or throughput.

Let U be the number of unique evaluation contexts and b=128. One K-step complete serve pays K*ceil(U/b) denoiser batch forwards and prior scores for all hidden subgraph pairs, plus graph construction/stitching. With one context per candidate occurrence and no deduplication:

- Pubmed's source-declared 1,110,216 score occurrences give **8,674 batch forwards/serve at K=1**, or 867,400 at K=100. With 100 complete selectors and two final replays: 884,748 / 88,474,800 batch forwards respectively. Actual U could be smaller; it is not known here. At the cap, prior matrices expose at most 5,062,584,960 unordered pair occurrences per complete serve before reuse/deduplication.
- Collab's source-declared 160,084 score occurrences give **1,251 batch forwards/serve at K=1**, or 125,100 at K=100. With 100 selectors and two replays: 127,602 / 12,760,200 respectively. At the cap, at most 729,983,040 unordered pair occurrences per serve before reuse/deduplication.

A balanced positive/negative seed-only adaptation using every Pubmed TRAIN row has 75,352 contexts and ceil(75,352/128)=589 flow updates/epoch: 58,900 at E=100, 294,500 at the public E=500. Using all 1,179,052 Collab TRAIN records would give 2,358,104 contexts, 18,423 updates/epoch and 1,842,300/9,211,500 updates for E=100/500. If root instead binds the ongoing Collab literal replay's 1,114,112 retained records per epoch, its two query groups give 2,228,224 contexts and **17,408 updates/epoch**. These alternatives must be frozen prospectively; counts are not an automatic authorization to change the native program. Optimizer-update counts alone cannot be converted into relative FLOPs across architectures.

The source/prior construction may dominate serving: each 96-node context has 4,560 unordered pairs, and each NCNC prior query has its own recursive completion support. Global embedding reuse can save work only under a declared information/masking contract. A query-specific recomputed global encoder can make training prohibitively expensive; sharing one encoder across the permitted native masked batch is a distinct adapter design root must freeze. Global N×N prior precomputation is not admissible at Collab scale.

No defensible wall-time, GPU-hour or peak estimate follows from the public paper/script or the native NCNC engineering runtime. Root should first obtain seconds/context-batch, prior queries/second, distribution of subgraph sizes, unique context count and actual coverage multiplicities. Then calculate:

`T_total = T_prior_fit + T_context_build + T_prior_matrices + E*T_full_flow_epoch + (V+2)*T_complete_K_serve + T_serialization/audit`.

Report uncertainty from cold/warm caches, variable sizes, duplicate reuse and GPU backward separately. Do not infer flow throughput from the faster shared full-graph NCNC route or assert that the smaller parameter bank saves compute before actual measurements.

## Attribution and the narrow distinction that remains testable

GRAN already supplies the mixture of conditionally factorial Bernoulli components, complete-pattern logsumexp likelihood and responsibility gradients. NCNC supplies recursive completion and its nonlinear target readout. Cardinality models supply normalized count potentials and exact sum-product/marginals. PIFM supplies the direct NCNC-prior-to-joint-graph-refinement operation and the coherent-configuration motivation. The source-only distinction is:

> A joint TRAIN observation-pattern auxiliary tied to the existing shared NCNC recursive scorers changes the marginals used by pooled detached completion, while serving retains the native target decoder and adds no flow refiner.

This is a specific learner/support/objective/serving composition. It is not a novel mixture principle, general graph joint model, posterior over true missing edges, ensemble-necessity result or novelty conclusion from an unsuccessful code search. C_mu conditions on full identity-bearing context but factors within count pairs; therefore it is capable yet does not exhaust same-count spatial dependencies. A PIFM adaptation with richer graph context is an informative comparator, even if its hidden support and cap are different.

The decisive measurement is a **replicated paired improvement in complete native served ranking**, J_P>P0 and J_P>F_P, not merely lower source NLL or more member disagreement. If C_mu matches/exceeds J_P, the current bank-specific explanation fails. If a qualified PIFM adaptation matches/exceeds at comparable full cost, the useful distinction may be only the training/serving interface or a measured efficiency tradeoff, not superior structural modeling. Any efficiency assertion requires quality at the stated budget, full training/inference work and peaks, not an implementation's observed failure.

For an identity-aware higher-order claim beyond counts, fixed-mask diagnostics must expose positive observation-pattern competence and the arrangement-sensitive closure statistic within fixed support/count strata, accompanied by a capable full-context structured single. Pooled serving currently uses only unary marginals, so a motif diagnostic alone does not show that joint configurations caused the ranking gain. A prospectively frozen arrangement intervention or capable autoregressive/latent single is required for that broader claim. Current five-seed private-versus-pooled and one-seed joint pilot figures were provided as task context, not independently audited or adopted in this packet.
