## The weakness to address

The completed Wiki24 unit-plus-contrast model has competent but mostly redundant members: mean member accuracy is about 81.58%, while its served probability mean is about 81.63%, below ordinary independent pooling at 82.04%. Its all-member-wrong nodes have strict common wrong rivals. Increasing useful alternatives requires changing learned predictions; reweighting those unchanged probabilities cannot overcome a strict common rival. A new pool can also become correct while its members remain individually wrong about different rivals, so correct-member acquisition and actual pooled-only rescue must remain separate observations.

Direct12 supplied no affirmative final-head result: full and factorized refits tie in mean accuracy, both below the untouched native model, with the declared convergence limitation retained. This neither proves lost graph information nor warrants another head-refit tuning attempt.

## Retained rule: graph-regime allocation of private supervised risk

Different routes can learn from different emphases on the same complete labeled graph, instead of being instructed to separate hidden coordinates. This reuses the **unexecuted structure-assignment question** saved on 3 October; its node-classification adaptation below is not a newly discovered primitive or an established novelty gap.

Use the existing four-route, unit-initialized internal-BE Polynormer. Train from scratch with the full original own-label recipe and graph. Keep attention ownership unchanged; add no router, teacher, contrastive head or private attention intervention.

Before training, rank TRAIN nodes within each observed class by incoming degree on the retained predictor graph, breaking ties by canonical TRAIN identity. Partition each class's cumulative unit node mass into four equal bins. Fractionally split a boundary node if necessary. Let q(i,m) be its allocated mass: every row sums to one, and every member receives one quarter of each class's TRAIN mass. Set

    w(i,m) = 0.5 + 2 q(i,m).

Every node retains positive supervision in every route; weights lie between 0.5 and 2.5. Their mean across members is exactly one, and each member's weighted class totals equal the unweighted totals. The fixed half anchor is inherited from the saved assignment proposal, not selected using outcomes.

For the original two-view per-node CE ell_m(i), take shared-parameter gradients from the ordinary mean-member CE, and private-factor gradients from the same-state weighted CE:

    shared θ:  gradient of mean_(i,m) ell_m(i)
    private φ: gradient of mean_(i,m) w(i,m) ell_m(i).

Collect both block derivatives before one native Adam transition. All existing private factors are eligible; no new recipient-site search is added. This is an explicit block learning rule, generally not the gradient of one common scalar objective. Later shared trajectories can change through the adapted factors. Degree allocation does not guarantee competence or imply that degree caused the observed mistakes. Serving remains the uniform probability mean of all four full routes.
