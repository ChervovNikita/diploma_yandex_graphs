# Wrong-class prediction contrast: independent bounded critique

10 October 2026. **No distinct graph learning mechanism survives this scoped prior and shortcut comparison.** The proposal is an attributed output-diversity adaptation with a real change from the fitted hidden objective. Its utility remains untested. This is not an exhaustive novelty exclusion or an experiment admission.

## What the preserved evidence establishes

The preserved Wiki15 report found practical decision redundancy:14 of15 banks disagree on only22–38 of5274 selected-development nodes, all15 have zero pooled-only rescues, and almost every all-member-wrong node has a strict common wrong rival. Greater disagreement in S/6203 came with weaker members and more pool harms. The report did not measure hidden separation, prediction-nullspace movement or graph-evidence specialization. Its failed hidden recipe does not falsify output diversity. No score was recomputed here.

## Exact candidate and closest prior

For a TRAIN target with truth y, the draft uses q_m=softmax(z_m,c for c≠y), u_m=q_m/||q_m||₂. Same member/target across two dropout views is positive; other members on that target are negatives in symmetric InfoNCE at temperature0.2. Existing internal factors receive ∂(F+.05A+.05R_out); shared and input/head boundary factors receive ∂F, at the same old parameters followed by one Adam update.

The wrong-class object is **exactly ADP's object**: p_m,c=(1−p_m,y)q_m,c for c≠y, so normalizing either vector gives the same u_m. ADP already excludes truth and combines own CE, ensemble entropy and the Gram logdet of these vectors. Replacing entropy/logdet with member/view InfoNCE changes optimization geometry and adds consistency; it is not the complete ADP algorithm.

CDLG already supplies same-object/different-channel negatives and same-channel/object cross-view positives. Its graph channels/routing and later classifier differ from independently label-supervised member probability pools, and the saved printed-loss caveat prevents claiming an exact author implementation. DICE supplies class-conditioned redundancy reduction with a learned joint-versus-same-class-product discriminator and VCEB terms. Removing y and contrasting members is neither conditional MI nor DICE. Private block routing is already specified by the existing adapter. These are material operational differences within established ancestry, not a demonstrated new graph consequence.

## The shortcut is a stable class-bias code

The loss identifies member identity from wrong-class allocation. A member-wide preferred wrong class, stable across dropout views, can do that without different graph dependence. Internal-only gradients do not prevent it: private scaling of shared biased or nearly constant hidden features can create a virtual member logit offset even while explicit head factors get own-only gradients.

A capable shared posterior plus constant member offsets, z_m,c(x)=g_c(x)+b_m,c, is the direct diagnostic. Its graph/input margin responses are identical across members:

    [z_m,y(Tx)−z_m,c(Tx)] − [z_m,y(x)−z_m,c(x)]
      = [g_y(Tx)−g_c(Tx)] − [g_y(x)−g_c(x)].

Nevertheless its probability vectors, conditional wrong vectors and decisions can differ. Such differences can sometimes improve a pool through posterior correction; they do not show distinct graph evidence. Probability-response differences alone also cannot exclude this control because nonlinear softmax changes with b.

Two analytic failure witnesses need no model or numerical fixture:

- Let p_m(y)=t and assign each member's remaining mass1−t to a different wrong class. Angular separation is maximal in the limiting case, yet every member is wrong when t<1/2 and the probability pool is still wrong when t<1/(M+1). For M4, separating four wrong rivals does not remove this competence threshold. At t>1/2, all members are already correct and large diversity need add no decision utility. Both are output-changing template effects, not graph mechanisms.
- Let q_m=a e_c+(1−a)e_d_m, with a>1/2, shared dominant wrong class c and different tail classes d_m. Decreasing a while it stays above1/2 increases angular diversity; the common rival still defeats truth whenever (1−t)a>t. Tail spread can lower R without a repair.

## Semantics, capacity and optimization limits

For a genuinely ambiguous or mislabeled example, common alternatives may be semantically correct uncertainty. Forcing different members to allocate that mass differently need not create missing information. Removing truth from the penalty gives zero direct derivative in z_y, but a private parameter step changes z_y through its Jacobian and affects future shared/head own-risk updates. It preserves neither true-class probability nor class means/member competence.

Binary degeneration is exact: the sole wrong coordinate makes u=1, R=log M and its gradient zero. Independent Bernoulli tasks cannot be turned into multiclass alternatives by stacking outputs. Native ADP has Gram rank at most C−1, so its bare determinant is zero when M>C−1; copied/saturated vectors can also be singular when that count permits full rank. Pairwise InfoNCE avoids the determinant but cannot create extra semantic alternatives in a small simplex.

Copied deterministic members are a stationary symmetry case. Separate dropout can break realized symmetry, with no guarantee of useful serving differences. Softmax wrong vectors are nonnegative; contrast can approach its limiting separation by making wrong-class relative logits extreme, leading to saturated gradients and member stereotypes. Equal coefficient0.05 across hidden/output losses does not establish equal force strength.

Exact hidden nullspace changes, compensated feature/head gauges and common shifts of all logits leave this output loss unchanged. That is useful. A common shift of all wrong logits relative to truth also leaves q/u unchanged while changing true mass and serving confidence. Label-independent posterior calibration, tail reallocation and virtual biases remain shortcuts; calling them exact function-preserving gauges would be incorrect.

The two gradient permissions generally form a mixed vector field rather than one scalar global-gradient update. Own-only shared/head gradients remove a direct auxiliary term, not its indirect trajectory effects or an Adam descent guarantee. A structurally zero prediction pullback into the allowed private block stays zero.

## Minimum competent comparisons and decision

For the narrow changed-object question, retain four otherwise matched policies: A-private/no R, hidden-private R, proposed output-private R, and complete ADP-objective-private with both entropy and bare logdet. Match all parameter permissions, initializations, TRAIN/auxiliary targets, two views, own-loss reductions, horizon, selector and probability serving. Declare one prospective competent ADP alpha/beta setting and all reductions; do not silently replace a singular native Gram with jitter/pseudodeterminant, or describe a failed restricted control as failure of published ADP. Charge extra reverse/retained-graph work.

For the graph-mechanism claim, add the **one capable shared-posterior/class-offset control** above. It must use the same information, supervision/view/selection allowance and an independently demonstrated competent shared predictor; a deliberately weak frozen predictor is not a meaningful comparator. Disclose its smaller private capacity and changed eligibility rather than pretending it is an exact architecture match. A within-TRAIN-class peer-target permutation is a complementary low-cost pairing falsifier, but defeating it alone does not exclude the posterior shortcut. Failure of constant biases excludes only that narrow shortcut, not every posterior-only transformation.

Competent native single, a capable single at the relevant capacity/stochastic-opportunity budget, and genuine independently trained4 remain utility references. If claiming that private routing itself is essential, the same output object with matched full-gradient recipients is an additional attribution comparison; it is not needed merely to name the limited output-object adaptation.

Keep whole-population served accuracy/NLL, mean/worst members, coverage, common rivals cleared/introduced, acquired-correct-member and all-wrong pooled-only repairs, and introduced errors separate. More spread, lower R, rival churn or Jensen gap is insufficient. If a class-offset control supplies the same effect, no graph specialization is established. Even a useful output-private result supports this attributed composition; a distinct graph mechanism additionally needs a demonstrated predictive consequence beyond posterior correction and the relevant prior controls.
