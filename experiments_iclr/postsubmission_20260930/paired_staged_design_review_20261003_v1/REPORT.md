## Design conclusions

- **Staging and failure accounting are coherent.** Stage 1 requires all three Squirrel joint-attempt records and all twelve arm terminals before comparison. A trigger additionally requires all twelve selected fits and passed prerequisites. Failure/deferment makes it unevaluable; there is no successful-subset score, extra alpha, per-arm fallback, restart, or replacement initializer. A single helper invocation shares the first jointly accepted alpha across the four arms.
- **Reuse is appropriately limited.** All four arms in each block share one exact recorded native warm donor, with fresh restorations and preserved optimizer/alias/phase/RNG context. Old fitted checkpoints, logits, comparisons, and registry claims are excluded from evidence. Outcome independence is declared in the author receipt; this narrow review did not independently reconstruct the donor history.
- **Pairing and scope are correctly stated.** `(17,0)`, `(29,1)`, and `(43,2)` are three matched seed/split blocks per graph, not a seed-by-split factorial or independent estimates of their effects. Overlapping blocks do not create independent-node replications. Photo also changes backbone, so its followup cannot isolate a causal heterophily effect. The stated per-graph means and equal-graph macro mean are descriptive comparisons.
- **Native continuation semantics are specified.** Each exact warm state must pass full-output AD/order/finiteness, native/K1/K4 equality, disposable actual-warm Adam-step equivalence, and installed-output equality. Photo requires its actual global cross-node closure. Named Adam coordinate transport, empty new-factor moments, the same post-warm RNG restored after initialization, FP32 training, mean member CE, and raw-logit pooling are fixed. Epoch 0 and every update compete under source-validation NLL with earliest strict minimum and selected-state replay. Secondary probability pooling cannot select another checkpoint.
- **The strict NLL gate is a development decision.** Full-node's three-block mean must beat each control, with no tolerance or secondary-metric substitution. Conditional Photo pursuit must be disclosed. A negative or unavailable Squirrel trigger defers this followup and does not reject the homophilic alternative; a different prospective test requires its own declared scope. This design alone supplies no superiority, generalization, or novelty conclusion.
- **Final-label closure is explicit.** Stage 1 opens no final labels. The defined two-graph confirmation requires all six attempts and all 24 successful selected fits, complete cost/error terminals, immutable fingerprints, and the frozen full comparison before the separate once-only six-pack release. All four arms are scored; validation signs do not choose a final arm. Missing/failed/deferred studies cannot open that gate, and the existing cohort's all72 rule is unchanged.
- **Resource planning remains separate from scientific merit.** Serial scheduling, cost exclusions, conservative memory screens, abort accounting, and resource deferral are identified. Forecasts are proxies and do not certify current availability or actual paired peaks.

The forthcoming sealed driver should implement these rules directly. Exact source review remains necessary before execution; no additional design changes or independent test suite are recommended here.
