## Fixed strict-rival cohorts and equal references

On BASE's fixed 1,955 strict-common-rival readouts, private corrects only 19 (5/7/7), versus pair-mixture 50, private full 39, shuffled 44 and equipped genuine I4 163. Private's own current strict-rival cohort increases to 1,986 (675/664/647); native pooling corrects zero of these current cases. Original graph serving also corrects zero BASE strict rivals. Thus private does change some cases unavailable to the fixed scorer on BASE, but the complete controls do not establish a distinctive useful private acquisition role.

These acquisition cases and original graph repairs occupy different BASE error categories: newly acquired coverage was unavailable in BASE, whereas all 281 original shared graph repairs recovered covered BASE pooling losses. The 19 fixes of original strict rivals also cannot be original graph repairs, since the original graph scorer fixes zero such cases. This corrects the prospective note's overly broad statement that these specific overlaps were unknown. Their error roles are distinct; what remains unmeasured is whether graph serving on the new context bank preserves those fixes and serves its other newly acquired alternatives with acceptable harms. The earlier paired-bootstrap comparison proves that some acquired alternatives can be rescued, while also recording serving harms and failed final capable-reference quality. Its rescue fraction is not a prediction for this context bank.

Original graph-processed ordinary and factorized I4 score 81.064341% and 80.887372%, respectively; original graph-processed BASE is 81.070661%. These are available benchmarks, not measured context-fusion contrasts. Private's positive raw comparison with equipped or original I4 does not establish superiority after equal serving. The equipped I4 would require the same fixed processing opportunity in any admitted serving comparison. No such comparison is justified by the present prerequisite assessment.

## Disposition

The conditional note required complete context utility and a supported acquisition role before serving work. The complete family supplies neither the frozen utility result nor evidence that private acquisition repairs plausibly survive enough serving harms to improve final protected quality. Close this exact conditional combination. Retain the measured repairs and the general acquisition/serving mechanism in history without a rescue grid, altered confidence rule or another fit.

This assessment used released complete JSON only, plus the prior sealed assessment and complete graph-serving evidence. No arrays, model/checkpoint state, remote access, new fit, launcher or analysis-source change occurred. Original scores, canonical research state, production freeze and reader source remain unchanged. The conclusion is scoped to this exact procedure on encountered development; it does not claim that all future acquisition/serving combinations fail.
