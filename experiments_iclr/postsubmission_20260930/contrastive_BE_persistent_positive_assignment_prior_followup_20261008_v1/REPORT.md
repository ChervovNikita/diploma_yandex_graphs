## Decision

**Continue the frozen context9 test; no new experiment is justified by this search alone.** The two newly read method sections do not reproduce the complete fixed-context positive assignment. This bounded finding does not establish novelty. Learned contrastive heads, adaptive temperatures, multiple GNN views and unchanged input graphs are established ingredients. MA-GCL and the retained PMGCL method remain unresolved access limits.

The operational hypothesis is narrow: when four members share a predictor backbone, persistent different label-compatible positive relations might produce useful member class rankings without the competence cost of arbitrary repulsion. It remains an empirical hypothesis. Neither supervised label agreement nor distinct hidden/factor updates guarantees improved serving.

AMCL's inspected construction supports the general possibility of learning different similarity functions under one feature backbone. It does not show that retained classification members become competent or that a probability ensemble benefits. CGCL aims at peer representation agreement. It shows that using several model views without perturbing the predictor graph is insufficient as a contribution claim.

A later trainable temperature or weighting proposal must credit AMCL and existing contrastive weighting methods. A free nonnegative loss coefficient can evade a penalty by falling toward zero. Learned coefficients also alter optimization and require their own controls; the current constant coefficient and frozen conditions remain unchanged.
