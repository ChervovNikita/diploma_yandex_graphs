PRECISE OPERATOR

Let Z_m be the actual input to the linear neighbor branch, with M members and a common fixed node operator P. If the layer first performs memberwise LayerNorm, use Z_m=LN(H_m); projecting H and normalizing later is a different model. Set mean Zbar=(1/M) sum_m Z_m and centered Delta_m=Z_m-Zbar. A global basis U in R^(M x R) satisfies U^T 1=0 and U^T U=I_R. It is constant across nodes and feature coordinates for this graph operation. Define J=11^T/M and Q=J+UU^T.

Compute C_r=sum_m U_mr Delta_m, then P Zbar and P C_r, and reconstruct

 Ahat_m = P Zbar + sum_r U_mr P C_r.

Because P and Q act on different axes, this equals applying P to sum_j Q_mj Z_j. In node-major replica coordinates the message operator is P tensor Q. The identity remains true for a data-dependent global U computed from the current states, conditional on that U; it does not make U's selection or differentiation free. No feature diagonal has changed P or manufactured a new graph filter.

R=0 gives the previous shared-mean message. R=M-1 and a complete contrast basis give Q=I_M and exact private messages for the same supplied states. For M=4, R=1 transports two feature-width matrices rather than four; R=2 transports three. These are edge-channel counts, not numbers of sparse API calls or end-to-end speedups. Packing an exact ensemble can also use one call. Orthogonal projectors can have negative mixing entries, so this is generally signed linear communication rather than a convex gate.

The private local/root/residual state and nonlinear update remain at full member width. Projection does not commute with LayerNorm, GELU or other private nonlinear transformations. Private trajectories may regenerate omitted contrasts, and their nonlinear responses can alter the next mean. Retaining them does not prove useful diversity, and transmitting a low-rank message does not algebraically collapse the whole recurrence into a narrow ordinary model. Simultaneously relabeling members and basis preserves the operator; a fixed labeled basis alone is not permutation-invariant pooling.

This factorization does not apply unchanged to member-dependent attention, different sampled neighborhoods or different graph normalizations P_m. Node-dependent mixing also fails the simple interchange: the source-node mixer and receiving-node mixer need not agree across an edge. Nonlinear edge messages, such as message functions depending jointly on both states and edge features, require separate qualification. The implementation-source agent owns actual family eligibility; no model source was opened for that purpose here.

HONEST QUALITY-PRESERVATION HYPOTHESIS AND FAILURE CONDITIONS

The plausible hypothesis is that task-relevant neighbor contrasts occupy a stable low-dimensional member subspace, so transmitting a preset R>0 retains complementary predictive information lost at R=0 while reducing total paid work relative to exact propagation. It needs prospective deployed pooled prediction/calibration and total cost evidence. High captured variance, persistent private states, different basis coefficients or an unchanged mean message do not establish that claim.

At the same states, omitted message error is P applied to the discarded member contrasts. Its Frobenius norm is bounded by ||P||_2 times the discarded input norm. With declared Lipschitz downstream maps, standard operator-norm bounds propagate this error, with amplification through layer products and accumulated approximation errors. These are ordinary spectral/subspace and Lipschitz bounds, not theorem novelty or an audited global quality guarantee. A row-stochastic directed P need not have Euclidean operator norm at most one. Normalization, learned scales and private maps must not be silently treated as contractive.

PCA on the unpropagated centered states minimizes their energy loss, not necessarily message error or label loss. Message-error PCA uses the propagated centered Gram matrix and can choose a different subspace. Rare-node signal can have little global energy; different nodes/features can require mutually incompatible directions; private readouts can amplify a small discarded mode; and a direction useful at one layer may disappear or rotate later. Input-energy optimality alone is insufficient.

An elementary failure construction sets Z_m=epsilon b_m s with b a centered unit direction orthogonal to U. The compressed neighbor contrast is zero. Private maps can scale by 1/epsilon and apply ReLU, making the mean nonlinear response depend on P s despite arbitrarily small discarded input energy. Keeping the root states does not guarantee recovery of this neighbor-dependent signal when the declared prediction needs it. This is algebraic counterexample logic, not a synthetic run or representative project outcome.

Fixing U gives a persistent global bottleneck; learning it optimizes the actual training loss rather than a PCA reconstruction objective. Dynamic PCA permits global adaptation but requires a selection rule, refresh schedule, tie/eigengap policy and train/serve behavior. Differentiating U(Z) adds its chain-rule terms; detaching it changes the training rule. Rotations within a retained subspace leave Q unchanged, so basis vectors are not individually identified mechanisms. Label or outcome-based rank/basis choices after evaluation would invalidate a prospective claim.

PAID COSTS

For N nodes, feature width d and E nonzero graph entries, exact sparse channel work is O(M E d); the compressed branch is O((R+1) E d). Centering costs O(M N d), and encoding plus reconstruction costs O(M R N d). All full private states, local normalization, dense/private/root/FFN maps and outputs remain. Their storage and computation do not acquire an R/M reduction.

A fixed basis has small stored coefficients. A learned per-layer basis adds O(MR) parameters, optimizer state and orthogonality maintenance. Dynamic input PCA can require O(M^2 N d) Gram construction plus O(M^3) eigendecomposition and reductions/communication. Obtaining the exact propagated Gram by first computing every P Delta_m spends the full graph work that compression was meant to avoid. Any sampled or cached substitute has paid estimation/refresh work and needs its own error/shift qualification. Small M makes eigendecomposition small, not the traversal of every member activation free.

Ordinary factored reverse mode propagates compressed adjoints through P^T; learning a global basis need not materialize all full-width P Z merely to form a basis gradient. Dynamic basis selection and its differentiation still have their declared costs. Charge forward, backward, optimizer, projection, basis selection/refresh, synchronization, private-state peak memory and inference graph passes. A 50% sparse-channel reduction for M4/R1 is not a 50% end-to-end saving. No historical timing ratios or current GPU limitation are used for rejection here.

CONTROLS AND FALSIFIER, NOT AN EXECUTION PLAN

Essential endpoints are efficient exact M-member propagation and R0 mean communication, with the same graph/operator, member architecture, actual pre-message normalization, visibility, private updates, loss, serving pool and comparable optimization opportunities. Full R=M-1 must recover exact messages at fixed inputs; separately trained arms need not share their trajectories. For a learned/data-adaptive R1 claim, use a same-rank fixed centered basis and a matched paid-basis alternative; the balanced vector (1,1,-1,-1)/2 is exactly two group means. These distinguish extra transported rank from learning a useful orientation.

An equal-propagation-budget ordinary alternative, such as two exact width-d members for M4/R1, tests whether retaining four private trajectories earns its additional dense work and storage. Equal edge channels do not imply equal parameters, total work or memory; report these separately. Keep rank, selection and any quality-retention criterion prospective and charge all stages. No dataset, threshold, fit matrix or tuning budget is adopted in this packet.

If R1 fails prospectively declared retention against exact, or does not improve the quality/cost tradeoff over R0 and competent equal-budget alternatives after projection/basis costs, reject useful rank-one member compression in that declared setting. Matching a fixed basis rejects necessity of learned orientation. Success would establish a useful attributable operating point; it would not rescue a new mechanism or first-use claim.
