## Private-attention 2×2 and anchor reuse

The planned shared/private-local-scorer × COMMON/ROUTE table is a coherent full-training extension question. Private-minus-shared within each column tests the total ownership/optimization intervention. The difference of those gains tests whether ROUTE-versus-COMMON utility changes with ownership. Similar gains in both columns favor generic attention capacity/optimization; a positive interaction favors this specific combination, not automatically factual topology or a new attention principle.

Reusing six completed Context9 anchors is scientifically valid **only** when the baseline source/recipe remains equivalent apart from the declared ownership intervention, with the same targets, graph/roles, initialization convention, view/RNG policy, loss reductions, optimizer/native schedule, selector, serving and terminal custody. Copy native scorer vectors after reset; do not warm-start the new fits from old selected states. Preserve all seeds and the existing PERMUTED evidence. Historical starting-tensor identity must not be asserted if it was not recorded: matched recipe/seed pairing is the defensible statement. Invalid reuse leaves the table unqualified rather than licensing replacement of old scores.

Ownership changes gradient aggregation and Adam histories as well as forward freedom. It is not a pure capacity experiment or an equal-gradient comparison. Do not compensate with an unplanned learning-rate/gradient-scale search. Charge complete route work and extra parameters/moments. Direct12's negative result makes the final-head route unpromising under its fixed recipe; it is not positive evidence for private attention. First interpret the current complete families. A current success should receive its competent references/confirmation before expansion; failure does not automatically admit the attention rescue.

## At most two additional comparisons needed for stronger claims

1. **Unused split/task confirmation against capable references.** Before a general quality claim, compare the prospectively frozen candidate with capable single/ordinary and a relevant qualified efficient/diverse-ensemble reference on unused evaluation roles. The candidate, selector, budget and comparison must be fixed before scoring. An unused official split tests split variation on the same graph; a broader cross-graph claim needs an unused task/graph. The current three seeds do neither. This is already required by the frozen protocol, not a new pilot arm.

2. **Matched PERMUTED targets in the private-attention row, only for a factual-context claim about that extension.** The existing PERMUTED result has shared scorers. A later positive ownership×target interaction can arise because private capacity uses any persistent heterogeneous targets better. To claim factual signature incidence drives the new architecture's gain requires ROUTE versus the same fixed PERMUTED control under that new ownership. The six-fit 2×2 is adequate for its narrower interaction question; do not silently transfer shared-row factual attribution to private scorers or add this comparator automatically.

No other new pilot comparison is demanded here. In particular, practical comparison against the prepared untied Qm reference need not be promoted into a causal theory that sharing uniquely makes target partitioning work. The current design does not identify that stronger claim. Target concentration, temporal association and feature-only X versus graph-filter contexts also keep the causal interpretation narrow.
