The reader does not export strict-correct masks for every noncommon pooled error. `Union minus common` can include ties, and different negatives can require different members. The present result supports a concrete common-error bottleneck and limited label-informed selection potential; it does not establish that a new routing or weighting rule will work. No novelty or method-specific superiority follows.
