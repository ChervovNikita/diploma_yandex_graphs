# What a rejected source-steering step establishes

9 October 2026. Root's analytical limitation of the inactive proposal. No model, dataset, prediction, scientific outcome or frozen source was accessed or changed.

The proposed correction must preserve every member's full-TRAIN own risk relative to the state **after** the ordinary update. This protects member competence on that population. It can also prevent a useful change before the source objective is properly tested.

An exact example shows why. Let one protected risk near the reference state be `R(v) = R(0) + ||v||²/2`, and let the source objective have a nonzero gradient there. The protected risk's gradient is zero, so its first-order cone constraint permits every direction. Nevertheless, the finite condition `R(v) <= R(0)` permits only `v=0`. Every nonzero source-steering trial is rejected, even if its first-order source objective decreases. This is ordinary local curvature, not a numerical defect or a proof that the source objective lacks information.

The example proves a limitation of the declared TRAIN safeguard. It does not show that actual GNNM training reaches such a point, that a competence tradeoff improves heldout predictions, or that the proposed source objective works. A strict local minimum of a protected private-block risk is a sufficient obstruction; a zero gradient alone is not.

Therefore retain the exact declared policy, but report zero projections, finite guard rejections, accepted corrections and accepted cumulative dose for every seed. An improvement with no accepted correction cannot be attributed to steering. Frequent rejection rejects the usefulness of **this constrained update**, while leaving a different risk-budget policy untested. Do not quietly loosen guards or add attempts after seeing outcomes.

One possible future amendment could let a correction spend part of the risk decrease supplied by the preceding ordinary update, with bounds and all pre-update measurement work fixed in advance. It would change the method and require a separately registered comparison. It is not implemented, admitted or a rescue arm for the current proposal. Any such policy must retain the absent-pool safeguard: decreasing supplied-minus-absent risk while worsening the absent reference can otherwise manufacture a source-credit gain.

These are elementary constrained-optimization observations applied to the proposed update. They are not a new theorem-priority claim, a generalization guarantee or evidence of an accuracy advantage.
