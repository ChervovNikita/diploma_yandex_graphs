## Scientific blind spots

1. **Aggregate AUC does not identify useful complementary evidence.** V2 reports every member's AUC/BCE and O→I pool pair transitions, but not candidate member pair coverage on the frozen O common-inversion cohort. It therefore cannot distinguish coverage acquired and successfully served from coverage acquired and lost by pooling. The completed Wiki24 analysis found 315 newly covered baseline common-competitor instances but only 128 served repairs; that distinction matters, although its numbers and multiclass node mechanism cannot be transferred to molecular AUC.

2. **The relevant common-error barrier is a same-pair ranking statement.** For a fixed positive-negative pair, let `d_m=z_m(pos)-z_m(neg)`. If every O member has `d_m<0`, any fixed positive convex mean remains inverted in exact arithmetic. Repairs require a changed member pair ranking under the current uniform pool; threshold accuracy, unanimous labels and global member AUC do not identify the barrier. Float32 near-tie disagreements must remain explicit. Candidate coverage here is an unavailable pair oracle, not deployable screening performance.

3. **Relative logit scales can improve the pool without new member ordering.** Positive scaling and translation preserve each member's ROC ranking. Different member scales can nevertheless change the mean-logit pool's ordering on pairs where members disagree. Unchanged aggregate member AUC is weaker than unchanged full pair ordering. A pooled AUC gain alone therefore does not establish acquired graph evidence. Conversely, a repaired strict O common inversion requires an individual ranking change, apart from rounding boundaries. No graph-response or causal evidence-use diagnostic is supplied.

4. **BCE is not a calibration certificate.** For mean-logit pooling, the member pool gradient on an example is the same residual `(sigmoid(mean(z))-y)/M`, pulled back through different member Jacobians; this is not Wiki's mean-probability CE responsibility. Convexity also gives theoretical pool BCE ≤ mean member BCE for exact logit averaging. A larger BCE aggregation gap can reflect greater logit spread while absolute member and served quality worsen. Lower BCE can reflect an improved confidence/intercept with unchanged ranks. V2 has no PR-AUC, Brier score, reliability curve, calibration-in-the-large or class-stratified BCE.

5. **ROC-AUC and rare-positive screening quality answer different questions.** ROC ranks all positive-negative pairs; PR depends on prevalence and precision among highly ranked candidates. Actual positive/negative counts are available after closure, but were not inspected here. Under scaffold shift, selected development prevalence, score calibration and difficult chemical groups can change. Neither high ROC nor low overall BCE proves good positive-class precision or calibrated probabilities on new scaffolds. Threshold selection or post-hoc calibration on these same development labels would be a new intervention and is outside this reader.

6. **Cohort repairs must retain their population weight.** V2 reports credit gains/harms and normalizes each cohort by its own pair count. A large common-inversion cohort repair fraction can have a small whole-population contribution. The primary full-population net repair remains `(gained−lost)/N_all_pairs`; report the cohort's same-denominator contribution beside its within-cohort rate, and retain harms outside the frozen cohort. Candidate-defined errors must not redefine O membership.

7. **Own-gradient permissions do not guarantee competence preservation.** Excluding pool gradients from shared/body boundary blocks does not freeze their future own-gradient trajectories: changed internal factors change activations and subsequent losses. Wiki24 demonstrated that coverage and pool-minus-member gain can rise while mean/worst member and served quality fall. Member mean, minimum and every paired member change must accompany net pool utility. O/I is the closer allocation intervention; I versus plain independent4 is a practical recipe comparison with different sharing and selection semantics.

8. **The statistical unit remains three optimizer seeds on one selected scaffold development split.** Pairs share molecules; members share fitting data/body; molecules share chemical structures/scaffolds. None is an independent model replicate. The df2 interval is exploratory and unstable; it is not independent-scaffold uncertainty. The same development labels selected checkpoints and evaluate these diagnoses. Six contrasts are not confirmatory evidence. A positive mean against both single and ordinary independent4 permits a separately frozen independent-evidence stage; it does not establish generalization, novelty or scientific success. P/G matching I leaves the restriction's added value undemonstrated.
