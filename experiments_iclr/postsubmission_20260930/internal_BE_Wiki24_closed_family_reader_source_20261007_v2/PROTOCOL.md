## Stage1: explicitly charged trusted metadata extraction

`extract_selected_metadata.py` is a distinct root-authorized CPU stage. The frozen
runner embeds official joint `selected_VALID`, `member_VALID`, selected epoch and
serving mode in `selected.pt`; there is no standalone joint scalar export. Source
checkpoints also contain NumPy RNG state, hence explicit trusted
`torch.load(map_location='cpu', weights_only=False)` in the original pinned runtime
is required. Exact source/jobs/data/closure/selected hashes establish trust before
deserialization. Tensor model/optimizer contents are incidentally deserialized
and discarded: this is not a metadata-only byte parser. No Ensemble construction,
`load_state_dict`, forward, dataset payload, optimizer step or selector executes.
No generic arbitrary-pickle input is accepted.

Ordinary `independent4` is kept separate: its source-selected evaluation-only
assembled bank `selected.pt` supplies pooled accuracy and per-body serving flags;
`CLOSED_OWN_BEST_BANK.json` supplies final official member accuracy; the four
`own_best_m.pt` supply independently selected member epochs and saved accuracy/
mode. Earlier own-saved member accuracy and later final-bank member accuracy are
distinct evaluation events. Both exact values, their final-bank-minus-own-saved
delta, member epochs/modes and checkpoint binding are retained in
`own_selected_member_diagnostics`; no equality between these metrics is required.
A discrepancy is diagnostic and does not discard official final-bank scores or
trigger further parity work. Final `CLOSED_OWN_BEST_BANK.json` member metrics remain
`member_VALID` table authority. Serving-mode/source integrity guards remain; pooled
accuracy equality between `selected.pt` and bank JSON from the same original final
evaluation remains a metadata consistency check. This bank is not a jointly selected epoch
and is not a coherent optimizer resume. Each other arm reads the original joint
selected checkpoint's pooled/member accuracy and single epoch. No CLOSED_TRACE
is opened or searched for a maximum. No rounded score, late tie, new selection
rule or hidden complete-case selection is introduced.

Native FREEZE authenticates `selected.pt`, not the four own files or own-bank
JSON separately. Their hashes are reader-recorded after closure, and source/mode
metadata is cross-checked with the selected bank; metric differences remain diagnostics.
This limited hash authority is
explicit in the export. It does not claim source-time own-file digest custody.

Outputs are SAVED_METADATA_GATE.json, METADATA_EXPORT.json and
EXTRACTION_COST.json. The cost receipt includes all elapsed/processCPU time from

## Interpretation

All metrics are full official split0 VALID accuracy from the source float32 mean.
VALID selected checkpoints over1100 epochs and the local transition; selected
scores have selection optimism. Three optimizer seeds share the graph and split.
Seed dispersion/df2 intervals are exploratory, not graph/split/query-population
uncertainty. There is no iid-node bootstrap, TEST/generalization estimate,
multiplicity correction, confirmation-seed evidence or quality/novelty claim.
Pool-minus-mean-member accuracy alone does not establish shared-error repair or
causal useful diversity. A separately authorized selected-prediction collector
may consume the gated metadata export without changing these metrics/selectors.
