The proposed normalized local model

 p(Y,S_L,S_R|x) = (1/M) sum_m p_m(Y|x) q_Lm(S_L|x) q_Rm(S_R|x)

implies p(Y|x,S_L,S_R)=sum_m rho_m p_m(Y|x), with rho_m=q_Lm q_Rm / sum_j q_Lj q_Rj, whenever the denominator is positive. This is ordinary latent-mixture conditioning, not a new theorem or mixture principle. It pools normalized label probabilities; a sigmoid of mean raw ranking logits is a different predictor. Native negative-sampled ranking training does not automatically calibrate population link probabilities. If structure is unobserved and marginalized, the normalized q factors integrate to one and the predictor is the uniform mean. Thus posterior serving changes the conditioning/served object relative to the stored auxiliary-removal design.

Separate mean-member CE plus a structure-only auxiliary is a different objective; it does not inherit joint-likelihood estimation guarantees.
