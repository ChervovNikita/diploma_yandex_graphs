Acknowledgments and other remarks
The experiments were conducted using PyTorch (Paszke et al. (2017)). The authors would like to thank
Facebook, CIFAR, Calcul Quebec and Compute Canada, for research funding and computational
resources.
Concurrently to our work, Liu et al. (2018) also proposed to use the KFE in order to improve the
elastic weight consolidation (Kirkpatrick et al., 2017) regularization strategy for continual learning.
Code EKFAC can be experimented with in PyTorch using the NNGeometry library (George, 2021)
available at https://github.com/tfjgeorge/nngeometry.
