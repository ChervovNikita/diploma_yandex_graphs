1. Eq2 and scaling. losses.py concatenates independently F.normalize'd a and b
within each member. Every anchor has all 2B-1 nonself candidates; its positive
set is every same-label position except its exact identity, with cardinality
2*n_class-1. The other view of the same object remains positive, including for
singleton classes. The scalar is mean_{member,anchor}[logsumexp(nonself scores)
- mean_{positive}(score)], exactly Eq2's outside-log uniform positive averaging.
It averages over M*2B anchors, uses tau=.2, and preserves F.normalize's default
epsilon. Raw scores must be finite. Masked -infinity is confined to logsumexp;
the positive numerator multiplies finite raw scores, avoiding 0*(-infinity).
There is no Eq3 positive logsumexp, detached view, or cross-member score.

2. Both-view gradients and recipients. Both normalized views remain live in
the anchor and candidate roles. The unchanged replay obtains cotangents jointly
for (la,ha,lb,hb), then propagates logits and representation cotangents together
through each member/view graph. Eight VJPs accumulate before one Adam update.
The captured representation is the active native task head's input, so auxiliary
gradients reach upstream shared/private parameters, including stems; that head
receives own CE only. There is no new projector or recipient rule.

3. Matched inherited contract. All three recompute.py copies are byte-identical.
The exact original device-side linspace(0,579,steps=512).long() panel is retained.
Own CE uses all 580 TRAIN labels in both views, with .5 view averaging and mean
member scaling; total=own.mean()+.05*auxiliary+.05*differentiable_zero_residual.
The successor make_session AST equals public attribution's. The same sealed

(lr=.001, weight_decay=0, eps=1e-8), and persistent member RNG streams. Replay
restores starting streams, checks exact shadow/replay endpoints and unchanged
