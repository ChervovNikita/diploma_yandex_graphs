## What the new diagnostic distinguishes

A strict common rival is one false class above truth in every member's raw scores; members need not share their top class. Unchanged, nonnegative scalar probability mixing cannot reverse that ordering under exact monotone probabilities. The current banks' zero served corrections on their own rival masks agree with that constraint. It restricts serving unchanged vectors, not learned class transformations or the information in hidden states.

Rival removal is not a repair. E removes 316 BASE rival predicates but correctly serves only 79 old BASE-rival readouts; it introduces 451 new rivals. Against I4, 436 removals include 122 served old-rival repairs, with 361 introductions. E can change the wrong ranking without acquiring or serving truth. Its 2,064 common-rival errors and 815 correct-member alternatives lost in pooling are different remaining problems.
