## Algebra disposes of equivalent recipes without scientific fits

For finite binary logits and an offset independent of η, `grad_eta CE(z+a,y) = stopgrad(alpha) grad_eta CE(z,y)`, with `alpha=(1-p_adjusted(y))/(1-p_ctx(y))`. With `r=p_null`, the loss itself is plain context CE. These are reductions, not empirical rival hypotheses. Future numerical equivalence checking qualifies an implementation; training both forms does not supply an extra control or novelty evidence. Fixed `1-r(y)` weights are different and unmatched; they are excluded from a contrast-novelty test.
