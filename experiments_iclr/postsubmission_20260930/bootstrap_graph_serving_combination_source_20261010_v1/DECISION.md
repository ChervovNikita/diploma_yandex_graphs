# Bootstrap acquisition and graph serving decision

10 October 2026. Admit one finite diagnostic on closed WikiCS development banks, with zero new GNN training. Bootstrap has acquired correct alternatives that native averaging discards; the closed graph scorer has served some discarded alternatives on other banks. This is enough to test their interaction once. It does not reopen either failed superiority recipe or erase the bootstrap member competence failure.

These distinct facts remain binding.

## Decisive readout and protection

For every block and backbone, report A−C, B−C, A+B−C, A+B−A and A+B−B with all three seed deltas. Measure exact C/A/B/A+B repairs, harms, unique/shared repairs, repair survival, new combined repairs, and combined harms where both ingredients were correct. Separately count newly covered bootstrap nodes served by A+B, newly covered nodes still lost, removed alternatives, losses introduced among previously served nodes, aggregation-only correctness and common strict rival cases. Graph weighting cannot acquire a member alternative or repair a common strict wrong rival in exact arithmetic.

Keep the original graph materiality criteria: A+B versus B native at least +0.2 pp mean; versus each fitted B control and equally graph-processed ordinary/factorized I4 at least +0.1 pp mean; all three seed deltas nonnegative and at least two positive. Add A+B versus equally graph-processed own unweighted A at least +0.1 pp mean with the same seed conditions, so a general graph gain cannot be credited to bootstrap. Report each block and require the complete declared roster for a whole-roster serving claim. Compare M1 and original shared endpoints as capable context.

Preserve NLL protection separately against every fitted B control, own unweighted graph A and both graph-processed I4 banks: mean deterioration at most 0.02 and every seed at most 0.05. Do not promote positive interaction when final accuracy or proper risk fails. Bootstrap member competence protection is already false; these heads leave members unchanged, so they cannot retroactively make that flag pass. Retain a true pooled accuracy and NLL protection win if it occurs, with the member failure reported separately. Weak individual members can form a useful ensemble; the measured member gate is not a theorem forbidding final pooled superiority or a requirement that each member beat I4.

Refutation is explicit: the scorer fails to retain the acquired alternatives, introduces offsetting harms, adds no material gain beyond the unweighted graph cell, or remains below equally processed capable references. Close the fixed diagnostic after its complete readout; any next mechanism needs new evidence rather than a rescue grid.

## Ancestry cost and claim scope

The acquisition ingredient uses class-normalized Exp(1) Bayesian-bootstrap weights in private coordinates with an ordinary shared gradient. Bootstrapped DQN already combines a shared trunk, private heads and bootstrap weighting, including Exp(1) masks in Appendix B; BatchEnsemble supplies shared matrices with private factors. The existing graph reliability scorer is an attributed learned convex mixture. This diagnostic claims no new principle.

The 36 new four-member banks require 900 small CPU fold fits and 450000 declared updates. There are zero new GNN training acquisitions; export and readout have real additional cost. The preceding 855-endpoint head study took approximately 1182 head-fitting seconds; comparable conditions suggest roughly 20–25 minutes for this diagnostic, with export and host contention additional. This is a planning estimate, not a speed result. Root retains its existing owner and native interfaces, verifies support, exports, executes, joins and freezes the result.

Checkpoint selection and prior diagnosis already used all VALID labels. Aggregator OOF is encountered development, not whole-pipeline cross-fitting or unused confirmation. Three seeds describe optimization repeats on one connected graph. Any manuscript promotion still needs a frozen whole pipeline, capable references and unused confirmation.
