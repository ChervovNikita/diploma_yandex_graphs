## The controls limit the interpretation

A larger unanimity contrast does not mean sharing produces more graph-local wrong pooled answers. Shared pooled wrong-class recurrence excess averages22.139%, versus23.122% independent: shared-minus-independent is−0.983 percentage points and is not uniformly positive across splits. Mean wrong-member-fraction excess is22.349% versus22.099%, a difference of only+0.249 points with mixed splitwise signs. The single also has substantial recurrence. Every pool/member-fraction/single primary comparison is retained in SUMMARY and ALL_PRIMARY_METRICS; complete other strata are preserved.

Thus the result describes stronger unanimous agreement among shared members around a common mistake, within this narrow population. It does not establish that the shared model spreads wrong classes through the graph more than the independent model, identify a graph-specific causal defect, or explain the entire accuracy gap. Generic error overlap alone remains inadequate justification for a new graph loss. This diagnostic can guide a candidate that preserves member competence while creating corrective information, but neither novelty nor a new training cohort is admitted from its sign.

## Limits and next decision

Original selected VALID labels are reused for targets, anchors, controls and matching. The three masks overlap on one graph; neighbors and reused controls are dependent. Same-class conditioning omits heterophilous edges, matching is coarse and only393/391/386 primary targets have matches. No iid significance, causal exchangeability, unseen-graph generalization or serving correction is asserted. Full unsupported cells remain undefined rather than zero.

The predeclared private-learning pilot remains a separate test, with capable single/untied and derivative/task controls. No original paper score changes. A new training intervention must identify corrective signal unavailable to arbitrary hidden repulsion or member selection, cite its antecedents, and beat equally treated competent references before confirmation.
