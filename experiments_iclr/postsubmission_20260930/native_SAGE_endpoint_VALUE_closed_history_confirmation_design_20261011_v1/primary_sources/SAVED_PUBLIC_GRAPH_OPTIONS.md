## Complete task protocols

### Flickr

**Input and target.** Nodes are Flickr images. The GraphSAINT paper describes an undirected graph linking images with shared properties such as location, gallery or commenting users. Inputs are released 500-dimensional image bag-of-words features from NUS-wide. The authors merge 81 source tags into **seven exclusive classes**; this is not 81-label prediction. PyG documents **89,250 nodes and 899,756 stored edge entries**. The complete published split is 50/25/25, supplied by `role.json`; exact integer cardinalities await authorized acquisition. [F1–F3]

**Native exposure rule.** GraphSAINT trains on `adj_train`, whose nonzeros connect two training nodes, and evaluates with `adj_full` on the complete graph. Its scaler is fitted on training nodes appearing in `adj_train`; the released implementation uses that definition rather than every node in `role['tr']`. Preserve it for faithful references and record any isolated training nodes. PyG's Flickr loader downloads **only `adj_full`**, exposes all features, and creates official masks. Using its full graph for training would be a different transductive variant. For the recommended task, preserve native training-node exposure for every arm and full-graph validation/inference. Deriving training adjacency from official role IDs is possible but must be checked against the provider's `adj_train` after authorized acquisition. [F3–F5]

**Edges, normalization and metric.** Retain provider adjacency entries; do not add a second reverse edge to an already undirected representation. The provider loader adds no self-loops or feature normalization. Native GraphSAINT uses row normalization and sampling/loss corrections, while its model has separate self/neighbor paths; a normalization comment mentions self-connections but the inspected `adj_norm` implementation does not add them. Freeze actual model-specific loop and degree rules. Native primary metric is **micro-F1**, which equals accuracy for seven-way single-label argmax predictions. Use softmax classification, not independent BCE or a multilabel threshold. [F3, F6–F9]

**Strong controls.** Retain competent full-training-graph GCN and mean GraphSAGE, the authors' **GraphSAINT random-walk reference** with its sampler normalization and full-graph inference, and a feature-only MLP. The pinned native Flickr RW configuration uses hidden dimension 256, concatenation, softmax loss, architecture `1-1-0`, and 6,000 roots and walk depth 2; it must not be called reproduced from a YAML alone. Charge sampling preparation and full validation/inference to its costs. A sampled training algorithm can still use the complete official task; a permanently reduced benchmark graph cannot substitute for it. [F7–F8]

### Coauthor Physics

**Input and target.** Authors are linked when they coauthor a paper; features are binary paper-keyword indicators and labels are the author's most active research field. PyG documents **34,493 nodes, 495,924 directed edge entries, 8,415 features and five classes**. The primary paper reports **247,962 undirected edges** for its complete largest connected component, consistent with the two-direction provider count. Physics tests a wide, sparse scientific input and low-label transductive learning. [C1–C3]

**Graph and split.** The paper uses undirected largest connected components, 20 training and 30 validation labels per class, with all remaining nodes as test. For Physics that implies 100 training and 150 validation labels if all five classes are retained. PyG supplies no masks. Its NPZ reader densifies binary features, removes self-loops and coalesces undirected edges, but does **not** select the largest component or normalize features. Use the complete paper-defined component and preserve its node mapping; verify that its counts match the source metadata rather than silently assuming the full archive is identical. Generate all prospective masks using the author's class-sampling rule and fixed RNGs under a steward, and save every mask before fitting. There is no released canonical official split to claim. [C2–C5, C7]

**Metric and controls.** Primary metric is multiclass accuracy; predictive cross-entropy can accompany it. Native controls are **GCN, GAT and mean GraphSAGE** under the benchmark's common preparation and model-selection rules, plus feature-only MLP/logistic regression and training-label-only label propagation. APPNP or GPR-GNN is a useful stronger propagation reference if separately pinned and specified. Dense raw features are about 1.08 GiB in float32, so raw-input projection and feature preparation costs matter even though the edge count is modest. The source benchmark used 100 random masks and 20 initializations per mask; a smaller prospective study cannot claim a faithful replication of that evaluation. Masks and seeds remain repetitions on one graph. [C2–C6]

**Independence limit.** Physics and CS have different corpora, field classes and sizes, but come from the same Microsoft Academic and KDD construction. Using both would provide replication within scientific collaboration, not two additional domains. Physics is preferable here because CS already has a local source-only proposal.

### Penn94

**Input and target.** This is a 2005 university Facebook friendship graph with **41,554 nodes**, a published edge count of **1,362,229**, and two reported-gender classes. The paper describes five profile attributes. The released benchmark and PyG loaders actually select `local_info[:, :1]` plus `local_info[:, 2:]`, thereby retaining status in addition to the other non-target metadata and removing the gender column. **Do not copy a feature width from another implementation:** the author uses `label_binarize`, whereas PyG uses full `one_hot`, which can differ for binary-valued columns. The canonical encoded dimension must be verified after acquisition. The paper's five raw attributes are not five float neural-network inputs. [L1–L3, L6]

**Edges and split.** Both loaders read all adjacency nonzeros; the native runner coalesces to an undirected graph. The paper's edge-count convention is insufficient to certify the final stored nonzeros without loading, so budget conservatively for up to **2,724,458 entries** and later record the exact canonical workload. Keep all 41,554 graph nodes, including nodes with missing gender (`y=-1`), for transductive message passing; exclude negative targets from every supervised split and metric. Use the published **five fixed 50/25/25 train/validation/test splits of eligible labeled nodes**, not a fresh advantageous mask. The exact labeled-node and split counts have not been read. PyG supplies Penn94 masks, and the pinned author repository tree confirms the raw matrix and split-file paths exist. [L3–L6]

**Metric and controls.** Primary metric is **accuracy**, with multiclass NLL/CE under the native runner. Required strong topology controls are **LINK and LINKX**, plus feature-only MLP. LINK predicts from adjacency; LINKX separately embeds adjacency and features, so ordinary neighbor mean alone is an inadequate native reference. Add GPR-GNN or H2GCN as a heterophily-aware propagation reference and competent GCN/SAGE. Two-hop methods need their own memory accounting. LINKX's node-identity-sized adjacency projection is valid in this transductive setting; it does not directly generalize to unseen node IDs or prove inductive communication savings. The native `evaluate` function calculates test metrics on each call; a future confirmation wrapper must expose validation only until all checkpoint identities are locked. [L7–L9]

**Why reserve it.** This graph is denser than Physics and has a low reported adjusted homophily measure in the primary paper. It probes whether useful member communication survives a graph where simple topology-feature embeddings are already strong. It is a distinct corpus from Flickr and coauthorship. Reservation is conditional on confirming that no prior project outcome is known; public test labels cannot be cryptographically sealed by this metadata exercise.

### genius

**Input and target.** The complete graph contains **421,961 users**, a published raw edge count of **984,979**, **12 usage features** and two classes. Features include expertise score, contributions and user roles. The task predicts whether an account is marked **“gone”**, approximately 20% of the dataset in the primary paper; it is not expertise classification and does not supply verified spam ground truth. Follow relations are the source edges, but the benchmark treats genius as **undirected**. PyG preserves the raw edge array, so explicit native symmetrization/coalescing is necessary. Budget at most 1,969,958 entries before loops until the actual duplicate count is established. [L1, L10–L11]

**Split, metric and controls.** Use the same five published 50/25/25 splits. The author repository tree includes `genius-splits.npy`; **PyG's genius loader has no masks**, so do not use a new random split by accident. Native primary metric is **ROC-AUC** because of class imbalance. The author runner uses a two-output one-hot BCE convention and evaluates class 1 softmax probability for ROC-AUC. A one-logit binary CE port is an adaptation that needs identical treatment across arms and a locked evaluator; ROC-AUC is not interchangeable with accuracy, AP or threshold F1. Retain LINK/LINKX, feature-only MLP, GPR-GNN and competent GCN/SAGE. [L4–L9, L11, L13–L14]

genius adds a much larger node-state workload and a different target, but overlaps Penn94's online-social-network domain. It should be a separately fixed fourth task or an alternative designated before any development/confirmation outcome. It cannot replace a failed confirmation graph after scores are exposed.

## One A100 planning arithmetic

The following is **metadata arithmetic**, using four members, width 128 and three propagation layers to make task scale comparable. It is not a selected architecture, a measured VRAM requirement, GPU-hour estimate or speed claim. One stored node state is `4*N*M*d` bytes. An explicitly materialized edge-by-width tensor is `4*E*M*d`; fused sparse aggregation can avoid it. Traversals are `E*M*3` for one complete forward. All omit backward, validation, normalization, feature projection, optimizer state, autograd copies and allocator reserve.

| Complete graph | Planning edge entries | Raw float32 feature MiB | One four-member node-state MiB | One explicit edge-width tensor GiB | Forward edge-layer-member traversals |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Flickr full inference | 899,756 | 170.2 | 174.3 | 1.72 | 10,797,072 |
| Coauthor Physics provider size | 495,924 | 1,107.2 | 67.4 | 0.95 | 5,951,088 |
| Penn94 conservative bound | 2,724,458 | `4*N*D/2^20`; D pending | 81.2 | 5.20 | 32,693,496 |
| genius conservative bound | 1,969,958 | 19.3 | 824.1 | 3.76 | 23,639,496 |

These sizes support the **inference** that modest-width sparse full-graph controls are plausible on the authorized one A100 80GB. Penn94 can be costly if edge messages are materialized, and Physics can be costly in the wide first dense layer. genius carries roughly 0.8 GiB for each four-member node state. H2GCN two-hop expansion, high-width attention and cached wide feature powers have no justified memory bound from this table. No multi-GPU request is warranted by the metadata. Actual complete-training-graph and complete-inference resource checks must precede an eventual launch; use the same execution exposure and charge all preparation costs. Flickr training edges are fewer than its complete inference graph, but their exact count remains unknown.
