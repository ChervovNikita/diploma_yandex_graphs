Unrestricted heads acquire **zero excess counts of newly correct alternatives** on the old all-wrong population and zero excess pooled repairs on the old all-wrong/common-rival population in every seed. Full BE does repair some native errors. But its full-population harms exceed repairs in every seed, giving net changes−54,−92,−22 nodes versus native. Mean/worst member accuracy shows no compensating full-specific competence improvement. Pooled NLL changes against factor heads are mixed and small; both BE refits have roughly1.63 mean NLL versus about1.18 for native unit+contrast. Neither a tiny averaged NLL difference nor a few retained-coverage nodes rescues a useful head-freedom claim. Refitted ordinary and single controls also underperform their corresponding native accuracy/NLL references, so the general fixed refitting/regularization recipe is not a demonstrated quality improvement.

## What remains unresolved

All three **full BE** endpoints meet the declared convergence criterion. Their negligible predictive advantage does not depend on tightening the factorized endpoints. Nonetheless, the retained factor/ordinary nonconvergence and nonconvex factor parameterization prevent a claim about global factor-family optima or strict predictive capacity. Leave those receipts and endpoints unchanged.

The result rejects the proposed opportunity under this fixed feature scaling, class-centered penalty, own-CE objective and readout training procedure. It does not establish that frozen hidden states contain no useful information, that every head recipe would fail, or that a whole-network private head can never help. Indeed, both refit families can create some newly correct decisions; they simply do not retain enough quality globally, and unrestricted freedom creates no additional correct alternatives on the diagnosed cohort.

The original missing-correct-evidence problem remains. This study does not locate it in the final diagonal head, prove an upstream feature deficit, or certify a graph/attention mechanism. Different upstream training and selected representations could change the result, but there is no evidence here to launch that extension. Three seeds on the same repeatedly selected development split remain descriptive evidence, not independent generalization confirmation.
