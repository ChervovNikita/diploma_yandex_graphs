rest of the results and plots appear in SI, section V. (b–d) The Area Under the Precision curve (AUP) summarises the general performance for each

Ontology (GO) performance robustness (PR) is computed as the minimum AUP among the networks and used for comparison between CAR and FSW.
(f) In-silico STRING validation: the first 100 best interactions for each method are tested in STRING. The minimum number of verified-interactions
among the three networks represents the minimum precision, which is a measure of performance robustness (PR) for comparison - with regard to
STRING - between CAR and FSW.
