## What the action measurement excludes—and leaves open

The exact native witness uses bias-free incidence output signs constant within each matrix row: a discrete edge-stalk symmetry that leaves cross/degree Grams, normalized `L` and the complete evaluation predictor unchanged in real arithmetic. Measuring the returned normalized operator's action avoids treating that parameter symmetry, or the detached unnormalized `.L` cache, as deployed diversity. It does not prove every input-factor direction is free of nuisance effects. It also avoids the basis-energy counterexample where right feature maps are zero and `L_off V` vanishes.

Remaining limitations are material:

- A large action angle can be dominated by channels invisible to the current head; a tiny visible component can satisfy the `>1e−6` floor. The gates do not measure what fraction of the action difference reaches the decision.
- Classifier visibility can arise through self/diagonal terms while the off-node difference occurs elsewhere. Passing both gates does not connect the visible margin change causally to neighbor action.
- `V`, the operator and the shared weights change during continuation. Initial diversity may disappear, or may merely align feature coordinates. It is not an irreducible connection/holonomy certificate for general NSD maps.
- A small warm TRAIN-NLL increase does not guarantee final own-member competence, useful complementary errors, or improved restored pooling after400 epochs. TRAIN-only selection can overfit the screen population.
