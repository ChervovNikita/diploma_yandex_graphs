## Procedures and required controls

There are five construction geometries: ordinary M1, unit-equipped M1,
first-stem-Rademacher equipped M1, unit shared4 and first-stem-Rademacher shared4.
Every geometry has native own CE, within-route SupCon, paired own CE, paired
class-full alignment and canonical paired SupCon Eq2. M1 geometries also have
the stronger native mean-four-dropout own control. Shared4 geometries additionally
have GNCL, GNCL+within-route SupCon and paired alignment+live residual InfoNCE.

That is **34 separately searched procedures**, comprising18 M1 procedures and
16 shared4 procedures. Each M1 procedure has a genuine I4 served variant from
four separately paid bodies, four construction/private-stream bindings and four
own selectors. Its singleton reference is the prospectively fixed body0 of that
same group, never an outcome-selected best member. All18 I4 variants inherit
their matched M1 search choice without fourfold HPO or a pooled checkpoint
selector. The source's M1 residual is zero; paired class-full M1/I4 is the
effective control for shared residual procedures. GNCL's M1 pool coefficient is
analytically ineffective and receives no duplicate search cell.

Native own procedures retain separate old-state CE backward calls followed by
one Adam update. Scalar callbacks retain one scalar backward/update. The paired
own control fixes alignment0 and temperature.2, representing exactly the two
live supervised views with zero auxiliary. Its ineffective temperature is not
tuned. Factor seeds and private stream identities are declared, not HPO axes.
Ordinary/equipped mean-four-dropout has four factual private view streams and
one serving prediction. Shared4 has four factor routes and one physical body.

All actual constructors use the pinned architecture and group parameters by
names containing `attnmodule` versus all others. The attention/ordinary learning
rate ratio and decay pair follow the source-scoped recipe. Adam, betas(.9,.999),
epsilon1e-8 and native constant scheduling are preserved. No boundary original

## Author reproduction status

Author command defaults, maximum2000, patience250, strict first validation accuracy
selection and ten fixed split seeds are verified from pinned script bytes. The
homophilic author scripts use60/20/20 random class-balanced TRAIN splits, while
the real standardized custodies use public/low-label roles and separate CAL.
Author training reads TEST every epoch and retains module/global RNG behavior
and transitive imports omitted by the AST bridge. Provider preprocessing and
published data/split/RNG/termination are not fully reproduced by this adapter.

Therefore fixed exact author reproduction is **blocked**, with **zero author
fits**. The source-native point is an adapted comparison anchor, not evidence
of published replication. The compiler records concrete blockers separately
from runnable standardized cells. There is no protected-unused claim from an
incomplete exposure audit and no manuscript acceptance guarantee.
