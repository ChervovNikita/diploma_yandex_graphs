## Full-node initialization

Retain the full-node graph-error initializer for a representative paired pilot, conditional on source qualification. Training errors are spread through the released graph. Each member then uses this signal to change its private factors. The proposed change keeps the signal at unlabeled output nodes when calculating that factor update. Only TRAIN labels supply the errors.

The exact extra parameter direction before the existing common projection is `J_U^T q_U`. Here `q_U` is the error signal transported to unlabeled output nodes, and `J_U` describes how those output logits change when the private factors change. TRAIN-root backpropagation already passes through unlabeled neighbors. This amendment adds output roots, rather than adding unlabeled ground truth.

The saved literature packet attributes error diffusion, graph filters, tangent pullbacks and corrected-target distillation to prior work. This is a support amendment to the current initializer. It does not yet establish methodological novelty or a predictive advantage.

Prior ingredients do not by themselves invalidate a new composition. A small contribution can consist of a precise graph-conditioned operation, a useful mechanism, and measured benefit against the closest complete operations. It need not invent a new mathematical primitive. The review must distinguish an established equivalent full method from related components, and keep ingredient attribution beside the proposed change. A global proof that no matching publication exists is not a realistic requirement.

The pilot should use complete Squirrel and Photo, the existing modern backbones, three paired seeds and identical warm states. Compare full support, TRAIN-remasked support, full support with permuted topology, and common-only descent. Freeze the private slice, parameter radius, optimizer-state convention, training streams, checkpoint selector and mean-logit pool before any comparison outcome is opened. Choose a common trial step across the paired contrast arms using TRAIN data. Existing branch-specific line searches cannot supply a matched-step comparison by themselves.

The signed finite diagnostic must compare every branch with common descent at the **same step size**. A positive signed diagnostic or a larger private tangent demonstrates construction, not better predictions. Later pooled validation quality against all three controls decides whether to continue. Retain failures and unsuccessful outcomes.
