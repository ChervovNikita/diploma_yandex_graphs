# Predictive hypotheses and resource decisions

Current resources affect when a representative experiment can run. They do not establish whether its hypothesis is false. Known ingredients also do not establish that their complete composition has already been published. Each candidate needs a specific changed operation, its closest complete comparators, a test that can distinguish the proposed mechanism, and an estimate of the resources that test needs.

## Retained support amendment

Training labels create an error signal. Graph filters spread that signal to other output nodes. Pulling the full signal back to private factors includes responses that disappear when those output nodes are remasked to TRAIN. The paired helper keeps common initialization, factor radius, step size and continuation choices fixed across full support, remasking, common descent and altered topology.

This tests whether the additional output roots improve later predictions. It does not assume unlabeled ground truth or promise that their signal helps. The representative development experiment uses the complete Squirrel and Photo graphs with modern native backbones and three paired seeds. Its continuation forecast is about 18 GPU hours, excluding native qualification, initialization and contention. The existing running cohort remains unchanged. A new integration and qualified native closure are still needed.

## Retained covariance selector

The separate head-only proposal starts members with opposite factor changes so their initial average logits are identical. It chooses among three graph-derived covariance allocations according to the average prediction after one cloned cross-entropy update. Trials are discarded, and the selected initialization resumes with the declared original optimizer state.

Cross-entropy provides a mathematical reason that later average predictions can change even when initial average logits do not. The sign can help or harm. An equally selected random span and equally selected altered-topology span are necessary comparators. A lower fitted trial loss is a construction result, not evidence of better unseen predictions. The five-arm development forecast is about 22.52 continuation GPU hours, plus unmeasured qualification and trial work. Exact head affinity and state cloning remain unqualified. This proposal is retained for source assessment, with no predictive or novelty claim.

## Distinct question during training

A separate literature assessment studies whether the shared optimizer displacement erases useful member advantages. Any proposed correction must identify the prediction responses it preserves and compare its complete operation with gradient projection and ensemble-objective priors. This question is distinct from changing only the initial factors. No operation or training run is adopted before that assessment is complete.

## Evidence and resource request

A failed numerical interface rules out that implementation on that interface. A null construction rules out that construction. A failed representative comparison weighs against the tested method in its stated setting. None of those findings warrants a claim about every possible GNNM extension.

Before requesting extra GPUs, save the eligible experiment, required device memory, measured or explicitly assumed cost, and remaining implementation work. If a supported test needs resources beyond the available allocation, report that need and stop its dependent compute. Keep the hypothesis and its scientific assessment recorded while it waits.

Original paper scores remain fixed. No audited new predictive gain or acceptance recommendation exists at this stage.
