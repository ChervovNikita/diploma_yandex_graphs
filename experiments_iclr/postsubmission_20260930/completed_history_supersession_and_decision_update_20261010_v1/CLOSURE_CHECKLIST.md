# Complete-family scientific decision checklist

Use once after every declared family closes. Reuse existing receipts and diagnostic paths. Do not create another audit merely because the result is inconvenient.

1. **Establish what closed.** Name exact frozen roster, successful/failed/missing cells, original direct-wait/absence adoption and immutable result paths. A retained failure is not a fabricated negative score; a closure gap is unknown. Keep excluded allocation lineages excluded.
2. **Open quality only for the admitted scope.** Use all paired blocks and original selected endpoints. Preserve TEST restrictions, original scores and frozen gates. No partial winner selection, seed replacement, shorter fit or after-outcome tolerance.
3. **State comparability before interpretation.** Record graph/split, information/task, architecture, initialization/factors, optimizer/horizon, own versus joint selector, pooling domain and cost ledger. Name the strongest capable single and genuine independent reference. A raw-native or differently selected positive is contextual.
4. **Apply the frozen decision.** Write full seed deltas and required risk/class/macro gates in natural units. Separate practical quality, cost and novelty. A tiny trigger, source approval or engineered tolerance is not superiority.
5. **Diagnose the predictive failure with existing evidence.** Report mean/worst competence; correct-alternative coverage/no-correct; served harm/lost alternatives; actual all-wrong rescues/common rivals. For F1/AUROC/ranking, keep these diagnostic counts separate from the metric rather than claim an additive metric decomposition. Use geometry/gradient activity only for its measured scope.
6. **For A/B/A+B, use actual matched flows.** Cite the existing exact path. Record baseline repairs each ingredient makes, repairs surviving the package and introduced harms. Negative component means can coexist with useful distinct repairs; aggregate counts/positive interaction cannot prove overlap. If joint identities are unavailable, say so—do not reopen fits or call another scalar reader for array-only information.
7. **Choose one action.** Frozen gate fails: close that recipe, identify what exact claim failed, and hold continuation. Positive only versus weak/contextual controls: qualify capable matched references. Positive versus capable matched references: mechanism comparison and unused confirmation precede paper claims. Missing required evidence: name the precise gap and preserve any independently complete contrasts. No renamed grid without a new mechanism or newly available evidence.
8. **Save one decision card and update the history map.** Root owns canonical state/publication; researchers provide the card. Preserve previous conclusions and explicitly name supersessions. Broad revision follows two closures or a changed leading explanation.
