# Complete DBLP development decision

All 35 selected cases passed the root artifact/trace audit; the selected-state replay and original checkpoint bindings are preserved. The unchanged prospective continuation criterion failed against both ordinary global BatchEnsemble and the common relation-conditioned control. CP ACM confirmation is not released. The independent mixed-objective study was fixed before these outcomes and does not depend on a CP pass.

CP minus global BE validation NLL averaged -0.0003424 nats, with three of five strict paired wins. The illustrative paired t interval is [-0.0008640, 0.0001792]; these overlapping graph splits are not independent datasets. CP minus shared relation conditioning averaged +0.0002235 nats, with two of five wins. Both effects fall far short of the prospective 0.005-nat improvement. CP also failed to establish superiority over the unrestricted, wider and independent-member controls. This is not a useful predictive improvement under the tested recipe.

The prospectively prepared saved-logit diagnostic opened only admitted development labels after full-family closure. Relative to global BE, the FP64 accounting identity separates -0.0003012 nats of mean-member NLL change and +0.00004124 nats of ambiguity change. Across five split blocks, CP rescues one global-BE error and harms one global-BE correct decision. Those are repeated split observations on one graph, not independent new-node evidence. The decomposition attributes numbers algebraically; it does not establish a causal mechanism, a new theorem or general uselessness of relation conditioning.

Every unsuccessful result, original failed memory attempt, source revision and checkpoint remains retained. No old benchmark score was changed, no heldout label was opened, and no manuscript acceptance claim follows.
