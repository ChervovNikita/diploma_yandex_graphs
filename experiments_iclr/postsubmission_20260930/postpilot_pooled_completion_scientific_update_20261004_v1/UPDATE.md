# Scientific update: pooled completion challenges private association

4 October 2026. This separate update uses only the four supplied, rounded, one-seed selected-VALID scores. It does not revise the outcome-blind plan or declare another winner. No outcome file, SSH, run or new primary paper was accessed.

## What the finding changes

| Fixed selected-state route | J | F | J minus F |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Private completion | 64.3066% | 64.0903% | +0.2163 percentage points |
| Pooled completion weights | 65.7596% | 64.6212% | +1.1384 percentage points |

Pooling raises J by 1.4530 points and F by 0.5309 points. These are arithmetic differences from rounded supplied scores, not uncertainty estimates or independent replications.

**Drop private completion-to-decoder association as the supported mechanism.** Its own-route advantage was a central falsifiable prediction; the supplied pooled route instead improves both selected states, especially J. This does not prove that every private association is harmful, but it removes the current reason to prioritize that explanation or spend on more reassignment variants.

Pooled weights expose the mean completion vector to each recipient, discarding completion-component identity at that interface. Thus the larger pooled J/F gap suggests a different possibility: joint observation-pattern training may improve marginal completion or shared representations. It does not show that served predictions exploit joint residual configurations. Generic regularization and optimization explanations remain live.

The pooled scores reuse selected states and VALID candidates from the completed private-training study. Training/serving differ, and this route became salient after inspection. The scores can motivate a new fixed hypothesis; they cannot establish its predictive superiority.

## Recommendation: one narrowly bounded screen

Keep a **low-priority, prospectively fixed pooled-training hypothesis**; abandon the private-association claim now. Its restricted question is:

> Does exact joint TRAIN observation-pattern supervision improve target ranking when completion is pooled during both training and serving, beyond matched marginal supervision and a capable structured single?

Prepare three fresh arms for one paired-seed futility screen:

1. **J_P:** frozen shared-backbone four-member architecture and J auxiliary, with mean native clamped completion weights supplied to every recipient during main training and serving.
2. **F_P:** exactly the same pooling, architecture, initialization, labels, trace, target BCE, optimizer and lambda 1; use F auxiliary.
3. **C_mu:** one fresh width-64 native encoder/scorer/target decoder with the existing nonlinear, full-context two-sided count-potential joint density. Use its exact expected observation bits as completion weights, `1.05 E[Z_i|context]`, followed by the nonlinear native decoder.

J_P/F_P retain detached completion and equal mean raw target logits. No current state is a donor. Fit with the separately qualified literal replay provider; retain every native slot, duplicate-record mask effect, empty query and original reduction. Fix 100 epochs, 1,700 updates, 100 complete official VALID Hits@50 selectors and first exact tie, followed by two selected-state replays. Do not add route, lambda or head searches.

C_mu is a proper correlated single distribution, not a terminal affine or independent-bit baseline. Implement the memo's count normalizer and exact unary marginals, holding the context/count potential fixed when obtaining those marginals. Its auxiliary trains the full density; detach density-derived completion from main BCE. Teacher counts remain labels only. This changes the memo's sampled target readout to deterministic exact marginal completion, so it needs a separate source specification and qualification.

C_mu can test whether count-aware joint supervision suffices for the pooled target interface. It does not exhaust arbitrary spatial dependence. Even beating it would not justify a higher-order or ensemble-necessity claim; the full-context autoregressive alternative remains a later requirement for such claims.

## Contribution boundary, work and stopping

Credit NCNC's recursive completion/readout, GRAN's factorial-Bernoulli mixture likelihood and responsibilities, masked topology/degree supervision, normalized count-potential models, and PIFM's direct NCNC-prior joint refinement. The possible contribution is the narrowly specified **joint source-pattern auxiliary inside the native scorer, transferred to pooled marginal completion without an additional inference refiner**. No new general joint-generation, mixture or marginalization principle is proposed. An operational difference from PIFM does not establish novelty.

The screen costs three complete fits: **5,100 optimizer updates, 300 selectors and 306 ordinary complete VALID evaluations**, plus qualification and replay-provider work. J_P/F_P retain four member decoder paths. C_mu pays exact count-state and DP work: approximately quadratic in left/right support sizes plus the full two-dimensional count head. Parameter totals, wall time and peaks remain unmeasured. Qualify fabricated likelihood/marginal/gradient parity and an uncapped complete native batch before fitting; do not substitute a weakened single if this fails.

Precommit these futility rules:

- If J_P does not improve served ranking over F_P, stop this pooled joint-training proposal; better source likelihood cannot rescue it.
- If C_mu matches or exceeds J_P, stop the bank-specific contribution and retain structured-single sufficiency as the simpler explanation.
- If improvement accompanies only source-zero fit, without observed-positive/marginal competence on the same diagnostic masks, do not attribute it to useful graph dependencies.
- If a complete capable-control comparison is infeasible, report that limit and suspend the mechanism claim.

A positive one-seed screen only warrants prospectively fixed fresh replication. It is not manuscript evidence. No PIFM fit is added to this screen; any later comparison must use the separately qualified common-task adaptation already specified. The immediate scientific action is to retire private association, not to rename the post hoc pooled score as the original success.
