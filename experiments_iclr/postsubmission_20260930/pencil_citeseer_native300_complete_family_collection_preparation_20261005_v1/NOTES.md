## Compatibility and interpretation

PENCIL and the saved NCN job bind the same released AVAILABLE_MANIFEST SHA-256 `1b9c8bb57278d91b0f6212136225afcfd6b067c6b17e0dfed7ee36dd6316efdc` and feature qualification. PENCIL calls the same pinned NCN TRAIN/VALID input loader. Both use 3,870 nonself TRAIN positives, 227 VALID positives, all 500 fixed row-associated negatives, and the original 3,327 × 3,703 raw features. The adapter restores all 113,727 VALID occurrences after native unordered-query deduplication.

Both metric sources calculate tie-averaged rank `1 + 0.5*(count(negative > positive) + count(negative >= positive))`, then mean reciprocal rank with float32 rank/reduction. NCN publishes rounded four-decimal MRR. PENCIL preserves both its prebroadcast evaluator MRR and the native float32 broadcast selection scalar.

The native selectors differ. NCN selects its first strict rounded-four-decimal maximum at five-epoch cadence, stopping after eleven consecutive misses or epoch 9,999; independent constituents are individually selected. PENCIL selects its first strict native broadcast maximum at epochs 2,4,...300, runs all 300 epochs and performs 150 VALID passes. The collector preserves both choices; it reads no history to recompute selection.

A direct scalar comparison is meaningful as a **descriptive selected VALID MRR comparison on the same fixed input/negative population**. It is not an equal-budget or equal-selector comparison, official structure-only SOTA reproduction, paired statistical test, TEST result or evidence of graph generality. PENCIL is the prospectively fixed raw-feature early-fusion native adaptation. Existing NCN four-decimal values retain their original precision; neither scores nor selectors are rewritten.
