## What the diagnostics can explain

Higher mean shared TRAIN eval NLL and lower VALID member quality describe a learning deficit. Distinct correct coverage, rescue/harm and complete rank disagreements describe complementary errors and how much the fixed probability mean converts them. These comparisons use every role row and all private members; indices are never arbitrarily paired across banks/seeds.

Prediction evidence cannot by itself assign cause to sharing, capacity, factor prior or functional geometry. Fresh serving references omit learned factor tensors and actual native operator responses. This reader makes no factor-norm/operator-response or causal geometry claim. Root's later tied-four-path/capacity and other frozen controls remain required before stronger claims; independently frozen tasks/splits and adequate repeats remain necessary for confirmation.
