## Interpretation and falsifiers

- **Hidden member heads beat their matched logit processors:** the frozen pre-head states support useful linear decisions beyond what this matched linear score transformation recovers. Mirrored single/ordinary gains are necessary to assess whether this is generic refitting/regularization. This does not establish that tied cross-ratios caused the deficit.
- **Hidden concat beats logit concat:** extra hidden directions help this fixed classifier. It does not quantify conditional mutual information or prove member-specific information.
- **Hidden concat beats hidden mean only:** coordinate averaging or metric/capacity can explain the gain. With four orthogonal coordinate relabelings of one common feature bank, normalized concat has the same Gram kernel as one stream, while their coordinate mean can discard information. Thus this contrast alone is not useful complementarity evidence.
- **Concat versus member0/all member-head diagnostics and mean private-head probabilities:** distinguishes multi-stream linear combination from a stronger single stream or ordinary independent readout fitting. No development-selected winner is silently substituted.
- **Logit processors match hidden probes:** the inexpensive class-score transformation explains the measured gain; do not claim undiscovered hidden information.
- **Unit+contrast processing does not beat the identically processed independent bank orsingle:** no demonstrated practical advantage. Existing backbone losses and joint versus own checkpoint selection differ, so even a favorable contrast is a frozen-state observation rather than a causal benefit of parameter sharing.
