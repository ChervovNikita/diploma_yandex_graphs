## What would count as a new functional direction

Fix the full input/context and a declared centered-logit output metric. Let J_fast be the complete current private Jacobian, and J_graph the derivative of an added graph block. Additional first-order directions require

    rank([J_fast,J_graph]) > rank(J_fast),
    equivalently (I−Pi_fast)J_graph != 0.

No Jacobian, rank, projector or tensor is computed here. The criterion is standard tangent-space analysis, not an invented learning rule. A nonzero coefficient gradient, different spectral curve, hidden separation or isolated query response does not establish it. The criterion concerns the complete stacked outputs and actual downstream processing. The native NCNC/private-stem nonlinearities can already generate graph-conditioned changes; the restrictive common-linear-P model cannot be substituted for its source.

A valid invertible R/S gauge right-multiplies the old Jacobian and leaves its range unchanged. A new propagation block can escape that coordinate transformation, but may still lie in the old output span or alter only the optimization metric. For a finite context even distinct abstract graph operators can have identical responses on the available features, and a downstream nullspace can remove their difference.

For node-independent coefficients, a one-stage member filter

    G_m(P)H = sum_k a_mk T_k(P)H

is exactly coefficient mixing inside a head on the common complete bank R=Concat_k[T_k(P)H]. Give that head the same nonlinearity, gate and readout, and it reproduces the entire member function. Keep the same all-member pool and it reproduces the served function too. Linear mean-*logit* folding is an additional special case; a mean of probabilities cannot silently be replaced by probabilities of mean logits. Repeated private nonlinear states or adaptive neighborhoods may need additional bank states and work; they do not invalidate the one-stage absorption result.

## A concrete error-repair hypothesis, and its equivalence

Suppose two *available meaningful* relations have fixed operators P1,P2. Write A=P1+P2 and C=P1−P2. Then

    [C,A] = 2[P1,P2].

If the commutator is nonzero,C cannot be any scalar polynomial p(A) as an operator: every polynomial inA commutes withA. This supplies a precise way to retain evidence absent from a single collapsed scalar spectral bank. For a particular feature stateH,CH can nevertheless remain in the same data-response span. Abstract noncommutation is not actual native Jacobian-rank evidence.

The formal correction site is

    H_m_plus = sigma((A H_m)W + q_m(C H_m)U + H_m V),

with sharedW,U,V and private scalarq_m, retaining all members at serving. This is a known two-relation bank with restricted feature-map tying, not proposed source or a frozen numeric recipe. Atq=0 it does not initially apply the contrast; its q derivative carries CH through the actual downstream derivative.

The hypothesized repair is specific: one relation carries useful neighbor evidence while another contaminates a common aggregate; the contrast preserves the distinction *before* aggregation/nonlinear decoding discards it, allowing TRAIN supervision to suppress the harmful relation without discarding the useful one. There is no evidence that this is the native Amazon/NCNC error cause. Relations must arise from allowed observed structure/features; arbitrary partitions or held-label meanings cannot be treated as task evidence.

R-GCN/HGT/GNN-FiLM already provide relation-conditioned maps and message modulation. A capable relation-bank single can learn the same contrast, usually with more flexible maps or node gates. An equally adapted independent ensemble can also use it. A relation-constant bias inside per-relation softmax is null, while a within-neighborhood score adapter is ordinary native attention. Thus this construction genuinely illustrates possible additional operator access relative to a deficient collapsed representation, but establishes no new graph-adaptation principle.

It can fail if the shared stem already erased the needed features, the contrast lies in a downstream nullspace, common modern features already preserve it, or different regions need opposite signs that one global coefficient cannot fit. Member coefficients can converge identically or separate nuisance evidence. Mean pooling may dilute a competent correction. A nonnegative probability pool cannot repair a unanimously wrong pairwise ordering unless some member predictions change, but changing them need not improve the complete pool or unseen examples. TRAIN improvement and tangent novelty alone supply no repair/generalization theorem.

## Fair capable-single and independent controls

These are attribution requirements, **not an arm list, source grid or execution protocol**:

- A tied graph-correction block on the same qualified model separates private freedom from ordinary common adaptation.
- A complete same polynomial/relation bank with equally capable nonlinear heads/gates removes the denied-information single handicap. Retain identity,root paths and correctly propagated biases.
- A capable relation-aware/local-global single gets the same edge/relation information and correction site, with separate maps or joint mixing within the paid capacity/work budget.
- A same-four-stream forward trained on the pooled task loss holds the function/information fixed while examining separate member supervision. A stronger joint readout is relevant if a restricted pooled single cannot exploit its bank competently.
- An ordinary all-layer shared-factor model preserves complete private nonlinear trajectories and exact member messages. Common stored weights do not make its graph response common.
- A same-operation independent ensemble separates operator utility from tying benefit, with competent packing and complete acquisition/training/selection budgets.

Published filter/MoE methods with learned inference gates add a different serving operation. Give them their declared information/budget or label the adaptation; uniform all-member serving cannot inherit their routing result silently. No generic single is certified strongest by a paper's baseline table or equal width alone.

For costs, a fixed common linear bank can share construction. Evolving embeddings,graph prefixes or masks require bank recomputation; private input masks require their distinct transport. Recurrent member nonlinearities/attention can require member-resolved states. Noncommuting relation words cannot all be transported for free at arbitrary depth. Charge preprocessing,edge-channel forward/backward work,dense maps,state memory,selection and failures. Fewer sparse API calls or shared parameter storage do not prove a complete paid-work advantage.
