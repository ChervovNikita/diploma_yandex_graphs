# Production adapter and measurement contract

## What this generation delivers

The finite plan and GPU worker dry-run are executable using only source/JSON metadata on the guarded allocation. The worker has **no child dispatch path**. `run` returns BLOCKED with zero children. This is a reviewable launcher skeleton: production dispatch still needs the concrete adapter below, exact readiness evidence and human GO. No numerical scheduler tests or model work are performed.

The predecessor mechanisms remain pinned in `REUSED_SUPERVISOR_PINS.json`. `launch_contracts.legacy` imports their stdlib modules, and the dry-run verifies `admissible`, `probe_system` and `terminal_record` are available. It does not call predecessor `execute`, change its runtime-v2 constants, or inherit its historical three-worker assignments. New typed `fixed_argv` proposals accept qualified runtime/job bindings without hardcoding a runtime generation.

## Required measured inputs

Each native preset × scientific procedure × protocol × target hardware/runtime/provider case needs a scope-pinned mature single-job profile, or an explicitly justified measured equivalence class. Record:

- native case/source/runtime/provider/evaluator hashes, literal split and fit seed scope, physical host/GPU UUID/model/VRAM/driver, interpreter/environment;
- initialization/provider/cache construction time and transient RAM/VRAM; exact native batch/graph/query population, variable-size tail and native preprocessing;
- mature optimizer state after real updates, total device-process VRAM as well as allocator peaks, host RSS and shared cache ownership; complete native TRAIN/whole VALID and selected save/reload/export peaks;
- measured disk peaks and retained bytes for source/data caches, selected/recovery/checkpoint history, actual/mean VALID exports, I4 exports, logs and interrupted attempts; no silent batch/cache reduction;
- time for construction, completed TRAIN epochs, required source TRAIN diagnostics/RNG emulation, VALID, selected checkpoint/export, resume and finalization; observed stop lengths/censoring and contention conditions;
- pair measurements for every admitted profile pairing on the same physical GPU, reciprocal profile IDs, combined GPU/host/disk peaks, launch-order/transient overlap and explicit measured headroom. At most two children is a ceiling, not a claim that every pair fits;
- source-reference profiles distinct from matched-extended profiles. Source OGB includes its required TRAIN diagnostics/source RNG semantics; matched pilot native costs cannot silently populate that track.

`MEASUREMENT_REQUIREMENTS.json` supplies machine-readable field names. The admission template has no fabricated reservations, PASS receipts, host probes or GO.
