# Research target, reaffirmed 15 October 2026

Build a trainable ensemble around one shared native GNN, with small additional
trainable parameter cost and better prediction quality than a matched native
single and four independently trained native models. Broad applicability is a
design goal, not a proven property. Task-specific wrapper settings are allowed.
BatchEnsemble factors are replaceable; every comparison must disclose actual
trainable parameters, training cost, native architecture and prediction rule.

The next decision should follow full-dataset, paired-seed evidence and adequate
training, with complete outcomes retained. Tiny smoke runs qualify execution;
they do not qualify scientific hypotheses. Use previous successful and failed
experiments, primary papers and author implementations to avoid repeating
work. Isolate route competence, useful complementarity and served prediction
quality. A diversity statistic or local gradient bound alone is insufficient.

Current authority: one-GPU allocation only. Representative bounded pilots are
authorized; no full 6,460-fit campaign GO has been given. Additional resources
will follow a concrete reviewed proposal. Original scores remain untouched.

Current parallel research: complete the nine-fit BN/geometry/correction study;
assess shared-gradient interference with matched controls; revisit strongest
retained cross-setting improvements; and finish actual resource estimates and
simple launch commands for the broad study. Manuscript review follows a
substantial supported update, not every engineering change.
