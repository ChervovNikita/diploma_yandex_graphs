## Frozen rejection and attribution

Reject the fixed structured pair recipe unless its mean served AUC strictly
improves over **residual_F, common and permuted**. A tie with permutation
does not support the structural explanation. Do not retune similarity,
bins, coefficient, initialization or selector. Advancement toward the main
goal additionally requires strict mean gains over genuine **BCE_I4 and
pair_I4**. Every paired seed and BCE harm remains visible; a mean-AUC
screen is not a superiority certificate or permission to hide harms.

A win over BCE-I4 without beating pairwise-I4 supports an objective benefit.
Even a win over pairwise-I4 compares complete procedures. Independent
members trained with structured roles would be needed to isolate sharing's
causal contribution and remain outside this bounded protocol. The stronger
competence-plus-useful-complementarity explanation also needs mean/worst
member AUC/BCE, absolute common reversals, acquired/lost alternatives,
pooling gain and served repairs minus harms. No new diagnostic acceptance
threshold is invented. Better members without pooling gain support a
competence result. AUC improvement with worse BCE is a ranking/probability
trade-off.

These are three paired optimizer seeds on one already exposed scaffold
VALID split (130 positives,21 negatives). The2730 pairs share molecules and
do not increase experimental replication. There is no independent TEST,
graph-generalization, calibration or novelty guarantee, no port to PubMed
or link tasks and no full-campaign GO. Partial structural outcomes were not
read or used to define this protocol.
