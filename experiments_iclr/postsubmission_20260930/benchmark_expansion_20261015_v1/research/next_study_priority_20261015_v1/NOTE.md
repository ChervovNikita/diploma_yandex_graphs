## Explicit falsifier and interpretation

Let S, U and P denote structured, uniform and permuted residual procedures. Preserve the frozen directional screen:

- **Structural utility:** both paired mean served-accuracy differences S−U and S−P must be positive. A nonpositive contrast fails that screen. An S/P tie supports no structural attribution, even if both beat U.
- **The user's main goal:** S must also improve on genuine native M1 and I4; a G0-only or U-only recovery is insufficient. Keep G0 visible rather than replacing the original method with a weak convenience baseline. An incremental gain that still loses to I4 remains incremental.
- **Useful alternatives:** report actual pooled node repairs/harms, new/lost correct-member alternatives, mean/worst member accuracy/NLL, and strict common-false-rival events. Multiclass all-member-wrong is not equivalent to a common rival. Disagreement or oracle coverage cannot rescue a failed accuracy comparison.

All contrasts, seed signs and NLL changes remain visible; no new NLL veto or significance threshold is introduced. Worse NLL limits probability-quality claims. A cap with unresolved convergence is not evidence that the mechanism cannot work. These screens are exploratory, not equivalence tests or proof of universal superiority.

If U improves over native I4 but S fails its ingredient contrasts, report **a uniform-residual result and a failed specialization result**. Do not retrospectively designate whichever arm wins as the prespecified successful method. If neither residual procedure improves over native I4, this study supplies no support for extending the present recipe as an architecture-general advantage; adding more variants on these exposed settings would need a new diagnosis.

## Transfer scope and known ancestry

PubMed has 3,943 VALID nodes with class counts **[72,1919,1952]**. Whole accuracy can hide minority harms; retain per-class support/accuracy/NLL and descriptive macro metrics. Nodes on one transductive graph are dependent. PubMed also has substantial prior development exposure: this is a different native setting, **not protected confirmation**. Changing task, dataset and backbone together tests representative transport, not which individual change caused an effect. Neither a PubMed win nor a pairwise MolBACE win grants generality or independent confirmation. A positive result would justify a separately frozen confirmation population and bounded claim. [E3]

Concrete retained closest priors are **TabLoRA** ([2607.10077v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10077v1), saved §§3.3–3.5: joint trainable shared weights/private low-rank corrections, own losses and raw-logit averaging), **TabM** ([2410.24210v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24210v3): efficient shared submodels, own-risk training and collective selection), and **ACE** ([2108.02385v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2108.02385v1), saved §3.4: different shared-backbone and private-expert supervision). Their architectures, assignments and serving differ; none guarantees native GNN transfer. Pairwise multitask ranking and proper own/pool losses also have explicit prior art in the retained notes. The contribution under investigation is a controlled, useful native-GNN adaptation, not a newly established sharing, specialization or pooling principle. [E4–E6]
