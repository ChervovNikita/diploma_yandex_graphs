# Common-error private credit: no additional candidate nominated

15 October 2026. **Keep the existing studies; nominate no new training rule or fit.** The most direct candidate considered was teacher-free, supervised private correction of errors shared by the routes. It is relevant to the observed failure, but the concrete realizations already have saved definitions and controls. The new evidence does not identify a missing learning capability that warrants another variant. This is a bounded scientific-priority decision, not a claim that known adaptations cannot help.

## Current evidence and the proposed causal step

The complete MolBACE residual-only bank has mean member AUC **74.560%**, served AUC **74.518%**, and I4 AUC **74.847%**. **95.54%** of its pool's ranking reversals are common to every route; pooling adds almost nothing. That is a real prediction deficit. It does not establish why TRAIN learning missed those distinctions, whether the private correction can represent them, or whether extra pressure on the same labels will generalize.

Competence must include the probability limitation: BCE is **1.952 versus I4 .890**. The subsequent complete diagnostic shows worse positive-class loss and better negative-class loss in every seed, with more false negatives and widespread excess loss. It neither establishes pure label shift nor licenses a VALID-fitted correction. Three seeds on the same 151 exposed molecules are development evidence. No partial structural-study result informs this note. [E1–E3]

**Candidate:** keep one live native body and small private input residuals, retain complete native own-risk F, and add bounded private true-label or ranking credit where the peers/common pool err. Retain the fixed mean serving. The attraction is direct supervised correction, without hidden-state repulsion. The unresolved premise is that the selected private update can acquire *different useful correct predictions*, rather than merely repeat the common learning signal or emphasize an unlearnable/noisy case.

Here it also means the needed questions and controls already exist: persistent responsibility, direct pairwise ranking, own/pool learning, and staged correction. ## Predictions and falsifiers if a distinct correction claim is later reopened

This is an interpretation contract, not a new run list or authorization. Reopening requires a specific completed-data diagnosis beyond error overlap: nontrivial TRAIN selector support in the actual forward convention and evidence that the proposed private correction can move the relevant native task scores. Those are necessary mechanism checks, not heldout-quality guarantees. A fixed update/serving definition and discriminating matched controls must then explain why this adaptation deserves priority over the existing questions. A newly located paper or another weight formula alone does not supply that premise.

1. **Acquisition:** an error-selected private rule should yield new correctly ranked alternatives on previously common-error cases and positive net served repairs on the complete evaluation population. Lower common-error *fraction*, larger spread, lower TRAIN loss or more oracle coverage alone would not support it. No reduction in absolute common errors/net served harms would reject the stated mechanism.
2. **Selection adds value:** any improvement must exceed the otherwise identical common-supervision/pool rule with matched supervision dose, state convention and selection opportunity. A match leaves a generic objective or weighting explanation. TRAIN event support and actual private learning response must be established in the specified forward convention before claiming that the selector operates usefully; selected VALID failures are not that evidence.
3. **Useful competence:** report mean/worst member task quality and proper loss together with served quality. AUC improvement with probability harm is a trade-off; an NLL-only gain does not meet the ranking goal. Positive background supervision supplies no automatic safeguard.
4. **Native reference bar:** retain unchanged native M1 and genuine independent I4, plus the *same additional learning rule* on native independent models for any wrapper-specific claim. If objective-matched I4 explains the gain, shared-wrapper superiority is unsupported. Native parameters, inputs, optimizer groups, controller and serving opportunities must remain explicit; small parameter overhead does not imply small training cost.

No new coefficient, mask, adapter site, threshold or grid is selected. The present queue already tests the important alternatives. **PubMed24 remains the conditional default transfer comparison; pairwise21 remains prepared; the running structural study stays frozen.** This bounded no-new-candidate conclusion does not imply hypothesis exhaustion: representative transfer and supervised pairwise competence remain distinct, unresolved and testable questions. No new source integration, fit, raw data/logit/checkpoint/TEST access, GPU work, Git operation or launch follows.
