## Three research directions, in priority order

### 1. Replace destructive route parameterization while retaining the complete native backbone

Test small additive private corrections initialized at the native evaluation function; permit removal of BatchEnsemble. All native operators remain shared and trainable. Distinct down projections with zero up projections supply distinct initial private learning features without changing initial predictions. Rank/site choices follow a declared parameter budget and task semantics before outcomes.

**Best support:** the MolBACE correction recovers almost four points; Penn and FiLM show that sharing can produce useful quality with small route parameters. **Strongest objection:** none proves this new residual-only recipe, and GCN/GAT transfer plus Penn's loss to original GNNM show placement/parameterization dependence. **Decisive test:** residual-only versus base GNNM and BE-plus-residual, each against unchanged native M1/I4. Winning only against a weak G0 is insufficient.

### 2. Give routes identifiable task responsibilities

Use declared TRAIN-only context emphasis with positive supervision everywhere; compare meaningful assignments with matched permuted/IID assignments. The same native shared core serves every route. Settings may differ by task, but targets must correspond to that task's actual evaluation units.

**Best support:** SAGE shared members were weaker on average yet jointly useful; useful complementarity matters more than every member beating M1. **Strongest objection:** the tested intermediate exchange improved average members but destroyed coverage on SAGE and lost accuracy on all nine SAGE/GCN/GAT seeds. Physics E largely matched its receiver control. NCNC private completion nearly matched I4 on VALID (**66.4130 vs 66.4150**), then lost on frozen TEST (**67.2909 vs 67.6298**); original-fit host authority remains qualified here. **Decisive test:** structured responsibility must beat uniform and matched permuted weighting in served native task quality, not merely disagreement or a member-correct union.

### 3. Learn to serve useful alternatives, conditional on having them

Compare mean, global and conditional readouts under the same training objective and backbone. Train the gate only on TRAIN with a declared information set; retain the fixed native evaluator. This is an existing mixture/stacking idea, not a novelty claim.

**Best support:** SAGE gained coverage but lost many alternatives in averaging; Physics had more covered cases than equipped I4 yet far more pooling losses. **Strongest objection:** a gate cannot recover information absent from all routes; fixed MolBACE readout changes did not close the native-I4 gap. The proposed trained conditional readout has no completed efficacy evidence. **Decisive test:** conditional beats global and mean with matched training and low overhead, while reporting repaired/harmed cases and unavailable alternatives.
