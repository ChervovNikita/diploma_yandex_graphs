5.2 Selecting submodels after training
The design of TabM allows selecting only a subset of submodels after training based on any criteria, simply by pruning extra prediction heads and the corresponding rows of the adapter matrices.
To showcase this mechanics, after the training, we Greedily construct a subset of TabM’s submodels with the best collective performance on the validation set, and denote this “pruned” TabM as \text{\mbox{\text{TabM}}}[\text{G}] .
The performance reported in Figure 6 shows that \text{\mbox{\text{TabM}}}[\text{G}] is slightly behind the vanilla TabM.
On average over 46 datasets, the greedy submodel selection results in 8.8\pm 6.6 submodels out of the initial k=32 , which can result in faster inference.
