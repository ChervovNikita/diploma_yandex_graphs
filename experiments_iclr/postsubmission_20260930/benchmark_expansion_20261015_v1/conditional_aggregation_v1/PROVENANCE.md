# Provenance and prior limitations

The method-matrix audit's C6 section establishes the matched comparison:
mean/global/conditional readouts under the same all-block global J, followed
by a same-selected-bank gate versus mean diagnostic. Its review of the old
link gate found scalar route-logit deviation and RMS reduce to delta and
approximately abs(delta), and multiclass logit deviation depends on arbitrary
class-common shifts. This package replaces that feature choice with two
fixed prediction-distribution statistics. It does not add feature selection.

The retained `Actual_GraphSAGE_common_wrapper_complete39_closed_interpreted_20261010_v1`
record in `prior_result_index.json` contains the historical error analysis.
For example, `shared4_unchanged` reports 993 lost correct alternatives over
three seed readouts, alongside its complete losses, costs and frozen decision.
This is a motivation to test whether a fixed mean cancels useful alternatives.
It is not new evidence for this aggregator, a result on the new molecular/link
tasks, or permission to rerun the old study/capacity controls. Historical
negative decisions remain unchanged. No historical score was recomputed.

The catalogue's C6 entry is a proposal and the prior ledger index is a
retrieval aid, not new primary-paper evidence. The design belongs to the
established mixture-of-experts / learned stacking family; normalized entropy
and Jensen-Shannon divergence are standard statistics. No novelty claim or
new literature-read credit is made. No additional primary paper was fetched
for this bounded source task.

`SOURCES.json` pins the exact audit, old gate, retained error-analysis index,
catalogue and native source interfaces used. Frozen source bytes and original
paper scores are preserved. Parent review approved the fixed method before
implementation/freezing, including the documented seed-offset correction.
