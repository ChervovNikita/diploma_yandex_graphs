## I4 and aggregation reporting

I4 retains four independent native fits, own optimizers/RNG/selectors/checkpoints,
seed offsets `[0, 100003, 200003, 300007]`, exact M1/body0 reuse, and arithmetic
raw-logit mean serving. Aligned saved member logits permit both raw-logit mean
and fixed probability mean without a fit or forward. Binary uses sigmoid;
multiclass uses softmax; the native evaluator and proper loss remain explicit.
A saved pooled mean alone cannot recover the mean of member probabilities.

The completed three-seed molecular route diagnosis already compares both fixed
rules for ordinary I4 and shared banks, retaining BCE tradeoffs. A learned shared
aggregator should be reported against that standard independent mixture and the
same selected shared bank under a fixed mean. Comparing only against an arbitrary
weak I4 readout would not justify a broad aggregation claim. No learned-on-both-
sides comparison, new recipe, serving change or tuning is introduced here.

## Portability

The frozen dispatcher enforces current authorization by fixing the allocation
root/UUID and rejecting 77. It is **not yet a portable three-GPU one-command
package**. Future implementation needs explicit host/GPU profiles, path and
dependency bindings, and global fit/I4 claims and leases across storage layouts.
Future deployment separately needs route authorization, literal guards, shared
locking/permissions, target-device lifecycle/resources and reviewed assignments.
The current guards remain unchanged; 77 was not accessed.
