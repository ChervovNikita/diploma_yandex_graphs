The epoch processed 793 batches, 811,404 mixed queries and 100 optimizer updates. TRAIN took 337.68 seconds, VALID 39.76 seconds; inclusive child time was 400.57 seconds. The harness sets native-created loader pin_memory=False. The original failure and all other deviations remain disclosed in the bound RESOURCE receipt. No root-cause, numerical transfer parity or native eight-GPU runtime equivalence is claimed.

No predictive metrics, selected scores, checkpoints or TEST reads were produced. The full 20-epoch scientific baseline still needs fresh fits. This result establishes practical feasibility only; it gives no quality comparison or GNNM advantage.
