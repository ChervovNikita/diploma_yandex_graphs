# Next experiment follows the observed errors

The complete WikiCS study supports two different conclusions. Unit factors with the combined contrastive objective improve served development accuracy by 0.5056 percentage points. Almost all of that gain comes from stronger individual routes: mean member accuracy improves by 0.480 points, while averaging benefit increases by only 0.025 points. The result remains 0.4108 points below the ordinary independent ensemble. It is a promising learning signal, not superiority.

Random sign initialization creates more predictive coverage but weakens the routes. The evidence therefore does not support pursuing arbitrary embedding separation as the main objective. Where every route puts the same wrong class above the truth, changing convex aggregation weights cannot repair the existing predictions. Training must acquire different correct rankings.

The next candidate assigns each route different same-class positives derived from root features, feature residuals, immediate neighbours and two-hop neighbours. Every route still learns every labelled training node. The shared backbone, unit initialization and mean-probability prediction stay fixed. The hypothesis is that these compatible but different targets can develop useful private-factor decisions without the competence loss of random sign starts.

The comparison is with an exact common-target control and a fixed class- and positive-count-preserving permutation control. The common target also changes each route's target concentration and entropy, so this comparison does not isolate persistent assignment from those properties. Permutation preserves positive-relation counts, not public graph degrees. With root features among the signatures, a positive result alone would not identify a topology-specific effect. Stage 1 has nine complete fits. A worthwhile result must improve served accuracy, preserve member competence and improve the observed common wrong-competitor errors. Conditional Stage 2 compares with ordinary and objective-matched single models and independent ensembles. The entire plan is frozen before any new fitted result, including the stopping rules. The separate WikiCS attribution and molecular studies remain unopened and continue unchanged.

Graph filters, supervised contrastive learning, positive mining and BatchEnsemble are established ingredients. This experiment tests their specific combination and mechanism; novelty and general superiority remain unestablished. It is exploratory on a previously inspected graph. Unused-task or split confirmation and capable published controls are required before making a broader claim.

## Checkpoint interpretation correction

The frozen Wiki24 `independent4_contrastive` arm is an untied **coupled** bank with joint pooled checkpoint selection and joint local restoration. Its residual cross-member loss couples its members. It is not an ordinary independently trained, own-selected ensemble. Its saved own checkpoints were not its served bank. The plain `independent4` arm remains the ordinary independent ensemble and its reported 82.0440% development score is unchanged. This correction changes the interpretation, not any frozen source, selected checkpoint or score.
