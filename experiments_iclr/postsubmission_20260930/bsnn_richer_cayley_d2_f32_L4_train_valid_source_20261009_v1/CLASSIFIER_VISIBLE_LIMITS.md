# What a competent comparator would establish

The three old BSNN results establish completion, learning and faithful selected-state reporting. Their pooled AUROCs alone provide no evidence about complementarity of individual sampled geometries. The richer tuple is intended to reduce a known feature-path/depth mismatch; its competence is not yet established.

A future advantage of a geometry-only bank requires the actual restored probability pool to improve against competent ordinary and independent predictors, with member quality and all training/serving costs retained. Different private factors, map norms, normalized operator signatures or conditional representability do not establish that result. The exact discrete row-sign witness in the saved NSD mechanism assessment shows that raw map/factor diversity can leave every deployed message and prediction unchanged. That witness belongs to the general tanh-factor sites; its representability is not asserted for the author BSNN concentration/Householder parameterization.

Classifier-visible evidence uses the complete function. For binary logits, the relevant quantity is the class log-odds `ell_m(q)=log p_m(1)_q−log p_m(0)_q`. Under a common prospectively fixed source-feature intervention T that preserves the query features and graph support, the response is `ell_m(TX,q)−ell_m(X,q)`. A resolved response difference identifies different functions on that probe. It does not establish useful graph evidence, exclusion of node-frame adaptation, causal mediation or novelty.

For BSNN, the same external input probe must also use matched recorded evaluation streams and fixed four-draw serving: otherwise Monte Carlo variability can masquerade as different evidence dependence. The unchanged support already exposes complete Python/NumPy/SciPy/Torch stream capture and restoration. No probe or extra call is implemented or admitted here. Fresh draws are samples from one learned distribution and network, not persistent separately trained expert identities.

The earlier operator-action proposal measures normalized coupling on node-basis probes. It can vary even when a zero right feature transform makes the actual propagated value identically zero, or when the shared classifier ignores the affected state direction. Therefore compare three distinct facts: operator difference, action on the actual task values, and classifier-visible source response. None substitutes for useful original-input pool quality.

Even a stronger richer BSNN result would be a result for this chosen architecture/objective/work package. Its separate raw-feature distribution path, orthogonal sampling, native KL, normalization and four-draw inference remain different from general NSD. A remaining gap does not identify incidence factorization as the cause, and a recovered gap would not isolate which depth/stem/dropout choice helped. All configurations selected after prior baseline evidence remain explicitly exploratory. No desired conclusion or new method claim follows from the conditional algebra.
