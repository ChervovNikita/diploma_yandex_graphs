variability on one selected graph; nodes and members are not independent graphs.

## Separate rank acquisition from an aggregation repair

For every COMMON object with one wrong class above truth in all four members,
retain the qualifying wrong-class identities from COMMON before candidate
opening. The sealed readout already reports strict reversal against the smallest
qualifying class. That is a bounded rank diagnostic; reversing one class does
not necessarily remove the complete obstruction.

A useful CPU-only refinement is to use ALL qualifying COMMON wrong classes:
count objects for which one same candidate member strictly ranks truth above
every old common competitor. Do not let different members each beat a different
competitor and call that one competent answer. Also record whether any candidate
member actually predicts truth, and whether the served mean probability repairs
the object. All these counts can be computed from existing logits without
another inference call. Root may add this refinement prospectively before any
candidate prediction opening; it is not a new gate.

Report four distinct outcomes on the COMMON-frozen obstruction population:
rank acquisition with a served repair; rank acquisition with the pool still
wrong; no rank acquisition and persistent error; and a changed common wrong
competitor. Ties remain excluded from strict-rank claims. Ordinary argmax accuracy
keeps the native tie convention. New competitors and introduced errors must also
be counted across the complete original COMMON-correct population.

## Explain where a gain comes from

Use the existing paired repair/harm accounting for the full population and
COMMON-frozen cohorts: all-member wrong, common competitor, at-least-one-member
correct but pool wrong, and ordinary COMMON-correct objects. Cohorts overlap and
their counts must not be added. A gain can come from stronger members, better
pooling, or both. The exact difference between served accuracy and mean member
accuracy is an accounting quantity, not a causal mechanism.

Prediction-rank changes are more informative than hidden distance or factor
norm. Raw logit-margin magnitudes can change with confidence/scale even without
an ordering change. Prioritize strict ranking flips, actual member correctness,
served repairs AND harms, and NLL/Brier. Hidden representations may provide
secondary illustrations, with no classifier-nullspace or collapse diagnosis
inferred from distance alone.

## Interpret outcomes without promoting a convenient story

If factors differ but rankings do not improve, this is active yet ineffective
steering. If mean members improve but pool-minus-member benefit does not, describe
stronger member learning rather than newly acquired complementary diversity.
If coverage increases while competence or whole-population quality falls, retain
the failure. If ROUTE and permuted targets perform similarly, context semantics
remains unsupported. Qm/Qbar concentration effects and X's feature-only context
prevent pure topology claims even after a positive Stage1 contrast.

A passing Stage1 result supports consideration of the unchanged12 ordinary and
objective-matched single/untied references. Those distinguish shared-route utility
from a general contrastive regularizer. An unused task/split confirmation and
qualified efficient-ensemble/diversity comparisons remain necessary before
broad quality or methodological novelty claims.
