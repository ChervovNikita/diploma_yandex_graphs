Scope
This is a source/protocol critique, not a manuscript decision, numerical qualification, or execution release. I read the complete sealed nine-file proposal, exact deltas, prospective fabricated qualification plan, and helper. I inspected the explicitly bound original replay code and transitive manifest-bound driver scorer/profile/state sources needed to verify the mechanism. No checkpoints, model payloads, data, TEST, diagnostic results, failed-qualification results, or baseline-result files were opened. No proposal/helper/numerical code was imported or executed. The packet's descriptions of the diagnostic and old failure remain packet assertions, not independently verified results in this review.

The seven proposal payloads match their manifest's bytes/SHA256 values, and the manifest matches the seal's binding. SOURCE_PINS.json pins all 25 source/metadata files used and distinguishes partial reading where only a function was needed. This review does not re-certify unread old payloads or results.

Principal assessment
The fixed False_anchor/True1/True2 schedule can supply historical vector references conditionally. A full newly produced vector with the authenticated original typed, ordered raw-score digest supplies those bytes under the existing SHA256 assumption. Anchor nondeterminism does not invalidate an exact match. A mismatching anchor supplies no historical error bound. Without the failed call's vector, its cause and magnitude remain unknown.

The proposal correctly preserves the old FAILED qualification and uses a distinct terminal. A successor cannot repair or substitute for old ALL25_SELECTED_STATE_REPLAY_PASS. This distinction must persist in its source, result schema, qualification, release and public account. New-profile outputs are historical bytes only for pools that actually authenticate.

The packet explicitly acknowledges the new tolerance scope, but does not justify that scope scientifically. The successor also needs explicit I4 reconstruction validation, failure-path accounting and post-accounting custody. Missing historical references block historical-error claims; they do not logically block a separately named full-family checkpoint/metric audit.

B1. 128*eps has no supplied scientific justification for the new acceptance scope.
Pins: PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT.json:80-92; reference_contract.py:43-51; bound qualify.py:20-24; QUALIFICATION_PLAN.json:17.

The old helper explicitly says "Engineering arithmetic tolerance only" and "neither a predictive success gate nor a selected experiment threshold." Reusing its values makes the new test reproducible, but does not establish that it bounds expected errors in complete graph computations or detects scientifically material discrepancies.

FP32 atol=rtol=1.52587890625e-5 is numerically correct. It is an absolute-plus-relative rule, not a universal 128-ULP bound: at reference=1, the permitted absolute difference is twice that number; near zero the absolute term permits many ULPs. No justification is supplied for reduction lengths, graph degree, cancellation, conditioning, score scale or third-party kernels. Two exact repeats and a fabricated one-ULP comparison cannot supply it. Fresh fabricated qualification checks the comparator and gate behavior, not a scientific forward-error bound.

Minimal correction: retain 128*eps as a named engineering receipt and report measured discrepancies against authenticated references. Remove its power to confer a scientifically interpreted raw-output reproduction PASS unless the intended limited meaning is independently justified. The safest minimal alternative is full 25 checkpoint/metric consistency under the new profile, with conditional historical-byte/error receipts. If a tolerance terminal is retained, define it literally as observed candidates within a preregistered engineering budget in this admitted invocation. It must imply neither theoretical accuracy, kernel correctness, predictive equivalence nor exact historical replay. Exact selected Hits50 remains required regardless of score tolerance.

B2. I4 needs a checked reference mean with explicit reconstruction provenance.
Pins: PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT.json:84,96; original replay_contract.py:48-59; driver pilot_evaluate.py:76-81.

I4 binds four historical member vectors, without an independently archived pooled-score hash. Selecting positive/negative references from different fixed labels, or different members from different labels, is valid when each pool separately authenticates. It is not metric selection.

Its reference mean is a new application of pinned float32 stack-and-mean in original member order to authenticated member bytes. Its computed digest is new reconstruction evidence. The scheduled metric gate mentions False_anchor/True1/True2 pools but does not explicitly require that reconstructed reference mean itself to equal the saved selected Hits50.

Minimal correction: bind member order, per-member/per-pool historical digests, selected states, canonical rows, exact pooling API, dtype and runtime. Record the new reference-mean digest with historical_pooled_digest_available=false. Require its official strict Hits50 to equal the saved scalar, rejecting inconsistency. Name measured errors candidate-vs-reconstructed-historical-member-reference errors. Do not claim exact archived pooled-byte identity. Qualify cross-label member/pool reconstruction and wrong-order/wrong-reduction failures.

75 is correct for only three scheduled profiles' 25 served metrics. Adding five I4 reference-mean checks yields 80; checking all 25 reconstructed/assembled references yields 100. Pin the revised count when adding reference metric checks.

B3. Nominal accounting is correct; failure-path accounting remains underspecified.
Pins: PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT.json:36-65; MINIMAL_REPLAY_PLAN.json:50-61; replay_numeric.py:97-143; replay_synthetic.py:87-97,121-145.

Original scoring snapshot slots: 5 N64 + 20 I4 member slots + 5 private F4 + 5 pooled F4 + 5 N70 = 40. An F4 call serves its four internal factor members using one full-model snapshot. Therefore the field "original_member_snapshot_slots=40" should say scoring snapshot slots; it is not forty unique fits or forty internal ensemble members. N64's reused fitting does not make its deployment scoring free, and its individual best snapshot can differ from I4's member0 snapshot.

Three profiles yield 120 nominal scorer invocations, 40 per profile. Routes total 75 native64, 15 native70, 15 private F4, 15 pooled F4. If every call returns, 120 encoder invocations and 240 complete-pool query batches follow from original receipts. These are not equal-FLOP claims. The 75 served metrics equal 25 cells * 3 profiles, before extra reference checks. Original 20 units / 35 unique fits / 59500 training steps remain unchanged. An audit reusing an authenticated existing fixture has zero new updates; fabricated failure cases add inference beyond baseline 120.

The old core increments score_valid_calls before score_valid. The old harness separately counts attempted wrapper calls and entered original-source invocations; its injected error produces 284 versus 283. These are not interchangeable. The new proposal permits early custody/setup stops but does not precisely specify remaining labels/member slots after a scorer error or comparison failure. Old per-cell exceptions can skip remaining members.

Minimal correction: predeclare all 120 slot/label identities. Record planned, attempted, entered-original-scorer and completed/validated counts by slot/profile/route, with failure/not-attempted reasons and cell attempted/completed counts. Explicitly state continuation or terminal-stop policy after errors; either policy must forbid retries or extra calls. Separate scoring, repeat, metric and reference-unavailability failures, and define terminal precedence when several occur. Qualify late scorer errors and partial counts. Derive the whole revised synthetic suite's work from its actual cases, not old 284/283 totals.

B4. Final custody must be checked after terminal accounting.
Pins: replay_run.py:88-99,119-130; FABRICATED_QUALIFICATION_PLAN.json post-accounting mutation case; EXACT_DELTAS.json public-writer/finalization site.

The inherited admitted_input_custody/family checks precede Accounting.finish. Public summary candidates are computed before finish, held provisionally private, then serialized after finish. A mutation during finish can evade the inherited pre-accounting guard. The prospective qualification requires post-accounting mutation suppression, but exact deltas do not explicitly insert the final guard needed to satisfy it.

Minimal correction: retain provisional null public values, finish required CUDA/accounting work, then rerun source/release/authority/runtime/tensor-or-data/family/journal/selected-byte custody immediately before final public serialization. Any failure keeps all 25 metrics/contrasts null and exact actual work. State the timing scope and unmeasured terminal-write tail honestly. Add a shared-core mutation during/after finish that is caught by the new final guard.

C1. Missing historical arrays restrict the claim rather than every useful audit.
Pins: PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT.json:75,97,103; README historical-reference section.

Blocking full historical numerical-audit PASS when any required pool is missing is correct. Equal saved/current Hits50, close new/new vectors and mismatching hashes cannot reconstruct missing historical values or bound error against them.

Authenticated checkpoints can nevertheless be restored exactly, scored in a pinned new profile, repeated byte-identically twice in the observed invocation, and compared with authenticated saved selected Hits50 for all 25 cells. Those facts establish checkpoint/metric consistency under the new profile. They do not establish historical vector reproduction or explain the old failure. Private logging is conservative, but missing old vectors do not logically make every checkpoint/metric audit impossible.

Minimal correction: preserve orthogonal statuses for old FAILED exact replay; exact checkpoint/state/selector/data custody; observed strict-True repeatability; exact saved-metric consistency; historical reference availability/byte identity; and measured-or-unavailable historical arithmetic. A separately reviewed full 25 metric-consistency terminal can depend on custody, strict repeats, saved metric equality and final accounting without requiring all historical references. It cannot silently substitute for the numerical terminal or inherit public disclosure authority from this packet. Preserve all 25/no-subset publication, and independently review/qualify any added public endpoint. False_anchor's metric can remain a diagnostic; requiring it to equal the saved scalar is an extra declared gate, not logically necessary for a terminal about strict True serving.

Q1. Scalar helper labels require verified arrays in the future caller.
Pins: reference_contract.py:13-40; pilot_evaluate.py:43-73; pilot_data.py:8-12.

The helper validates fixed labels, coverage, graph/pool tags and formatted hashes. It does not inspect actual arrays, finiteness, dtype, shape, profiles, restoration or row order. Its docstring delegates those checks, so it is not presently an independent array authenticator.

The core must retain each full vector, validate it, recompute its typed canonical digest from that same vector, and bind it to the actual slot/profile and before/after receipts before using a label as reference availability. Never treat a detached scalar receipt as an array. A True candidate that authenticates history may compare with itself at zero error: valid byte evidence, not an independent error estimate. Qualify wrong labels/profiles, cross-cell vectors, nonfinite/wrong-shape/wrong-dtype arrays and reordered rows.

The full saved state digest must match before every call. Preserve original score_valid's intentional eval-flag transition. It sets flags false at pilot_evaluate.py:45; old post-call guards check models/optimizer and RNG. Do not misread exact-restoration wording as requiring saved training/mixed flags to persist after prescribed eval mode.

Minimal successor protocol
Keep the fixed 120 slots, exact original contracts, strict True1/True2 byte equality, every reached metric, original digest-equality flags and old FAILED record. Authenticate historical references only with exact old digests in fixed priority and no retry/search. Report historical discrepancies only when authenticated; otherwise report unavailable. Reconstruct/check I4 with explicit newly generated reference provenance.

The minimal new claim is full 25 checkpoint/metric consistency: exact custody, both strict True metrics equal saved selection, exact observed repeats, frozen full family, and final accounting/custody. Retain 128*eps as engineering evidence while its scientific acceptance scope is unresolved. Do not call unauthenticated True outputs historical bytes. Preserve conditional historical evidence and all 25/no-subset requirements.

Fresh fabricated qualification must use the eventual successor core. Exercise comparator inside/equal/outside boundaries for both absolute/relative terms; strict ties; unavailable/partial references; cross-label I4 reconstruction; profile/state/row/dtype faults; late scorer counts; and post-accounting mutation. This demonstrates gate behavior, not universal extension determinism or scientific equivalence. This review authorizes no implementation or run.
