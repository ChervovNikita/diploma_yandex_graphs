# Accuracy claim scope for a shared backbone wrapper

A portable wrapper can provide a common interface for native graph models, private factors and explicit parameter ownership. Accuracy improvement is a separate empirical claim about a particular training and serving pipeline, reference and data setting. Valid reconstruction and correct optimization bookkeeping do not guarantee that the learned ensemble will improve.

## Acquisition serving and member quality

The exact decision identity is

`pooled correct = any-member coverage − lost correct alternatives + aggregation-only correct`.

These terms can change independently of mean member accuracy. Strong nearly identical members may acquire few additional correct alternatives. More varied members may provide useful alternatives while becoming weaker individually. A serving rule can recover alternatives and introduce errors elsewhere. Fewer pooling losses can also follow from disappearing alternatives. Each quantity and its harms therefore needs its own evidence; hidden-vector distance does not establish prediction utility.

With the restricted private factors tested here, shared dense parameters couple route learning and restrict the family of independently expressible member functions. Function-preserving initialization preserves a forward at that point, not its subsequent training trajectory. Extra private capacity, orthogonal coordinates, matched parameter counts and an optimizer normalization rule do not establish equal tangent freedom, calibration, optimization geometry or final accuracy. Positive mean bootstrap weights preserve expected weighted risk at fixed parameters; they provide no learned competence guarantee.

## What convex serving can establish

For member probabilities p_m and scalar nonnegative weights alpha_m summing to one, q is their convex mixture. If one wrong class r has p_m(r) greater than p_m(y) for every member, then q(r) is greater than q(y). Such a common strict rival cannot be reversed by these weights in exact arithmetic. All members being wrong is a weaker statement: different wrong rivals can still permit aggregation-only correctness. Coverage alone is therefore not a general accuracy ceiling for convex probability pooling.

The convexity of negative log gives NLL(q) at most the corresponding weighted mean member NLL for a fixed mixture. This does not guarantee better classification accuracy, lower risk than the strongest member, or superiority to a genuine independent ensemble. Temperature scaling and unrestricted stacking are different serving maps and need their own matched comparisons.

## What the closed history actually rejects

Original shared minus genuine factorized-four accuracy was +0.486664 pp on SAGE, −0.151688 pp on GCN and −0.872203 pp on GAT; shared NLL was worse at all nine seeds. The SAGE clue did not transfer. Exchange lost accuracy and coverage at all nine seeds, although its GAT member/NLL atomic conditions passed. Private-hop and normalized-private-Adam failed their intended competence roles. Rotation and private bootstrap failed their fixed transfer criteria; bootstrap acquired alternatives but lost most through averaging and failed member protection. These close those measured recipes, without establishing that sharing is universally useless or that hidden collapse caused every failure.

The complete graph scorer improved original shared accuracy by +0.499305/+0.783719/+0.025281 pp on SAGE/GCN/GAT, but shared minus equally graph-processed ordinary independent-four was +0.006320/−0.252813/−1.118695 pp. Its global accuracy and NLL protection rules failed. This retains a scoped serving fact while rejecting that fixed broader superiority claim. A capable factorized single explaining much of the earlier PubMed shared signal also prevents crediting single-model strength as an ensemble-specific gain.

Weak individual members can form a useful ensemble. A failed member criterion does not mathematically forbid a subsequent pooled accuracy and proper-risk win; it remains a distinct recorded failure. Conversely, a favorable interaction statistic cannot rescue worse final pooled quality. Claims should match the endpoint actually measured.

## A defensible wrapper claim

Report quality, storage and work at specified operating points against capable native singles, factorized singles and genuine independent ensembles, with equal serving opportunities. Keep implementation portability, resource savings, member protection, pooled accuracy and proper risk as separate claims. State ancestry and preserve unsuccessful results. Development OOF formed after full-VALID checkpoint selection is encountered development. A broad accuracy claim requires a frozen whole pipeline and unused confirmation across the intended settings; the current three-seed repeats on one connected development graph do not supply that evidence.
