## The single aggregate

`aggregate.py` produces one compact fifteen-cell table, with all members, nodes,
classes and seeds retained. It reuses the existing Wiki12 metadata binding
helpers and unchanged reviewed MacLink transport. No model object is built.

1. **Class geometry in the actual objective coordinates.** Normalize each hidden
   vector with the original 1e-12 floor. Compute all within-member same-class
   distinct-node mean cosines, different-class mean cosines, and their gap.
   Compute class-centered residuals from this whole development population and
   the six same-node cross-member residual cosines, including floor counts.
   These describe the intended class alignment and residual repulsion sites.
   They are static eval analogues, not the original auxiliary loss or its
   success on the sampled two-view TRAIN support.

2. **Exact head visibility.** Fold the actual saved r/s/W maps, then remove the
   class-offset gauge: `B_m^c = (I-11^T/10) B_m`. Map each class-centered hidden
   residual through this exact map. Report pairwise RMS dispersion, absolute
   energy and the normalized dispersion of those class-contrast outputs.
   Separately retain the full cached truth-versus-every-other-class margins,
   including bias/class means, and their member dispersion. These are fixed
   class-semantic quantities. No accuracy, NLL, Brier, calibration or new argmax
   score is calculated.

3. **Native metric null component, as a qualified description.** At each member's
   fixed class-contrast head, use its row space to split residual energy into
   visible and exact null directions. Report rank and native-coordinate null
   energy fraction. SVD uses the conventional fixed `max(shape)*eps*smax`
   numerical rank tolerance, with no search. This fraction and ambient cosines
   depend on the representation metric; they are not invariant under arbitrary
   diagonal or other non-orthogonal coordinate changes.

Different member/state heads define different row spaces. These fractions do
not decompose the R−P representation change into a common exact nullspace.

The folded maps reconstruct only the already-cached logits as a source fidelity
check. A conservative FP32 rounding bound is fixed in source; mismatch stops
interpretation. The exact cached logits remain authoritative. No serving score
is recomputed or replaced.

Report the same-seed A−P, R−P, C−P, C−A, S−A and S−P endpoint differences, keeping
each seed and both selected epochs/stages visible. Comparisons are descriptive;
no seed-node confidence interval or favorable subset is selected. Original
competence, repair/harm and decision-redundancy results remain their prior
authority and supply the practical outcome interpretation.

## What can be learned

Larger hidden residual separation with little head-visible/margin separation
would support **selected representation changes that scarcely change the
classifier's class evidence**. A larger class gap with redundant decisions can
likewise show class geometry changing without acquiring useful alternatives.
Head-visible margin changes with unchanged argmax decisions describe confidence
or subthreshold evidence changes. Increased native null energy is a metric-bound
localization of frozen-head insensitivity, not a whole-network failure proof.

If R/C do not separate the selected development residuals more than their
controls, that is absence of the intended endpoint geometry in this cache. It
does **not** establish failed optimization, unsuccessful two-view TRAIN
regularization, or a hidden-collapse trajectory. Actual TRAIN views/centers,
gradients and trajectories are absent. This task cannot identify those causes.

Compensating hidden-coordinate changes with the corresponding folded head can
alter ambient cosine/null-energy fractions while preserving class margins.
The class-contrast output and full-margin comparisons survive that gauge. Do
not promote hidden distance or native null fraction alone. Across states,
different selected epochs and local/global stages confound a causal loss claim:
6101 P/R are global and A/C/S local; 6203 A is local while P/R/C/S are global;
6307 R is global while P/A/C/S are local.

Class labels identify semantic same-class pairs. Graph adjacency is not used as
a positive or negative label; no claim of distinct graph evidence, neighborhoods,
edge routes, or graph-specific mechanism follows. The same development population
selected these checkpoints and remains reused descriptive data.
