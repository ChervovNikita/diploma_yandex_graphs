## What PT can change

PT v2 freezes the selected ordinary meanSAGE first layer and its undropped H0. It learns repeated PP/PT/TP/TT transformations and current-state gates on the original support, then a residual projection and the native decoder. Four states may retain different nonlinear histories; only 32 gate e coordinates are private. The common head still serves the arithmetic mean of member probabilities. It has no optimizer-step memory, raw-feature support union, learned graph or private class head.

It can change scores and make previously wrong source predictions correct when the frozen H0 and its graph neighborhoods contain useful discriminating signals. It can also create correct alternatives outside old I4 coverage. It cannot directly recover raw-X information irreversibly discarded by the frozen stem or add evidence absent from its H0/support interface. Current-state operation choice is an architectural hypothesis, not proof that early mixing caused the preceding failures.

The zero residual projection initially contains the selected source evaluation function in exact arithmetic. This requires full-input qualification before a bitwise or tolerance-based runtime statement. It does not constrain later competence. Own-member CE plus unchanged probability pooling does not itself solve retention: PT can introduce correct members while weakening others or losing them in the mean.

The middle-state-mean control removes first-block private histories while preserving e, shared maps, gate parameterization and final route predictions. Its contrast tests that whole interface, including changed backward coupling and second-block computation. The fixed bias-coordinate single/I4 controls address a different concern: e also adds a redundant common-bias optimizer coordinate path. PT must beat capable controls with that path before attributing utility to private states. GNNMoE/Node-MoE remain known ancestry; a pilot cannot establish a new primitive.

## Is three-block Physics representative enough?

Yes for one economical, complete development decision: each block has 100 TRAIN, 150 SELECT, 150 CAL and 34,093 DEVELOPMENT_EVAL nodes; the source is demonstrably competent and the relevant failures are observed on precisely these roles. Three repeated mask/optimizer blocks on one connected graph with overlapping evaluation populations do not identify dataset variation, independent node uncertainty or transferable benefit. This is encountered development after many proposals and previous result inspection. Three all-positive deltas would be useful pilot evidence but not strong confirmation. E's earlier ten-block TEST was already released and has different roles; borrowing its heldout label does not restore an unused pipeline.

1. **Bind one complete three-block panel before fitting.** Retain PT private4, middle-state mean, head-only, original mixed single/I4 and fixed bias-coordinate single/I4, plus the unchanged same-role source/factor benchmarks and an original-GNNM predictive reference on those exact roles. 2. **Freeze and qualify the actual procedure once.** Bind optimizer/groups, objective/selector/ties, independent stopping, horizons, role targets, RNG streams, selected restoration, cache/source/support custody, calibration, practical raw/member/proper-loss margins and finite costs. No repeated tiny qualifier grid is needed; the required checks are source containment, live continuation, real independent ownership and full selected-state restoration for these interfaces.
3. **Release one whole comparison and decide on served utility.** Require the frozen net accuracy improvement versus source/head-only, capable singles, both genuine equipped I4s and the middle-state control, with member and proper-loss protection. Preserve all block/class repairs and harms, coverage, pooling losses and aggregation-only correctness, including harms outside the predefined source/common-wrong cohorts. Gate entropy, coverage and a repaired subset cannot substitute for this conjunction. A complete pass supports a bounded development procedure; only then freeze a separate unused graph/role confirmation. A failure closes this exact recipe without a coefficient, optimizer or serving rescue grid.
