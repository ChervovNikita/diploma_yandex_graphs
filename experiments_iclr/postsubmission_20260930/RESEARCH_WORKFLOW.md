# Three research roles

The user's 10 October request is the standing workflow. With four available concurrent slots, keep the root and three distinct researchers active when there is useful independent work.

| Role | Responsibility | Concrete output |
|---|---|---|
| New method researcher | Find a substantially different mechanism, read saved conclusions first, check the closest primary sources | One falsifiable shared-backbone hypothesis, strongest prior collision, decisive reference and estimated compute |
| Combination researcher | Combine mechanisms that target different observed failures | A paired baseline/A/B/A+B design with member competence, pooled quality, interaction and cost measurements |
| History researcher | Revisit complete families and their error diagnostics after each closure | Corrected synthesis, contradictions, promising untested implications and at most three next actions |
| Root | Choose, execute, interpret and publish | A small active hypothesis queue, fixed studies, complete outcomes, current state and Git publication |

## Decide with prediction evidence

Separate two possible failures. A route may become a weaker predictor, or correct route predictions may be lost when outputs are averaged. Different remedies follow. Hidden-vector distance does not establish either failure. Prefer complete stored-error diagnostics before another diversity loss.

Combine mechanisms when each has a plausible distinct role. One can help members become better predictors, while another can teach them different useful dependencies. Treat positive interaction as a hypothesis. A package must improve final predictions and preserve member competence. A favorable interaction cannot rescue worse final quality. Paired seeds and the full fixed roster are required.

Every completed family triggers a history update and a next-step decision. Preserve unsuccessful results and describe why a hypothesis failed within its measured scope. Reopen it only with a new mechanism or newly available evidence. Do not spend another grid on a renamed existing loss. A known method may be a useful ingredient or baseline, with its ancestry stated explicitly.

## Continue toward the paper

Engineering checks precede full fits. A positive exploratory screen leads to matched capable references, mechanism comparisons and unused confirmation. Paper claims follow audited results and uncertainty estimates. Fresh manuscript reviewers start without author history or a requested verdict. Save every review and address its scientific concerns. Acceptance remains a goal, never an assumption used to interpret data.
