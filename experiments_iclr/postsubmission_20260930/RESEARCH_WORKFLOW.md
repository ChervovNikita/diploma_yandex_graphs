## Decide with prediction evidence

Separate two possible failures. A route may become a weaker predictor, or correct route predictions may be lost when outputs are averaged. Different remedies follow. Hidden-vector distance does not establish either failure. Prefer complete stored-error diagnostics before another diversity loss.

Combine mechanisms when each has a plausible distinct role. One can help members become better predictors, while another can teach them different useful dependencies. An ingredient can have a negative total delta yet contribute stable distinct repairs; this can justify a combination when its harms and the interaction mechanism are explicit. Treat positive interaction as a hypothesis. A package must improve final predictions and preserve member competence. A favorable interaction cannot rescue worse final quality. Paired seeds and the full fixed roster are required.

The combination researcher supplies one interaction hypothesis with a common baseline and both ingredients measured separately.
