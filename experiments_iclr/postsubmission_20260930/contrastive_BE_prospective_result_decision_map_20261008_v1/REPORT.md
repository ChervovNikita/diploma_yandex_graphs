## The failure to repair

Unit+contrast has slightly stronger mean members than ordinary independent4, yet less any-correct coverage; even its selected-state argmax oracle trails ordinary pooling. Rademacher initialization buys alternatives at too much competence cost. The objective is therefore **useful correct alternatives that improve the actual served predictor**, with mean/minimum member quality and risk checked separately. Larger hidden distance, target variation, first-update differences, pooling benefit or stronger average members alone cannot establish that result.
