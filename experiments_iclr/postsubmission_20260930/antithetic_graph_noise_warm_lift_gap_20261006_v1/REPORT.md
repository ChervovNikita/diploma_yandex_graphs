## Exact operation and limits

`P_m = S + (4/3) (B_m ⊙ P_off)`.

Do not recompute masked degrees or renormalize surviving rows. Then `mean_m P_m = P` for every assignment. With independent edge assignments, each member's edge keep bits have the ordinary independent Bernoulli(3/4) marginal, while for one edge the four keep bits sum to three and have pairwise correlation `−1/3`. This is fixed-size sampling without replacement across member indices. The construction is also the discrete stratification obtained by cyclically shifting a uniform variate by quarters and thresholding at 3/4. These observations are our algebraic comparisons, not claimed ARMS graph results.

For a common state `H` and shared channel map `W`, `mean_m P_m H W = P H W` is exact before nonlinear processing. Write `E_m=P_m−P`. For a differentiable common full network, the derivative at `ε=0` of `mean_m f(P+εE_m;θ)` vanishes because its common derivative is linear and `sum_m E_m=0`. The same local statement extends to balanced perturbations at each layer. It requires the common parameter/state expansion point and differentiability. Actual deletion amplitudes are finite (`−1` or `+1/3` times an edge coefficient), and ReLU activation boundaries need not admit that expansion. Even a two-layer linear construction has `mean_m(P+E_m)^2 = P^2 + mean_m E_m^2` when one mask is reused across layers.
