## Why embedding repulsion is an inadequate quality objective

An invertible feature change `h_m'=h_m Q_m` together with classifier `W_m'=Q_m^{-1}W_m` leaves every member logit and prediction error unchanged while changing the feature geometry. Classifier-nullspace directions can change representations without changing logits. Conversely, output spread can increase because a previously competent member becomes worse. Shared parameters also allow a private diversity auxiliary to distort common predictive features. These are mechanism counterexamples, not findings from a score cohort.

Useful evidence must therefore measure the deployed pool's NLL/accuracy **and per-member competence**, with the actual pool fixed. Mean raw-logit pooling is a normalized geometric probability pool, not an arithmetic probability mean. The saved kernel-NCL identities and any auxiliary/pool comparison must retain this distinction.
