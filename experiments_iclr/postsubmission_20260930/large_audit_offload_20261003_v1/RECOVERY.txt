Recover FILE_INVENTORY.json and REMOTE_GIT_TREE.z

OFFLOAD_RECEIPT.json records the authorized server, final archive path, byte count
and SHA256. The archive contains only these two audit payloads, their exact file
manifest, the recovery source and this guide. It contains no real Git objects.

Copy large_audits.tar.gz from the authorized server with existing SSH credentials.
Check its SHA256 against OFFLOAD_RECEIPT.json, then run:

python3 -B recover_large_audits.py large_audits.tar.gz NEW_RESTORED_PHASE

Choose a new directory under an existing canonical parent. Recovery verifies both
payload SHA256 hashes and recreates their phase-relative paths, file permissions
and modification times. Existing destinations are refused. Nothing in the archive
is executed, and original ownership becomes the restorer's ownership. Recovery
requires about 34 MB of space plus the compressed archive.

All decisions, manifests, compact custody metadata, current sources and states
remain local. The archive and independent verification/recovery records are kept
in the authorized repository under author_archives/mac_cleanup_20261003_large_audits_v1.
