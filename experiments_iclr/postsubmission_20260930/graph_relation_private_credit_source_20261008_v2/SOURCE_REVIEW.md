## Exact native partition

The native global attention explicitly uses `qk_shared=True` and `q=k` from
`sigmoid(k_lins[i](x))`. There are no independent `q_lins`. The relation block
therefore contains exactly:

- Four r/s parameter banks from `global_attn.k_lins.0` and `.1`, counted once
  for their shared Q/K role, with 8,192 scalars in total.
- Fourteen copied private local `att_src`/`att_dst` banks, with 28,672 scalars.

Missing/different maps or unknown/aliased parameters are rejected. The original
audited dense inventory has 56 internal factor tensors and six input/classifier
boundary tensors. Its overlap with the 18 relation tensors is exactly the four
tied-QK factor banks. The relation-only tensors are the fourteen scorer banks;
the phi-only tensors are the other 52 internal factor tensors.

| Policy | First disjoint group | Complement |
|---|---|---|
| alphaF | Relation block: F | F |
| allJ | Relation block: J | J |
| phiJ | Original internal dense factors: J | F, including local scorers |
| relationJ | Local scorers plus tied-QK factors: J | F |

All four conditions share the same architecture, initialization and two-group
replay. Value projection **factor banks** receive J in allJ/phiJ, while their
shared weights/biases receive J only in allJ. All other shared weights, norms
and classifier/input factors receive F in relationJ. No coarse “attention
versus features” interpretation replaces these exact overlaps.

## Learning and execution semantics

F is complete mean-member own CE. J is `.5 F + .5 CE(mean probabilities)` within
each of the two original stochastic views, then averaged across views. The
stable pool helper is reused from the existing attributed adapter. No embedding
loss, auxiliary panel, teacher, learned multiplier or serving router exists.

The existing constructor inserts copied private scorers before fresh native
Adam. Its unit factory requires the `be_unit_contrastive` arm name; the owned
successor explicitly disables the contrastive flag before installing replay.
That suffix does not introduce an auxiliary term or a different initializer.
Dense factors remain one and scorer rows copy native vectors without extra
random draws. This initial symmetry gives matching F/J derivatives with
identical deterministic member paths. Actual native independent dropout paths
need not produce equal probabilities or derivatives.

The replay reuses public shadow capture and exact member RNG rewind/end checks.
Two output cotangent collections compute F/J; two disjoint parameter VJPs are
collected for each member/view from the same tape and old parameters. All group
gradients accumulate before one native Adam step. None gradients on inactive
global paths remain None. Every condition charges the same structure: eight
shadow and eight replay member forwards, two output collections, sixteen member
reverse collections and one Adam per update. No speed claim is made.

The original full1100-epoch loop, original joint strict-first complete5274 VALID
selector and selected-local model/Adam restore with live end-local streams are
reused. All12 cells use seeds6101/6203/6307, two complete580-label views and the
same11701-node/442907-edge graph. Parent must freeze the physical GPU assignment
within each paired seed and use actual same-GPU native qualification. Original
plain anchors are reserved by source metadata before new outcomes. Their later
numerical reuse requires the complete old-ten-plus-new-two union; the failed old
owner closure stays false. That union is not required to begin unrelated new TRAIN
fits once actual successor qualification and root execution admission pass. Their
different scorer ownership and old one-group replay costs are disclosed, so an
anchor contrast is context, not isolated proof about the new risk recipients.

## Scientific scope and safeguards

relationJ−alphaF is the frozen primary contrast. It changes local scorer and
tied-QK recipients together; the study cannot separate their causal roles.
relationJ−allJ/phiJ tests the combined restriction against scalar risk and
broader internal-factor placement. phiJ already trains the tied-QK factors
with J. No extra local/global ablation grid is admitted.

Both all12 new fits and exact original12 union must close before anchor numerical
reuse or comparative opening. Every training release explicitly denies those
permissions, regardless of whether its optional union binding is already present.
The original utility screen is retained: positive primary accuracy difference
at all three seeds, mean gain at least .2pp, no worse mean pooled NLL, and at
most .1pp mean/.2pp worst-member degradation. All12 must close before quality
opening. Keep every failure, cost, repair and introduced error. A tied or better
allJ/phiJ undermines a distinct recipient-placement claim. Three development
optimizer replicates are not independent confirmation.

Eventual ensemble superiority requires a competent native single and an
ordinary independently trained four-model ensemble, with their original own
selectors and opportunity/cost disclosed. A matched untied counterpart is
required for a sharing-specific claim. Broad efficient-ensemble claims also
need a source-qualified efficient/diversity reference. None is supplied by
the twelve mechanism fits or original plain shared anchors.

A conditional **one-body eight-view** reference is also required to attribute
benefit beyond extra stochastic training views. It trains one capable predictor
with the mean CE of the same four stream pairs, one Adam per update and the
original full horizon/selector, then serves one deterministic prediction. There
are no learned private routes, graph-context targets or auxiliary terms. The
saved single8 scaffold supplies ancestry, but its eight live tapes are not a
qualified match to the present replay; a separate source/runtime adaptation and
actual cost accounting are needed. This reference is inactive and adds no pilot
fit. Neither matched view count nor its result alone isolates route capacity
from multi-function serving.

After a supported full pilot, freeze the unchanged method and source on
genuinely unused official split roles and a separately chosen complete graph/
backbone before heldout scoring. Audit past role/test exposure; do not reuse
published values as a reproduced comparison or tune beta. Attention-free
backbones need a separately qualified architecture adaptation.

The local bank retains GATv1's static ranking limitation. Query-dependent global
