# Conditional serving from the three-edge matching law: reject as the next method

5 October 2026. One bounded theoretical assessment using saved scopes. No retrieval, numerical run, model/queue change or canonical edit.

**Decision: the existing complete-matching law does not supply a defensible conditional probability for the queried link given the proposed observed companions.** A partially conditioned mask score can expose its extra parity, but it is ordinary mixture conditioning on a graph law with the wrong support for negative queries. Repair would require a new count/degree or binary-link model. That is established graph reconstruction/mixture machinery rather than a novel consequence of the current matching objective. Do not add a predictive serving cohort for this construction.

## A precise label-free observable construction

For each official candidate `q=(a1,b1)`, form `G_minus_q` by removing every TRAIN record and both adjacency directions of `q`, whether present or absent. Choose two node-disjoint observed TRAIN companion pairs `e2=(a2,b2)` and `e3=(a3,b3)` from this candidate-excluded graph, with all six endpoints distinct. Selection uses the candidate endpoints and candidate-excluded TRAIN topology only. It must not read the candidate's removal count, its TRAIN incidence bit, VALID/TEST membership, or any heldout topology. The off-diagonal status and structural-witness rules, if retained, inspect this same TRAIN-only graph. TRAIN absence means unknown latent/future status.

Remove both companions, including all duplicates/directions, to construct the common visible graph `H`. Retain their identities as explicitly known observations. Compute all nine native pair logits on `H`, construct the six member matching laws `q_m(pi|H)`, then their arithmetic mixture `q_J`. No hypothetical matching is inserted into `H`. This can be applied without changing the official candidate list. If companion selection fails, the proposed conditional law supplies no score; a predeclared native fallback could cover that candidate, but would not fix the support problem below.

For undirected graphs, orientation is an artificial representation of the bundle. A serving rule would need orientation averaging or an invariant equivalent; choosing orientations according to observed query status is forbidden. No particular sampling budget, fallback mixture or orientation schedule is adopted here.

This construction does not leak the queried link label. It also differs from the rejected complete-TRAIN teacher posterior: companions are intentionally hidden, so their reconstruction likelihood need not be one and component weights need not be uniform. The objection is therefore not that all masked evidence is uninformative.

## What the conditional scores actually say

Let `B1` denote that the queried pair is included in the restored matching and `Ci` that companion `ei` is included. Every feasible assignment restores exactly one edge incident to each of the six endpoints.

**Use no companion labels.** The query marginal is

```
P_J(B1|H) = sum_{pi: pi(1)=1} q_J(pi|H).
```

This is one of the nine row marginals. It is exactly equal under the fixed-marginal `q_ME` control. It can be a different heuristic from the raw native logit, but it does not serve the extra parity whose transfer was questioned.

**Condition on both known companions.** Only the identity matching contains both `e2` and `e3`. Therefore

```
P_J(B1 | C2=1,C3=1,H) = 1.
```

The matching constraint forces the unknown query to be present. This is not evidence that the candidate is a real link; the restricted model excluded the genuine alternative graph containing just the two observed companions. Such a graph is possible for any negative query. A nonuniform member posterior from the companion event cannot rescue the deterministic conditional query probability.

**Condition on only one companion.** If `C2=1`, exactly two matchings remain: the identity and the permutation exchanging columns 1 and 3. Calling them `id` and `swap13`,

```
S_one(q) = P_J(B1 | C2=1,H)
         = q_J(id) / [q_J(id)+q_J(swap13)].
```

This score is nontrivial and does use parity. The saved mean-logit failure path has uniform row marginals but different even/odd assignment probabilities; `S_one` consequently changes on that path while the native mean logits remain fixed. It is a mathematical way to expose the extra learned assignment dependence.

However, the selected third companion `e3` is known to be observed, and the `swap13` hypothesis requires replacing it by two different, unobserved pairs. The construction deliberately withholds that known evidence. Its conditional probability is valid only for the artificial matching-completion problem in which that evidence is absent and the unknown first endpoints are assumed to need exactly one restored incident edge. It is not a conditional probability for a candidate link given the actual known companions. Returning it as a heuristic mask score is possible, but would change the task interpretation and require independent supervised evidence of native link-ranking utility.

Ignoring known graph evidence is not itself label leakage or a general prohibition on inference masking. The decisive issue is that the learned conditional matching law has no probability mass for `B1=0,C2=C3=1`. The negative candidate's scientifically possible graph lies outside its support. Training only on observed three-edge bundles supplies neither its mass nor a calibrated odds comparison with the three-edge case.

## What a support repair would require

A coherent binary query probability would need a graph law assigning nonzero mass to both the two-companion graph and graphs containing the queried edge. For example it would need a learned distribution over the missing degrees/counts of the first endpoints, or a label-conditioned graph model under both `Y_q=0` and `Y_q=1`. A law conditioned on the three restored edges does not identify the relative mass of those cases. Arbitrarily supplying that mass is an untrained prior, not a derivation from `q_J`.

With a valid enlarged law, mixture conditioning has the ordinary identity

```
P_J(B1|C,H) = sum_m gamma_m(C,H) * P_m(B1|C,H),
gamma_m(C,H) proportional to P_m(C|H).
```

Observable companion masking could make these responsibilities nonuniform. This is classical mixture conditioning, already covered by the saved latent compatibility/posterior assessments. A count/label model and this posterior are not a new serving principle. Changing the served prediction from raw-logit pooling to a masked conditional score would also require an entirely prospective comparison; current frozen cohorts cannot supply that evidence.

TRAIN-only companion choice avoids heldout selection leakage. It does not make the mask distribution match the future task: three historically observed disjoint edges with absent cross pairs and common-neighbour witnesses select a particular historical pattern. On temporal Collab, the queried pair can be a repeated collaboration, and an unobserved alternative can become a future positive. The saved HeaRT caveat is directly relevant. These are transfer risks, not a claim that all TRAIN-only graph selection is indefensible. The present rejection follows already from the conditional support and lack of a distinct mechanism.

## Matched controls and retained priors

Any heuristic `S_one` study would need the exact same companions, orientations, masks, candidate coverage and fallback in its controls. Four ordinary independently trained models can each learn the same complete-matching loss and serve the arithmetic mixture followed by the identical conditional-event calculation. Independent parameters do not remove this mixture dependence. This is the appropriate ordinary ensemble with the same observation/serving operation; comparing only against an unaugmented raw-logit ensemble would confound the changed evidence and serving rule.

A capable structured single must have the same bundle features/context and a nonadditive, invariant six-assignment decoder, followed by the same conditional calculation. If support is enlarged to allow the two-companion case, it needs that same enlarged support and count/binary supervision as well. Either control can match the entire conditional operation. Weight sharing has no representational advantage here.

The existing saved assessments already attribute degree-preserving switches to Maslov–Sneppen; endpoint-related hard alternatives and temporal filtering concerns to HeaRT; reconstruction supervision with an inference-omitted decoder to MaskGAE; block mixture responsibilities to GRAN; and coherent/joint graph modeling to CAM, subgraph diffusion and PIFM. The existing conditional-endpoint assessment also records the uniform full-TRAIN teacher limit and the need for a separately specified observable inference mask. This note reuses those conclusions and the matching/mean-logit notes; it adds no primary-paper read or novelty clearance.

The concrete choice is to stop this conditional-serving repair at the design stage. Preserve the matching source as an untested auxiliary option. The only proposed formula that exposes parity either becomes deterministic when all known companions are respected or becomes an artificial-mask heuristic needing additional probabilistic support and generic conditioning. It does not yet supply a method expected to beat competent singles and ordinary independent ensembles.
