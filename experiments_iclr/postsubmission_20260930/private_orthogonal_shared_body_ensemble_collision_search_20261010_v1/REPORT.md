## What the retained sources already settle

- OFT/BOFT: identity-start learned orthogonal transformations of a frozen pretrained map, including products of structured factors.
- ETHER/ETHER+: Householder adaptation and a two-normal neutral relaxation; the latter has a different, generally nonorthogonal/symmetric tangent.
- HousE: even products of Householder reflections, including graph relation rotations.
- LoRA-Ensemble: different private dictionaries with zero outgoing maps preserve adapter starts and change private tangents; private heads can break complete initial predictor equality.
- TabLoRA: teacher-free joint learning of live shared W and private additive adapters from scratch.
- Parameter superposition: private fixed orthogonal/permutation contexts before shared matrices; its sequential-task contract differs.

Thus neither orthogonal private adaptation nor neutral/different-tangent ensembling is a new principle. Missing a verified exact assembly does not change that attribution.
