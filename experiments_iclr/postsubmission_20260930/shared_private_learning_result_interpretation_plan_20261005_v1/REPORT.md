## The possible contribution

The defensible hypothesis is that **endpoint-conditioned training lookahead improves the final committed graph predictor**, and that four limited private routes provide useful estimation or optimization behavior beyond competent distinct single and ensemble procedures. The candidate trains shared NCN features/dense bases through a current private Adam response, then recomputes and commits each private response from its old parameters/moments after the shared update. Serving averages committed raw logits without query-time adaptation.

Shared-feature/private-head meta-learning is already ANIL/BMAML ancestry; training-only transfer with fixed deployment is MLDG/MetaReg ancestry; virtual learning, a meta update and recomputed commitment have SELAR ancestry; persistent training partners have Episodic DG ancestry. The saved ABMLL and METDG scopes add efficient global/local factor and graph-conditioned generated-predictor ancestry. An incremental contribution would therefore be a precise, attributed coupling of graph eligibility, parameter roles, persistent state, objective and commitment, with demonstrated quality and claim-matched explanation. It need not establish universal ensemble necessity or an unrepresentable function. An unmatched combination, a correct derivative or an improved score alone does not settle its methodological significance.

## Keep quality and explanation separate

Interpret the complete authenticated family at its existing first maximum complete VALID MRR rounded to four decimals, with all three paired blocks and all failures/horizon limits retained. The original quality lead requires **all six** frozen quality contrasts to have a strictly positive mean and at least two higher blocks: true independent-four anchor, capable adapted single, paid single joint, jointly trained native four, paid shared F4 joint, and unchanged F4 anchor. These are the existing exploratory criteria, not confirmation or a significance test.

The five existing attribution flags are separate: F4 live credit, F4 credit beyond the richer single's credit, endpoint construction, endpoint-by-credit interaction, and shared versus initially matched untied four. The original analysis reports a quality lead separately from support for its complete explanation. If an explanation condition fails, narrow attribution under that policy. **The F1 companions add descriptive evidence and no acceptance gate.** No new threshold, equivalence requirement, checkpoint choice or favorable-seed rescue is introduced.

For each block, let `R(X)` denote its frozen served MRR. The relevant existing comparisons can be read as:

- F4 credit: `C_end = R(E_end_live) − R(E_end_detached)` and the corresponding `C_random`.
- Endpoint interaction: `D = C_end − C_random`; also inspect endpoint minus random within each rule. A positive interaction alone can mean “less harmful”; it does not establish benefit if endpoint live credit or served quality is unfavorable.
- Matched-role companion credit: `C_F1 = R(F1_end_live) − R(F1_end_detached)`. The descriptive `C_end − C_F1` asks whether the live intervention adds more under the four-route construction. F4–F1 served differences under live, detached and joint distinguish this from a general four-route advantage.

## Theory that can and cannot explain a result

Private response lookahead has a coherent same-episode commitment interpretation: recomputation anticipates the private state actually committed after the shared update. That statement concerns masked TRAIN outer queries; it does not equate their surrogate with full-support VALID ranking risk. Old optimizer history is carried but held fixed during current differentiation. This study cannot establish a benefit from history alignment itself, because it has no such intervention.

Native Adam's response and shared movement depend on moments, bias correction and epsilon. A correct mixed derivative, nonzero credit or a negative gradient dot gives no guaranteed descent or useful prediction. BCE progress does not guarantee MRR improvement. The half pooled/half member BCE objective adds competence pressure through a nonnegative Jensen gap; it is not an explicit reward for complementary errors.

Sharing can plausibly improve finite-sample estimation or optimization while introducing bias and correlated errors. The saved shrinkage analysis illustrates this under explicit assumptions; it is no theorem for this network, Adam or ranking metric. A rich single can represent similar functions, and an exact branched reimplementation is the same algorithm packaged differently. Neither fact rules out benefit over competent **distinct** training procedures. Conversely, endpoint eligibility does not erase historical label influence, common graph context or query dependence. Three seed/provider-confounded blocks on one consumed development split cannot establish independent tasks, unbiased cross-fitting or broad generalization. Confirmation requires a locked method on fresh evidence, with these limitations reported.
