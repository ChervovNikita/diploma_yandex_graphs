# Why portable sharing still fails and what the repairs establish

Completed prediction evidence identifies several distinct obstacles: weaker members, insufficient correct alternatives, alternatives lost during pooling, and common wrong rankings that scalar mixing cannot change. Local accuracy gains repeatedly survive one comparison and fail capable references or NLL protection. No single cause such as hidden collapse, gradient conflict or calibration has been established. The complete retrieval closure strengthens this account; it does not justify another combination grid.

## The measured mechanism map

For these classification predictions, `pool correct = any-member coverage − lost alternatives + aggregation-only correct`. Counts below sum three dependent readouts of the same 5,274 WikiCS VALID nodes; they are neither unique cross-seed nodes nor independent graph samples. Accuracy differences are percentage points. NLL increases are adverse.

| Mechanism | Complete evidence | Scientific hook and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Member competence | Original shared mean members trail factorized I4 at every SAGE/GCN/GAT seed. Yet original SAGE shared beats factorized I4 by +0.486664 pp while its mean member accuracy is lower by 0.826381 pp. Exchange raises SAGE mean-member accuracy +0.287574 pp but lowers pool accuracy −1.283024 pp. | Average member quality matters, but improving it alone can erase useful alternatives. A weaker mean member is not an automatic veto of a useful pool. |
| Correct-alternative acquisition | Original SAGE shared adds 234 covered readouts versus factorized I4, but loses 158 additional alternatives and serves 77 additional correct answers, including one aggregation-only answer. Original GAT instead loses coverage at every seed. | Useful member differences must contain correct predictions. Hidden or score distance does not establish acquisition. Sharing has no uniform sign across the native backbones. |
| Loss during pooling | Original GCN shared adds 2/33/43 covered nodes versus factorized I4, while losing 27/37/38 additional alternatives: served differences −25/−4/+5. In the original I4-correct/shared-wrong cohorts, a correct shared member is available in roughly 65–69% SAGE, 74–76% GCN and 57–69% GAT. | Serving is a real opportunity on those cohorts. Label-informed oracle availability is not a prospective selector, and these cohorts do not characterize every shared error. |
| Common strict wrong rival | A single wrong class outranks truth in every member. Any nonnegative member mixture using the same weights across class coordinates preserves that ordering. The frozen graph scorer repairs zero such cases across nine original shared banks. | A scalar router cannot solve this ranking obstruction. Changing class scores or training new rankings can, but must actually acquire and serve correct answers. “All members wrong” alone is not this obstruction. |
| Strongest-reference and risk gap | Original shared accuracy gains over factorized I4 are +0.486664/−0.151688/−0.872203 pp for SAGE/GCN/GAT; NLL is worse on all three. Equally graph-scored shared minus ordinary I4 is +0.006320/−0.252813/−1.118695 pp, with all full accuracy and NLL rules failing. | A better shared baseline or serving rule need not yield a portable advantage. Accuracy improvement and protected probability quality are separate obligations. |

Shared affine weights with private factors restrict the joint function family relative to four independently fitted bodies, while still producing four nonlinear native trajectories. The restriction can regularize useful learning or couple errors; these complete interventions do not isolate a universal capacity or optimization cause. Genuine I4 also has more stored bodies, separate optimizers and own checkpoint selection. A sharing claim needs capable singles/I4 and explicit measured work, rather than credit from parameter count alone.

Live shared minus detached accuracy is **−0.107445/−0.214891/+0.063203 pp** for SAGE/GCN/GAT. No backbone passes the frozen accuracy conjunction; every backbone also fails the whole NLL-protection conjunction. The terminal memory's same-state contrast is more specific. It raises accuracy +0.164328/+0.164328/+0.316016 pp and lowers NLL −0.017099/−0.060166/−0.035842. Of its 79/85/92 pooled repairs, **79/83/89 serve correct alternatives already present in the native bank**. It creates 40/41/56 new member alternatives but serves only 0/2/3. It repairs zero native common strict-rival cases in all nine live shared banks, and has zero aggregation-only rescues. This supports a conditional serving effect, rather than the hoped-for common-error acquisition effect of this exact live recipe. The retrieval operation can change rankings in principle; the zero count is empirical, not a convex-mixture impossibility theorem for retrieval.

Native components above are measured at mixed-selected checkpoints. Live/detached comparisons include learned parameters, stopping and selection; same-state terminal contrasts describe the chosen checkpoints. They do not isolate retrieval training causally. GAT nevertheless improves original shared accuracy +0.265453 pp at every seed and lowers mean NLL; detached already supplies +0.202250 pp of that gain. SAGE live improves original shared only +0.006320 pp, with one positive and two negative seeds. All three lower mean NLL versus original shared, yet retain their same-information I4 NLL deficits. SAGE's live-minus-detached mean NLL −0.110402 does not pass protection because one seed worsens +0.083478.

## Two ingredients really do repair different errors

Bootstrap acquisition and graph-context serving have an exact completed four-cell comparison. SAGE supplies defensible local complementarity: AB preserves every A-only repair and 11/16 B-only repairs, plus both shared repairs. It still loses 77 of those 127 and 48 alternatives B had served. These are observed repair roles, not a guarantee that a new combination would preserve them.

AB improves equally graph-served A by +0.233852 pp on SAGE and +0.158008 pp on GAT, with all seed accuracy deltas positive and the declared NLL protection passing. Mean NLL changes are −0.013995/−0.010228; GAT has one adverse seed that remains inside its protection bound. Original bootstrap member-protection flags remain false. A local protected pool gain is real despite that member result.

The completed package remains insufficient. Paired AB reaches 79.591708% SAGE and 81.342435% GAT, below equally graph-processed ordinary I4's 81.064341%/82.088232% at every seed, with worse mean NLL. Its +0.189609 pp SAGE gain over B narrowly misses the fixed +0.2 pp requirement. Temperature-reference NLL protection fails. All six coherent/pair/LoRA blocks fail the full frozen package rules. The combination is already tested and closed; these repair intersections supersede the earlier lack of a joint repair table for this exact bootstrap-serving comparison only.

## No additional A B A plus B study is justified

**Nominate none.** Bootstrap plus graph serving already tested the strongest evidenced acquisition/serving complementarity. Adding retrieval would combine a closed weak learning increment with a largely serving effect, without exact common-baseline cross-family repair identities. Adding the current decoder before its complete result would turn an unresolved utility test into a new ingredient grid. Positive interaction, marginal coverage or different method names do not supply that missing evidence.

The current frozen recurrent class decoder is a distinct falsifiable utility question: neighbor predicted-class mass can contribute a learned class-score direction beyond scalar member weighting. It can mathematically cross an original common-rival margin, and may also reinforce common mistakes. Its common/private B, P=I, capable M1, genuine I4 and joint untied/commonB controls determine whether neighbor computation, decoder sharing or native backbone sharing explains any eventual gain. This is a pending test, not evidence in this map or another combination nomination.

Matching Networks/NCA retrieval, bootstrap, reliability weighting, BatchEnsemble, CPGNN/GBPN and recurrent inference decoders are known ingredients. Their adaptation or local predictive benefit establishes no novel principle. Complete final accuracy/NLL, repairs and harms against equally informed capable controls can establish utility; a positive screen then needs unused confirmation before a portable claim. Three backbones and repeated seeds on the same encountered graph cannot supply that confirmation.
